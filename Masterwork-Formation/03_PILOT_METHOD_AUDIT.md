# Pilot Method Audit — Dante / Proust / Borges

## Purpose

The first three dossiers were selected to stress the Masterwork Formation instrument under radically different conditions:

- Dante — strong architecture, weak surviving compositional archive.
- Proust — abundant genetic archive, open growth through return and revision.
- Borges — compressed form, abundant revision evidence, and deliberate instability of textual authority.

The audit asks:

1. Which research categories repeatedly produced real explanatory value?
2. Which categories produced noise or invited overreach?
3. What important variables were missing?
4. Which apparent patterns survived enough difference to be promoted?
5. What must change before the authorship-spine research begins?

---

# I. What worked

## 1. Inheritance

This category was productive in all three cases.

Dante:
multiple theological, philosophical, classical, political, and vernacular inheritances become one traversable architecture.

Proust:
criticism, Ruskin, pastiche, prior fiction, salons, philosophy, and memory practices become material for a new form.

Borges:
libraries, philosophy, translation, detective fiction, encyclopedias, literary criticism, and apocrypha become conceptual machinery.

Finding:

INHERITANCE should not merely list influences.

The instrument must ask:

WHAT DID THE WORK DO TO ITS INHERITANCE?

---

## 2. Pressure

Pressure mattered in all three cases, but only when linked to form.

Dante:
exile and political fracture sharpen problems of position, order, legitimacy, and return.

Proust:
illness, loss, publication constraints, and World War I affect scale, revision, duration, and the work's capacity to absorb new history.

Borges:
war, nationalism, ideological systems, library work, illness, and literary institutions become pressures around authority, systems, archives, and compression.

Finding:

Biography by itself is low-value.

Pressure becomes useful when we can trace:

PRESSURE
-> FORMAL PROBLEM
-> FORMAL RESPONSE.

---

## 3. Decisive formal move

This was one of the strongest categories.

Dante:
do not explain the order; make someone traverse it.

Proust:
do not state the problem of misrecognition only; make the reader live long enough to misread, return, and see differently.

Borges:
borrow the forms of documentary authority; place fiction inside them; make the reader test the warrant.

Finding:

The decisive move should be framed as an OPERATION, not a stylistic description.

---

## 4. Failure mode

This category proved unexpectedly important.

Dante:
architecture -> totalization.

Proust:
retrospective recognition -> retrospective destiny.

Borges:
coherence -> self-certification.

The failure mode often reveals the constitutional limit of the successful technique.

Finding:

Every dossier should ask not only WHY THE FORM WORKS but:

WHAT DOES THIS SAME FORM BECOME WHEN IT EXCEEDS ITS WARRANT?

---

## 5. Outside / independent standing

The category worked best when it referred to actual pressures the author did not control rather than symbolic "otherness."

Proust provides the clearest pilot example:
World War I interrupts publication, changes the historical world, and enters the work.

Borges gives another kind:
real documents, sources, historical events, and publication institutions can expose or constrain invented authority.

Dante is harder because the compositional archive is weaker.

Finding:

Do not force an Outside into every dossier.

Ask instead:

WHAT, IF ANYTHING, COULD ACTUALLY CHANGE THE WORK WITHOUT ASKING THE WORK'S PERMISSION?

---

# II. What was missing

## 1. Problem trajectory

The original instrument asked for a single "problem perceived."

That is too static.

Proust demonstrates why:
an anti–Sainte-Beuve critical problem becomes a novelistic problem of time, misrecognition, memory, and art.

Borges often begins from an intellectual proposition and converts it into a fictional warrant problem.

Dante's problem is largely reconstructed because no compositional diary survives.

The instrument now needs:

- INITIAL PROBLEM — what can be documented at the beginning?
- DEVELOPING PROBLEM — what changed while the work was being made?
- OPERATIVE PROBLEM — what problem does the finished form actually make the reader undergo?

This prevents us from pretending that the final work was fully conceived at the outset.

---

## 2. Formation mode

The three pilots were made in radically different ways.

Dante:
ARCHITECTURAL / sequential / large-scale formal design.

Proust:
EXPANSIVE / recursive / accretive / return-driven.

Borges:
PROLIFERATIVE IN DRAFT -> SELECTIVE -> COMPRESSED.

Therefore there is no single "masterwork process."

The instrument needs to record HOW THE WORK GROWS.

Possible dimensions:
- blueprint vs emergence
- expansion vs compression
- sequential vs fragmentary composition
- revision before vs after publication
- stable ending vs moving ending
- modular vs continuous construction

Call this:

FORMATION DYNAMICS.

---

## 3. Reader operation

"Stance toward reader" was too weak.

All three pilots make the reader DO something.

Dante:
traverse, compare positions, revise judgment.

Proust:
wait, forget, desire, misread, return, recognize.

Borges:
trust, infer, verify, doubt, test warrant.

This is not merely tone.

It is part of the machinery of the work.

Add:

READER OPERATION

Question:

WHAT MUST HAPPEN IN THE READER FOR THE FORM TO COMPLETE ITS WORK?

---

## 4. Transformation of inheritance

Listing sources is insufficient.

We need to distinguish:
- received material
- selected material
- transformed material
- refused inheritance
- recombined inheritance

The key question is:

WHAT RELATION TO INHERITANCE PRODUCED THE NEW FORM?

---

## 5. Evidentiary survivability as a front-end condition

Dante and Proust prove that archive condition changes what kind of claims are possible.

This should no longer be a late caution.

Every dossier should begin with:

ARCHIVE CONDITION / EVIDENTIARY SURVIVABILITY.

Possible values should remain descriptive rather than numerical:
- LOW
- PARTIAL
- RICH
- EXCEPTIONAL

with an explanation of what survives.

---

# III. What produced noise

## 1. Biography without formal consequence

Personal facts are seductive.

A death, illness, marriage, exile, love affair, job, or injury belongs in the dossier only if:
- there is evidence it affected composition,
- it altered the work's problem,
- it changed a decision,
- or it provides necessary historical context.

Otherwise it belongs in background notes, not the causal chain.

Rule:

NO BIOGRAPHICAL FACT BECOMES FORMATION EVIDENCE MERELY BECAUSE IT IS INTERESTING.

---

## 2. Voice metrics without operation

Sentence length, punctuation, diction, and rhythm can become stylistic trivia.

They matter when connected to an operation.

Examples:

Proust:
syntactic delay -> delayed epistemic closure.

Borges:
scholarly calm -> manufactured authority.

Dante:
register shifts -> ability to make one architecture hold radically different kinds of encounter.

Rule:

STYLE MUST BE READ AS FUNCTION BEFORE IT IS READ AS IMITABLE SURFACE.

---

## 3. Authorial office too early

ARCHITECT, GROWER, COUNTER-ARCHITECT are useful compressions.

But assigning the office too early risks making the evidence fit the category.

Revision:

Authorial office should be assigned only after:
- pressure
- problem trajectory
- formation dynamics
- formal move
- reader operation
- failure mode

have been established.

And it should remain provisional until cross-work comparison.

---

## 4. Contemporary equivalent too early

The THEN -> NOW translation is productive, but it can tempt the dossier to recruit the historical work for a present thesis.

Revision:

The contemporary translation belongs at the END of the dossier.

It must be downstream of:
- historical reconstruction
- deep operation
- failure mode
- deposit

not mixed into evidence sections.

---

# IV. What survived across all three pilots

## A. FORM DOES EPISTEMIC WORK

This is the strongest earned finding.

Dante's passage changes what can be seen.
Proust's duration and return change what can be seen.
Borges's documentary machinery changes what the reader trusts and then forces that trust into question.

Therefore:

A MASTERWORK DOES NOT MERELY STATE ITS CENTRAL PROBLEM.

ITS FORM PLACES THE READER INSIDE AN OPERATION WHERE THE PROBLEM BECOMES EXPERIENCED.

This is now strong enough to enter the working poetics.

---

## B. THE READER IS PART OF THE FORMAL MACHINE

Across all three, the work is incomplete as an operation unless the reader undergoes something.

Dante requires traversal.
Proust requires temporal revision.
Borges requires epistemic suspicion.

Therefore reader activity is not reception after the work.

It is part of the work's architecture.

---

## C. INHERITANCE BECOMES ORIGINAL THROUGH TRANSFORMATION

None of the three emerges from pure originality.

Dante recomposes inherited cosmology, epic, theology, politics, and vernacular tradition.

Proust transforms criticism, pastiche, earlier fiction, social observation, and aesthetic inheritance.

Borges turns libraries, philosophy, citation, criticism, detective form, and prior literature into fictional machines.

Therefore:

ORIGINALITY SHOULD BE STUDIED AS A RELATION TO INHERITANCE, NOT AS ABSENCE OF INHERITANCE.

---

## D. HISTORICAL PRESSURE CAN BECOME FORMAL PRESSURE

This is supported strongly enough to keep as a working principle, but not a universal law.

Historical conditions need not appear as literal subject matter.

They can alter:
- scale
- structure
- epistemic stance
- genre
- revision
- reader relation

This must still be demonstrated case by case.

---

## E. SURFACE STYLE IS DOWNSTREAM OF FUNCTION

Dante's architecture, Proust's syntax, and Borges's pseudo-scholarship become useful only when we understand what they DO.

Therefore a contemporary equivalent should replicate the pressure-to-operation relation, not the visible costume.

---

# V. What does NOT yet survive the audit

Keep these provisional:

- LOSS OF POSITION -> POSITION BECOMES VISIBLE.
- THE WORK DISCOVERS ITS FINAL FORM BY OUTGROWING ITS INTENDED FORM.
- EXTERNAL EVENTS FORCE MASTERWORKS TO CHANGE WHILE IDENTITY SURVIVES.
- GREAT AUTHORSHIP REQUIRES A CHANGE OF OFFICE BETWEEN EXPERIENCE AND COMPOSITION.
- APPARENT INEVITABILITY IS PRODUCED BY PRIOR PROLIFERATION.
- RETROSPECTIVE FORM MUST PRESERVE ACCIDENT OR BECOME DESTINY.

Each remains promising.

None has yet survived enough different authors.

---

# VI. Instrument revision

The next-generation instrument should proceed in this order:

1. IDENTITY
2. ARCHIVE CONDITION
3. INHERITANCE
4. PRESSURE
5. PROBLEM TRAJECTORY
6. CONSTRAINT
7. FORMATION DYNAMICS
8. DECISIVE FORMAL MOVE
9. REJECTED / TRANSFORMED ALTERNATIVES
10. VOICE AS FUNCTION
11. ATTENTION
12. READER OPERATION
13. FAILURE MODE
14. OUTSIDE / INDEPENDENT STANDING
15. AUTHORIAL OFFICE
16. LANDING / RECEPTION
17. DEPOSIT / SUCCESSION
18. SURVIVING OPERATION
19. THEN -> NOW
20. CONTEMPORARY QUESTION

This order better prevents present theory from contaminating historical reconstruction.

---

# VII. Result of the pilot

The three pilots do not give us a recipe for greatness.

They give us something better:

A method for asking what relation among

INHERITANCE
+ PRESSURE
+ PROBLEM
+ FORMATION
+ DECISION
+ READER
+ OUTSIDE
+ DEPOSIT

allowed a particular work to become more than an expression of its author's intention.

The instrument is ready for the authorship spine, with revision.

Next recommended case:

HOMER / HOMERIC TRADITION

because it will break one of the assumptions still built into the dossiers:

that "the author" is necessarily a single recoverable individual.
