# Seed Stress Test

## Status

Completed comparative stress test of the four strongest story-world seeds:

1. The Repair Shop
2. The Orchard
3. The Family Business Ledger
4. The Unfinished Score

The purpose is not to choose the most attractive metaphor.

It is to test which world best carries:

**SEE -> TRUST -> MEET -> CHANGE -> RETURN -> SEE AGAIN**

without collapsing into:
- twist;
- thesis;
- allegory;
- interface dependence;
- explanatory fiction.

Scoring scale:

- 5 = very strong
- 4 = strong
- 3 = viable but exposed
- 2 = weak
- 1 = structural failure

The scores are heuristic, not mathematical proof.

---

# Test 1 — Can the first reading stand without the handoff?

## Repair Shop — 5

Yes.

A skilled repairer, shop practice, damaged objects, removed parts, tools, and customers can sustain a complete first-pass world.

The handoff deepens it but is not needed to make it worth reading.

## Orchard — 5

Yes.

Seasonal work, pruning, grafting, disease, weather, yield, memory, inheritance, and land can produce a complete world before any seam appears.

## Family Business Ledger — 5

Yes.

Work, payroll, customers, machines, inherited routines, debt, suppliers, and daily closing can support a strong independent core.

## Unfinished Score — 4

Mostly.

The world can live through rehearsal, notation, performance, archive, memory, and interpretation.

But the unfinished work itself already creates expectation of later discovery.

There is greater risk that the first pass feels like setup.

# New Discovery — The Best World Does Not Need the Theory to Make It Answerable

This may be the most important result of the stress test.

The weaker seeds depend more heavily on:
- records;
- archive;
- contracts;
- provenance;
- interpretive revelation.

The Orchard can receive all of those too.

But it does not require them.

The material world itself already answers back.

Therefore:

> **THE STRONGEST STORY-WORLD MAY BE ONE IN WHICH INDEPENDENT STANDING IS NOT AN EVENT ADDED TO THE WORLD, BUT A CONDITION OF THE WORLD'S CONTINUED LIFE.**

This is stronger than:
"the seam happens later."

The seam can remain latent because the world has always exceeded the keeper.

---

# New Discovery — Living Custody

The Orchard also clarifies a stronger version of custody.

Ordinary custody can imply:
KEEP THE THING.

Living custody means:

> **KEEP THE CONDITIONS UNDER WHICH THE THING CAN CONTINUE TO BECOME.**

This changes stewardship.

The keeper is not preserving a fixed object.

The keeper is preserving:
- viability;
- relation;
- succession;
- capacity for future form.

Compression:

FIXED CUSTODY:
PRESERVE THE FORM.

LIVING CUSTODY:
PRESERVE THE CAPACITY TO CONTINUE.

This may matter far beyond the Orchard.
