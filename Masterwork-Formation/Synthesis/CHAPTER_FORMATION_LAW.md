# Chapter Formation Law

## Status

Active formal law for the emerging Masterwork.

This file records the method by which chapters are formed **when chapter-form is actually warranted**.

The full Collision Field execution order is owned by `Masterwork-Formation/Protocols/COLLISION_FIELD_RUN_PROTOCOL.md`, and form selection is governed at CF-05 by `Masterwork-Formation/Protocols/MASTERWORK_CREATION_PROTOCOL.md`.

It does **not** define the final number of chapters, final sequence, final medium, or final architecture of the complete work.

---

# I. THE CHAPTER IS NOT CHOSEN IN ADVANCE

A chapter does not begin with:
- a theme;
- a thesis;
- a title;
- a plot requirement;
- an explanatory concept.

When CF-05 determines that chapter-form is warranted, that chapter begins with the already-recovered **collision field**.

This law must not be read as a requirement that every Collision Field become a chapter.

Unlike materials are gathered and allowed to remain themselves.

The field is enriched until something begins to pull.

That attraction is the chapter's **gravity**.

The gravity is not named into doctrine before it is voiced.

---

# II. THE POET'S OFFICE

The poet enters the collision field as a tensioned instrument.

The poet does not:
- summarize the field;
- explain the gravity;
- force synthesis;
- illustrate a preselected thesis.

The poet receives the field and allows the gravity to become audible through voice.

So the operative motion is:

**COLLISION FIELD
→ GRAVITY
→ POETIC VOICE**

The chapter is the collision field after its gravity has found a voice.

---

# III. THE TITLE COMES AFTER THE SONG

The chapter is **not** titled before the poet speaks.

Only after the field has been voiced do we listen back through:

# **διά ⇄ σύν**

Working sense:

**διά**
- through;
- across;
- apart;
- passage;
- differentiation.

**σύν**
- with;
- together;
- gathered;
- relation;
- conjunction.

The purpose is not to assign one word to each side.

The purpose is to hear where:

> **ONE SIDE IS THE OTHER.**

The voiced chapter is read for the relation in which:
- change contains continuity;
- separation contains gathering;
- destruction contains preservation;
- difference contains relation;
- passing-through produces holding-together;
- holding-together makes passing-through perceptible.

The title is the phrase that emerges from that relation.

So the full chapter-generation motion is:

> **COLLISION FIELD
> → GRAVITY
> → POETIC VOICE
> → διά ⇄ σύν
> → CHAPTER NAME**

---

# IV. THE TITLE MUST BE EARNED

The title should not merely describe the topic.

It should name the relational discovery that became audible **after** the poet sounded the field.

Therefore:

- do not title from the research notes;
- do not title from the strongest concept before writing;
- do not make the poem prove the title;
- do not select an elegant paradox and force the chapter beneath it.

The title is a **Return** from the voiced chapter.

The poem comes first.

Then we listen.

Then the name arrives.

# VI. OPERATING LAW

This law applies only after the Masterwork Creation stage has earned **CHAPTER** as the form.

For every future chapter:

1. Build or encounter a collision field.
2. Allow gravity to emerge by attraction rather than elimination.
3. Do not prematurely convert gravity into doctrine.
4. Let the poet enter the field.
5. Let the field become audible through voice.
6. Preserve the different standing of materials inside the song.
7. Read the finished song through **διά ⇄ σύν**.
8. Listen for the relation in which one side is the other.
9. Name the chapter from what was actually heard.
10. If the title could have been chosen before the poem existed, it is probably not yet the title.

---

# VII. GOVERNING FORMULATION

> **WHEN CHAPTER-FORM IS WARRANTED, EACH CHAPTER IS A COLLISION FIELD
> WHOSE GRAVITY HAS GAINED A VOICE THROUGH THE POET
> AND WHOSE NAME IS HEARD AFTERWARD THROUGH διά ⇄ σύν.**

> **BUT NOT EVERY COLLISION FIELD OWES US A CHAPTER.**

Do not reverse the order.
