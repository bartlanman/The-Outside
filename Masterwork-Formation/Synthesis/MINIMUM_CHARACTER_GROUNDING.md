# Minimum Character Grounding — Before Full Biography

## Status

First cautious biographical grounding.

This document does **not** establish full biographies.

It only admits facts that materially alter:
- what a person notices;
- what they can do;
- what they fear;
- what they want;
- what costs them something;
- how scenes behave around them.

Current perceptual fields:

### A
**CONTINUITY / PATTERN / LANGUAGE / OBLIGATION**

### B
**PRESENT / PARTICULAR / ACTION / POSSIBILITY**

Current voice:
**MOBILE FREE INDIRECT THIRD PERSON WITH BOUNDED INTERIOR JURISDICTION**

Current relational field:
**LONG PARTNERSHIP**

---

# I. Admission Rule

A biographical fact enters only if it changes at least one of:

1. perception;
2. syntax;
3. material competence;
4. social standing;
5. bodily reality;
6. economic constraint;
7. relational consequence;
8. available action.

Working law:

> **IF A FACT DOES NOT CHANGE THE SCENE, IT IS NOT YET BIOGRAPHY WE NEED.**

---

# II. Life Stage

## Provisional range

Both partners should be in **midlife rather than early adulthood**.

Working range:
approximately late 40s to mid-50s.

## Why this earns entry

The central relational problem requires:
- accumulated history;
- enough shared life for one person to become a living archive of the other;
- bodily change;
- altered ambition;
- revised desire;
- some sense that earlier choices have become material structure.

Midlife gives the relation enough duration for:
- promises to have consequences;
- old identities to be socially established;
- bodily knowledge to accumulate;
- change to become both possible and costly.

## What this changes in prose

Objects can genuinely have 15–25 years of standing.

The body can carry:
- minor pain;
- altered sleep;
- changed appetite;
- changed sexuality;
- aging parents;
- fatigue;
- new physical limits.

Time is no longer abstract.

## Status

**ADMITTED PROVISIONALLY.**

---

# III. Relationship Duration

## Provisional range

Together approximately **18–25 years**.

## Why this earns entry

Shorter duration weakens:
- living archive;
- shared story;
- bodily familiarity;
- accumulated mutual misrecognition.

Much longer duration may move the primary tension toward late-life memory / mortality too quickly.

## What this changes in scenes

They can plausibly possess:
- repeated jokes;
- recurring stories;
- old evidence;
- familiar routines;
- shared financial structures;
- years of embodied care;
- versions of one another from multiple phases.

## Status

**ADMITTED PROVISIONALLY.**

---

# IV. Economic Reality

## Requirement

They should be **materially comfortable enough to possess choices, but constrained enough that choices still cost something**.

Avoid:
- extreme wealth;
- financial precarity so severe that every scene collapses into survival economics.

Working field:
stable but consequential middle / upper-middle material life, with real obligations.

## Why this earns entry

The masterwork requires decisions that are not purely symbolic.

Money can make:
- continuity real;
- promises real;
- changing desire costly;
- care measurable;
- future plans material.

The repair-bill micro-scene worked partly because:
the future had a price.

## What this changes in prose

They notice:
- replacement versus repair;
- travel;
- retirement;
- debt;
- housing;
- family support;
- career risk.

Neither can simply reinvent without consequence.

## Status

**ADMITTED AS A FIELD, NOT YET A SPECIFIC INCOME OR CLASS LABEL.**

---

# V. Material Competence — A

A needs at least one lived competence involving:

- keeping systems functioning;
- remembering dependencies;
- preserving continuity;
- noticing failure before it becomes crisis.

Do **not** yet assign a profession.

Possible competence domains:
- managing schedules;
- budgets;
- records;
- long projects;
- maintenance;
- logistics;
- caregiving;
- institutional continuity.

## Why this earns entry

A's attention must come from actual usefulness.

We should see A being right about:
- recurrence;
- consequence;
- timing;
- commitments.

A must sometimes save the day.

Otherwise continuity becomes merely controlling.

## What this changes in prose

A naturally notices:
- what is due;
- what is wearing out;
- what was promised;
- what happened last time;
- which small failure will become large.

## Status

**ADMITTED AS COMPETENCE, PROFESSION OPEN.**

---

# VI. Material Competence — B

B needs at least one lived competence involving:

- present-state judgment;
- adaptation;
- practical improvisation;
- response under changing conditions.

Do **not** yet assign a profession.

Possible competence domains:
- live operations;
- hospitality;
- performance;
- crisis response;
- sales;
- repair;
- field work;
- production;
- caregiving under uncertainty;
- entrepreneurship.

## Why this earns entry

B's attention must also be useful.

We should see B being right when:
- the plan no longer fits;
- an exception matters;
- the room has changed;
- action is needed before the explanation is finished.

B must also sometimes save the day.

## What this changes in prose

B notices:
- the person waiting too long;
- the machine sounding different;
- the room going flat;
- the plan breaking;
- the faster route;
- the body signaling change.

## Status

**ADMITTED AS COMPETENCE, PROFESSION OPEN.**

---

# VII. Outside Social World — A

A needs at least one social field in which their historical / patterning intelligence is respected rather than treated as a marital flaw.

Reason:

The reader must see A outside B's interpretation.

Possible environments:
- colleagues who rely on A;
- family members who call A because A remembers;
- a community role where continuity matters;
- a long-standing friendship.

## Why this earns entry

Without an outside witness, A risks becoming only "the spouse who cannot let go."

A needs a world that says:

> **THIS WAY OF SEEING HAS VALUE.**

## Status

**ADMITTED AS REQUIREMENT, SPECIFICS OPEN.**

---

# VIII. Outside Social World — B

B needs at least one field in which adaptation / present judgment is respected.

Possible environments:
- people who rely on B in changing situations;
- friends who experience B as enlivening;
- a practical setting where B's improvisation is genuinely useful;
- a newer relationship or community that sees current capacities clearly.

## Why this earns entry

Without this, B risks becoming only "the spouse trying to reinvent themselves."

The reader needs evidence:

> **THIS WAY OF SEEING ALSO HAS VALUE.**

## Status

**ADMITTED AS REQUIREMENT, SPECIFICS OPEN.**

---

# IX. One Bodily Fact Each

Do not yet choose a dramatic illness.

Each person should have one modest bodily fact that changes attention.

## A — candidate bodily fields

Something that reinforces awareness of continuity without defining identity:
- sleep issue;
- recurring joint pain;
- blood pressure;
- old injury;
- hearing change;
- digestive routine.

The body can remind A that history accumulates.

## B — candidate bodily fields

Something that makes present-state signal impossible to ignore:
- changed energy;
- hormonal transition;
- recurring pain that presents differently;
- altered vision;
- physical training;
- recovery from injury.

The body can remind B that the present may not obey old categories.

## Rule

The bodily fact should produce scenes before it produces meaning.

## Status

**CATEGORY ADMITTED, SPECIFICS NOT YET SELECTED.**

---

# X. Family Relation Outside the Marriage

At least one external family relation should matter because marriage alone cannot carry all identity.

Possible roles:
- aging parent;
- adult or near-adult child;
- sibling.

## Why this earns entry

External family relations test:
- memory;
- obligation;
- changed identity;
- continuity across generations.

They also provide independent accounts of A and B.

The marriage should not be the only archive.

## Important restraint

Do not choose children merely to raise stakes.

If children exist, they must have their own standing.

## Status

**ADMITTED AS NEED FOR EXTERNAL FAMILY FIELD, EXACT RELATION OPEN.**

---

# XI. Current Phase of Marriage

The marriage should not begin in open collapse.

That would overdetermine the reading.

Preferred initial state:

> **FUNCTIONING, INTIMATE ENOUGH TO CONTAIN REAL CARE, BUT EXPERIENCING A SUBTLE ASYMMETRY OF UPDATE.**

They still:
- make plans;
- share routines;
- know one another's bodies;
- manage practical life;
- make each other laugh.

But one or both have begun to feel:

**THE PERSON I AM BECOMING IS ARRIVING FASTER THAN THIS RELATION KNOWS HOW TO SEE.**

This keeps the marriage worth preserving before it becomes pressured.

## Status

**ADMITTED PROVISIONALLY.**

---

# XII. No Infidelity Requirement

Do not use infidelity as the default mechanism.

Why:

It would immediately:
- moralize the field;
- simplify allegiance;
- make the new recognition too easy to externalize into another lover;
- turn the marriage into a familiar triangle.

Infidelity may later earn entry.

It should not be used to manufacture pressure the relation already contains.

## Status

**EXCLUDED AS DEFAULT ENGINE.**

---

# XIII. No Major Secret Requirement

Do not begin with:
- hidden child;
- secret crime;
- secret affair;
- concealed diagnosis;
- dramatic past revelation.

Why:

The present inquiry is strong precisely because ordinary life already carries enough pressure.

A "big secret" risks turning relation into puzzle.

## Status

**EXCLUDED AS DEFAULT ENGINE.**

---

# XIV. No Symbolic Profession Pairing

Do not cast:

A = accountant / lawyer / archivist

B = artist / musician / traveler

merely because the jobs match the perceptual fields.

That would make the characters legible too quickly.

Preferred:

Each profession or material world should contain **cross-pressure**.

A's work should require some improvisation.

B's work should require some continuity.

This keeps them human.

---

# XVII. Strongest New Discovery — Biography Should Enter Through Friction

A fact earns entry when it creates pressure in the prose.

Examples:

A's prior responsibility matters when:
someone forgets something consequential.

B's practical competence matters when:
the plan fails.

Economic class matters when:
a desired change has a price.

Age matters when:
the body changes the scene.

Family matters when:
someone outside the marriage remembers differently.

Therefore:

> **BIOGRAPHY SHOULD ARRIVE AS FRICTION, NOT AS INFORMATION.**

This is the strongest current craft law.

---

# XVIII. The First Facts Most Likely to Matter

The next details most likely to change the prose are:

2. exact relationship duration;
3. whether they have children / dependents;
4. the kind of material work each is actually good at;
5. whether one or both have aging parents;
6. current economic commitments;
7. one bodily fact each.

But these should be selected by **scene productivity**, not by realism checklist.

# XIX. Current Edge

The minimum grounding is now enough to begin **biographical stress testing**.

Do not choose the final biography.

Generate 3–4 candidate grounding configurations using the same perceptual pair.

Each configuration should vary:
- material competence;
- social world;
- family structure;
- bodily pressure;
- economic consequence.

Then test:

> **WHICH CONFIGURATION PRODUCES THE MOST LITERARY LIFE WITHOUT MAKING THE CHARACTERS FEEL ENGINEERED?**
