# Independent Standing / Handoff Ethics Sources

## Purpose

External sources consulted to discipline the ethical architecture of the Irreducible Handoff.

These sources do not prove The Outside.

They provide established ethical frameworks for:
- autonomy;
- informed consent;
- risk minimization;
- justice;
- narrator rights;
- community self-determination;
- shared governance;
- preservation and use.

---

## 1. Belmont Report — HHS

Source:
https://www.hhs.gov/ohrp/regulations-and-policy/belmont-report/read-the-belmont-report/index.html

Status:
AUTHORITATIVE U.S. RESEARCH-ETHICS SOURCE.

Key principles:
- respect for persons;
- beneficence;
- justice.

Relevant applications:
- informed consent;
- risk / benefit assessment;
- equitable subject selection.

Use in this project:
Analogical ethical discipline for any human-involving handoff.

Important limitation:
The Irreducible Handoff is not automatically human-subjects research merely because it involves people. Legal / regulatory status depends on actual implementation.

# Derived Project Principles

The following are PROJECT INTERPRETATIONS informed by the sources above.

They are not claims made verbatim by those sources.

## A. Refusal is formal success

If a human participant's refusal destroys the work, the person was functioning as required material rather than an independent participant.

## B. Consent is architectural

For a human handoff, consent is not paperwork added after design.

The ability to say no is what makes the seam independently governed.

## C. Community standing requires governance

A community cannot be reduced to:
- one spokesperson;
- a convenience sample;
- a simulated population;
- an aggregate without internal difference.

## D. Use the least coercive sufficient Other

If the needed independent standing can be supplied by:
- public source;
- external institution;
- material condition;

do not escalate to intimate human participation merely for dramatic authenticity.

## E. More transformation requires more permission

Interview consent does not automatically authorize:
- synthetic voice;
- generated persona;
- AI continuation;
- avatar;
- new words attributed to the contributor.

Separate transformation requires separate authorization.
