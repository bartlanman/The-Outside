## AI Companionship / Social Spillover

### Zhang et al. — Nature Human Behaviour — 2026
**Title:** Interaction with AI companions and psychological well-being  
**Published:** 4 August 2026  
**Source:** Nature Human Behaviour  
**URL:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-026-02516-2

Supports:
- companionship-like bonds with socially responsive AI;
- empirical study of AI companion use and well-being.

Does **not** prove:
- equivalence between AI and human relationships;
- that all AI use produces attachment.

**URL:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-026-02569-3

## Art / Machine Reconstruction of Imagined Images

### Museum of Modern Art — Pierre Huyghe: UUmwelt
**Exhibition:** 1 July – 29 November 2026  
**URL:** https://www.moma.org/calendar/exhibitions/5912

MoMA describes:
- human subject imagining images in fMRI;
- neural-network reconstruction / interpretation from recorded brain activity;
- thousands of hallucinatory / fragmentary generated images;
- visitor gaze and other data influencing real-time sequencing.

Supports project claim:
the inner / outer and imagined / generated boundary is becoming artistically operable.

Does **not** support:
transparent or complete "mind reading."
