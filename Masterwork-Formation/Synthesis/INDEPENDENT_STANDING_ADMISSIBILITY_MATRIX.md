# Independent Standing Admissibility Matrix

## Purpose

Use this matrix before selecting what will occupy an Irreducible Handoff seam.

The goal is not to maximize dramatic intensity.

The goal is to identify the **least coercive form of independent standing sufficient to make the formal operation real**.

| Class | External provenance | Can contradict | Can refuse | Can change work | Human ethical risk | Best formal use | Main danger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Documentary source | High | High | No | High | Low-medium | Evidentiary correction | Decontextualization / provenance error |
| Independent institution | High | High | Yes, procedurally | High | Low-medium | Jurisdictional decision | Misstating response as endorsement |
| Consenting human | High | Very high | Yes | Very high | High | Relational handoff | Instrumentalization / coercion |

# Admissibility Questions

Before a seam is approved, answer:

1. What class of standing is this?
2. What exists independently of the work?
3. What jurisdiction remains outside the author?
4. What can this term actually change?
5. Could the work substitute a simulation and still claim the same operation?
6. If human, can refusal occur without penalty?
7. If community, who governs participation and use?
8. If documentary, who maintains provenance?
9. If material, what exactly is being measured or constrained?
10. What before / after trace will be preserved?
11. What would make us stop using this seam?
12. Why is this class necessary rather than a lower-risk alternative?

# Least-Coercive Sufficient Other Principle

Use this order as an ethical presumption, not an absolute ladder:

PUBLIC / DOCUMENTARY CONSTRAINT
↓
INDEPENDENT DATA / MATERIAL CONDITION
↓
INSTITUTIONAL JURISDICTION
↓
CONSENTING PROFESSIONAL / COLLABORATOR
↓
INTIMATE HUMAN TESTIMONY
↓
COMMUNITY / VULNERABLE-PARTICIPANT INVOLVEMENT

Move downward only when the formal operation genuinely requires capabilities unavailable above.

Do not move downward for:
- drama;
- emotional intensity;
- authenticity branding;
- prestige;
- spectacle.

The stronger the vulnerability, the stronger the burden of justification.

# Current Recommendation

For the **first prototype** of the Irreducible Handoff:

DO NOT BEGIN
WITH INTIMATE HUMAN MATERIAL.

Prototype the seam using some combination of:
- independently custodied source;
- external institutional process;
- independently maintained data;
- low-risk material condition.

Only after the formal operation proves it requires reciprocal human standing should human collaboration become necessary.

This protects both:
- the Other;
- the integrity of the formal research.

It also creates a stronger test:

IF THE OPERATION ONLY FEELS REAL
WHEN A HUMAN BEING
IS PLACED AT RISK,

THE OPERATION IS NOT READY.
