FIXED CORE

---

# I. Why a Hybrid Medium Is Being Considered

The Relational Return requires two things that pull in opposite directions.

## Requirement A — Stability

The receiver must return to substantially the same material.

Without stability, the receiver cannot know whether:
- they changed;
- the relation changed;
- or the work simply rewrote itself.

## Requirement B — Live answerability

Something outside the fixed authorial system must be able to enter and alter warrant.

Without live answerability, the handoff is already authored.

Conventional fixed media solve A better than B.

Interactive media solve B better than A.

The hybrid architecture assigns each medium the jurisdiction it handles best.

---

# II. The Fixed Core

The core is the durable deposit.

It should contain:
- the first traversal;
- the material that will later be re-seen;
- enough formal integrity to matter without the interface;
- stable version identity.

The core may be:
- textual;
- visual;
- cinematic;
- sonic;
- spatial;
- multimodal.

The current audit does **not** yet choose which.

## Constitutional requirement

> **THE CORE MUST NOT SILENTLY CHANGE AFTER THE HANDOFF.**

If later revisions occur, they must be new editions with preserved trace.

---

# III. The Live Seam

The seam has a narrower jurisdiction.

It may:
- receive a receiver commitment;
- open or query an independent source;
- register an external event;
- introduce an institutional response;
- preserve before / after state;
- direct the receiver back to the core.

It should not:
- become the primary storyteller;
- explain The Outside;
- generate endless personalized content;
- rewrite the core;
- manufacture the Other.

## Constitutional requirement

> **THE INTERFACE MAY MEDIATE CONTACT. IT MAY NOT SUBSTITUTE FOR CONTACT.**

---

# IV. Is the Interface Part of the Work?

Yes, but not in the same way as the core.

The core is the **deposit body**.

The interface is the **encounter body**.

This yields a two-body architecture:

## BODY 1 — DEPOSIT

What can survive.
What can be inherited.
What can be returned to.

## BODY 2 — ENCOUNTER

What can register:
- receiver state;
- present conditions;
- independent response;
- change.

The work is the relation between them.

This may be a new medium category for the project:

> **DUAL-BODY WORK**

or:

> **DEPOSIT BODY / ENCOUNTER BODY**

---

# V. What Happens if the Live Seam Dies?

This is a decisive test.

The work should degrade gracefully.

If the interface disappears:
- the core remains encounterable;
- prior handoff records may remain archived;
- future readers can know that another encounter body once existed;
- the work may lose one operational layer but should not become meaningless.

Therefore:

> **THE ENCOUNTER BODY MAY DEEPEN THE WORK, BUT MUST NOT BE ITS LIFE SUPPORT.**

This directly satisfies the Masterwork Formation requirement that the deposit survive the death of its explanation.

---

# VI. What Is the Minimum Interaction?

The prototype research strongly suggests:

VERY LITTLE.

The receiver need not:
- role-play;
- chat indefinitely;
- build a character;
- navigate a giant world;
- learn a game system.

A minimum seam might require only:

1. encounter core;
2. commit a judgment / expectation / relation;
3. cross to independently standing source;
4. preserve change;
5. return.

The interface should be thinner than the work.

> **MINIMUM SUFFICIENT INTERFACE.**

This protects against technology becoming spectacle.

---

# VII. Does AI Belong in the Live Seam?

Potentially yes.

But AI's role must be jurisdictionally explicit.

AI may:
- orient;
- summarize;
- query;
- translate;
- compare;
- preserve state;
- surface source material;
- assist return.

AI may not count as the independent standing by itself.

The seam must distinguish:

AI MEDIATION

from

ACTUAL EXTERNAL SOURCE / PERSON / EVENT / CONDITION.

Therefore:

> **AI CAN GUIDE THE HANDOFF. AI CANNOT BE THE FACT THAT MAKES THE HANDOFF IRREDUCIBLE.**

This is a particularly strong contemporary fit.

---

# VIII. Can Future Technology Replace the Interface?

Yes, if the interface protocol is deposited.

The exact software should not be sacred.

The durable deposit should preserve:
- sequence;
- constraints;
- admissibility rules;
- handoff protocol;
- trace requirements.

Then later technologies may re-realize the encounter body.

This echoes:
- score and performance;
- script and production;
- game and play;
- text and reading.

The interface becomes a realization layer.

This strengthens succession.

---

# IX. Failure Modes

## 1. App Capture

The technology becomes the attraction.

## 2. Platform Death

The seam disappears and takes the work with it.

## 3. Synthetic Other Capture

The interface generates the "outside."

## 4. Personalization Capture

The work becomes a private mirror optimized around the receiver.

## 5. Tutorial Capture

The seam explains what the receiver is meant to learn.

## 6. Core / Seam Divorce

The fixed artifact and interface feel like unrelated products.

## 7. Infinite Update Capture

The live layer never yields to deposit.

## Law 2

## Law 3

# XI. Strongest Current Architecture

The medium problem now compresses to:

> **A STABLE DEPOSIT BODY + A REPLACEABLE ENCOUNTER BODY.**

Or:

**DEPOSIT BODY ⇄ ENCOUNTER BODY**

The receiver moves:

DEPOSIT
-> ENCOUNTER
-> DEPOSIT AGAIN.

That is exactly the Relational Return in medium form.

---

# XII. Current Edge

The medium architecture is now strong enough to move one layer closer to realization.

But do not write the story yet.

Next question:

> **WHAT SHOULD THE FIXED CORE ACTUALLY BE REQUIRED TO DO BEFORE ANY SUBJECT MATTER IS CHOSEN?**

We need a **Core Design Specification**:
- length / scale;
- recurrence;
- what must repeat;
- what cannot change;
- where receiver investment happens;
- how much ambiguity is needed;
- what kind of material can survive two readings;
- how the seam is invited without being announced.

Only after that should subject matter or narrative world be selected.
