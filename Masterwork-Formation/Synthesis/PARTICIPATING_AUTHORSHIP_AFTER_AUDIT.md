# 1. Revised Definition

> **Participating Authorship is the disciplined exercise of bounded authorial jurisdiction: receiving what predates the maker, shaping and relating without claiming total origin, creating conditions in which other voices, participants, evidence, and worlds retain standing, entering the consequences of those conditions, allowing encounter to revise the author, preserving developmental trace, and depositing the work into a succession the author does not control.**

The key correction is:

PARTICIPATING
does not mean
LESS AUTHORIAL.

It means:

AUTHORIAL POWER
SUBJECT TO
EXPLICIT LIMITS OF JURISDICTION.

---

# 2. Bounded Authorial Jurisdiction

The author may legitimately decide:
- what form to attempt;
- what material to select;
- what constraints to establish;
- what sequence to use;
- what to cut;
- when to revise;
- when to stop;
- what to deposit.

The author does not thereby acquire ownership of:
- the history of inherited material;
- another person's experience;
- the standing of actual evidence;
- the interiority of an actual participant;
- the future meaning of the deposit;
- the right to treat every resistance as part of the author's own system;
- the claim that later revision was always already known.

This is the jurisdictional boundary.

---

# 3. The Participating Cycle

Participating Authorship operates recursively:

RECEIVE
-> LOCATE JURISDICTION
-> RELATE
-> SHAPE CONDITIONS
-> RELEASE LOCAL STANDING
-> ENTER CONSEQUENCE
-> ACCEPT COUNTERPRESSURE
-> RETURN
-> REVISE WITHOUT ERASURE
-> STEWARD
-> DEPOSIT
-> WITHDRAW
-> SUCCESSION.

The cycle is not mandatory in one fixed order.

Different works emphasize different offices.

But a living work must retain routes among them.

---

# 4. The Offices

## Carrier

Receives inherited form without pretending to originate it.

Question:
WHAT MUST BE CARRIED?

## Custodian / Curator

Determines relation, boundary, adjacency, and selection.

Question:
WHAT DESERVES TO STAND TOGETHER, AND WHAT REMAINS OUTSIDE?

## Architect / Condition-Maker

Builds constraints and pathways.

Question:
WHAT CONDITIONS MAKE THE OPERATION POSSIBLE?

## Host of Local Standing

Allows voices, participants, agents, or sources to remain locally consequential.

Question:
WHAT CAN SPEAK OR ACT WITHOUT BECOMING MY MOUTHPIECE?

## Participant / Witness

Enters the field of consequence and records what actually occurred.

Question:
WHAT CHANGED ME OR THE WORK?

## Counter-Architect

Tests whether coherence has begun certifying itself.

Question:
WHAT WOULD DISCONFIRM THIS BEAUTIFUL STRUCTURE?

## Steward

Protects value without protecting the system merely because it is the system.

Question:
WHAT IS THIS FOR?

## Returning Reviser

Re-enters prior work after time and changed capacity.

Question:
WHAT CAN I NOW SEE THAT AUTHOR_0 COULD NOT?

## Depositor

Releases the work into succession.

Question:
CAN THE FUTURE FIND THIS WITHOUT BEING FORCED TO BECOME ME?

## Absent Author

Allows the work to stand after explanatory authority is gone.

Question:
DOES THE WORK SURVIVE THE DEATH OF ITS EXPLANATION?

# 5. The Outside Test

Participating Authorship requires more than staged plurality.

Something must retain standing the author did not merely grant as a fictional effect.

Possible forms:
- another person;
- independently checkable evidence;
- material consequence;
- historical event;
- embodied limit;
- source provenance;
- institutional resistance;
- reader refusal;
- world constraint;
- failed prediction;
- prior published version;
- future reception.

The minimum question is:

WHAT COULD HAVE MADE THE AUTHOR CHANGE THE WORK?

If the answer is "nothing," the work may still be beautiful.

But it has not demonstrated participating authorship.

---

# 6. The Revision Law

A living authorial system must be capable of:

"I SEE THIS DIFFERENTLY NOW."

It must not protect authority by implying:

"I ALWAYS KNEW."

Therefore:

REVISION
REQUIRES
DEVELOPMENTAL TRACE.

A changed work should preserve enough of the prior state to distinguish:
- learning from rewriting history;
- deeper position from opportunistic retcon;
- correction from disappearance of evidence.

This is especially important for persistent AI-mediated systems.

---

# 7. The Particularity Test

The spine repeatedly discovered that large-scale relation becomes answerable through the particular.

Hesse:
Game -> Tito.

Tolkien:
Quest -> Shire / land / family.

Le Guin:
Archmage-scale world -> Tenar / Therru / household / care.

Therefore a Participating Author should ask:

WHERE DOES THE LARGE CLAIM BECOME SOMEONE'S ACTUAL CONSEQUENCE?

If the work can only operate at the level of civilization, humanity, culture, meaning, intelligence, or systems without ever becoming answerable to a body, person, place, obligation, or cost, it remains vulnerable to hermetic abstraction.

# 8. The AI Jurisdiction

Generative AI radically expands:
- generation,
- variation,
- retrieval,
- comparison,
- simulation,
- recombination,
- compression,
- world production.

Those capacities do not settle authorship.

The Participating Author remains responsible for:
- source standing;
- provenance;
- selection;
- boundary;
- warrant;
- which generated possibility is admitted;
- which beautiful coherence is rejected;
- what actual evidence changes the model;
- whether participants retain independence;
- whether revision preserves trace;
- what is deposited.

AI may participate extensively in poiesis.

The jurisdictional question remains:

WHO IS ANSWERABLE FOR THE RELATION BETWEEN GENERATED MATERIAL AND WHAT HAS INDEPENDENT STANDING?

# 10. Participating Authorship Test

Before deposit, ask:

1. What did this work receive that it did not originate?
2. What relations were deliberately authored?
3. Where does authorial jurisdiction stop?
4. Which voices or sources retain local standing?
5. What could refuse the architecture?
6. What actually changed the work?
7. Where did coherence outrun warrant?
8. Where did large-scale meaning become particular responsibility?
9. What did the author revise?
10. Is the prior state still visible?
11. What remains unresolved?
12. What can a future reader reject while still finding something alive?
13. Would the work survive if the author could no longer explain it?

The test cannot certify greatness.

It can test whether the deposit has earned release.

# 11. The New Compression

The authorship spine no longer compresses to:

AUTHOR
-> WORK
-> READER.

Nor even:

MAKER
-> ARCHITECT
-> PARTICIPANT
-> STEWARD.

The stronger compression is:

INHERITANCE
-> BOUNDED JURISDICTION
-> CONDITION
-> OTHER STANDING
-> CONSEQUENCE
-> REVISION
-> STEWARDSHIP
-> DEPOSIT
-> SUCCESSION.

Or in human terms:

RECEIVE
-> MAKE
-> MEET
-> CHANGE
-> RETURN
-> LEAVE.

---

# Conclusion

The Participating Author is not the person who gives up authorship.

The Participating Author is the person who knows which parts of authorship remain theirs to exercise and which claims would exceed their jurisdiction.

The author is responsible for making the conditions strong enough to matter.

The author is responsible for what those conditions exclude.

The author is responsible for noticing when encounter changes the warrant.

The author is responsible for preserving the trace of revision.

The author is responsible for release.

The author is not responsible for owning the future.

> **PARTICIPATING AUTHORSHIP IS STRONG AUTHORSHIP THAT REMAINS CORRECTABLE.**
