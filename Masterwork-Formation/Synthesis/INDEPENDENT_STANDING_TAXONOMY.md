# Independent Standing Taxonomy

## Status

Working taxonomy for the Irreducible Handoff.

Question:

> **WHAT FORMS OF INDEPENDENT STANDING CAN ENTER THE HANDOFF ETHICALLY AND MATERIALLY, WITHOUT BECOMING PROPS FOR THE WORK?**

This document distinguishes:

UNPREDICTABILITY
from
INDEPENDENT STANDING.

Something is not an Actual Other merely because the author does not know what it will do.

Randomness can surprise.

A simulated agent can surprise.

A procedural system can surprise.

Surprise alone does not establish standing.

Independent standing requires some combination of:
- provenance outside the work;
- interests or rules not authored by the work;
- capacity to contradict;
- capacity to refuse;
- material consequence;
- persistence outside the work;
- a history the work did not create;
- authority over its own contribution.

Different classes satisfy these differently.

---

# I. Documentary Standing

## Definition

A record, source, prior deposit, or externally custodied document whose existence and content do not depend on the work.

Examples:
- archival record;
- published prior version;
- court filing;
- legislative record;
- historical document;
- independently maintained database;
- authenticated correspondence;
- recorded testimony deposited elsewhere.

## What it can do

A documentary source can:
- contradict the model;
- narrow a claim;
- establish provenance;
- reveal omission;
- preserve developmental trace;
- force revision.

It cannot:
- consent as a person;
- negotiate;
- change its mind;
- refuse in real time.

## Strength

HIGH EVIDENTIARY STANDING.

LOW LIVE RELATIONAL STANDING.

## Ethical conditions

- provenance must be real;
- quotation / use must respect copyright and privacy;
- sensitive records require care even if accessible;
- excerpting must not reverse the source's meaning;
- uncertainty about authenticity must remain visible.

## Best use in handoff

MODEL
-> DOCUMENTARY CONTRADICTION
-> RETURN
-> REVISED WARRANT.

---

# II. Institutional Standing

## Definition

An institution, organization, court, archive, governing body, business, public agency, community organization, or other entity operating under rules and interests independent of the work.

## What it can do

An institution can:
- refuse;
- delay;
- deny access;
- approve conditionally;
- issue decisions;
- enforce rules;
- contradict the work's assumptions;
- create real procedural consequence.

## Strength

VERY HIGH PROCEDURAL STANDING.

The response is not merely content.

It comes from an external jurisdiction.

## Ethical conditions

- do not misrepresent the work to obtain cooperation;
- do not manipulate staff into becoming unwitting performers;
- use normal procedures unless explicit collaboration is agreed;
- preserve the difference between institutional statement and individual employee opinion;
- do not claim institutional endorsement from mere response.

## Best use in handoff

MODEL
-> CLAIM / REQUEST / INTERPRETATION
-> EXTERNAL INSTITUTION
-> INDEPENDENT DECISION
-> CONSEQUENCE
-> RETURN.

---

# III. Human Standing

## Definition

An actual person participating under their own agency.

This is the strongest relational class and therefore carries the strongest ethical obligations.

## What a person can do

A person can:
- consent;
- refuse;
- withdraw;
- contradict;
- reinterpret;
- remain silent;
- change terms;
- refuse the author's framing;
- continue to exist beyond the work.

## Strength

VERY HIGH RELATIONAL STANDING.

## Ethical baseline

The handoff must treat the person as a participant with jurisdiction, not as raw material.

At minimum:
- transparent purpose;
- informed consent;
- understandable description of use;
- voluntariness;
- right to refuse;
- reasonable ability to withdraw before agreed release points;
- risk minimization;
- privacy choices;
- clarity about recording / preservation / public access;
- fair credit and compensation where appropriate;
- no deceptive "surprise" whose artistic effect depends on violating consent.

The Belmont tradition grounds respect for persons in autonomy, beneficence in minimizing harm, and justice in fair distribution of burdens and benefits.

Oral-history best practice adds a particularly relevant model:
- ongoing consent;
- attention to power differences;
- narrator review where possible;
- ability to restrict, redact, clarify, or decline release;
- clear preservation and access terms.

## Formal implication

The work cannot demand that the person produce an aesthetically useful answer.

A refusal is a successful handoff.

Silence is a successful handoff.

Withdrawal is a successful handoff.

An inconvenient answer is a successful handoff.

If the work requires cooperation to preserve its form, the Other has already been reduced to function.

## Best use in handoff

MODEL
-> APPROACH
-> CONSENT / REFUSAL
-> ACTUAL PERSON'S ACCOUNT OR ACTION
-> WORK CHANGES
-> PERSON RETAINS OWN STANDING.

---

# IV. Community / Collective Standing

## Definition

A community, cultural group, neighborhood, profession, collective, movement, or other plural social body with its own internal diversity.

## Central caution

NO SINGLE PARTICIPANT
EQUALS
THE COMMUNITY.

A spokesperson can speak from a position.

They cannot automatically exhaust the collective.

## What community standing can do

A community process can:
- define what questions matter;
- reject the author's framing;
- establish terms of engagement;
- govern access;
- set boundaries;
- produce competing internal accounts;
- redirect benefit;
- refuse extraction.

## Strength

VERY HIGH SOCIAL STANDING
WHEN GOVERNANCE IS REAL.

## Ethical baseline

Community-engaged research provides a strong analogical discipline:
- transparency of purpose;
- reciprocity;
- self-determination;
- inclusive participation;
- shared governance;
- co-created goals;
- mutual benefit;
- attention to history and context;
- sustainability.

The crucial formal lesson is:

DO NOT ASK A COMMUNITY
TO AUTHENTICATE
A WORK ABOUT THE COMMUNITY.

Instead:

ALLOW THE COMMUNITY
TO ALTER THE TERMS
UNDER WHICH THE WORK
MAY CONTINUE.

## Best use in handoff

AUTHORIAL FRAME
-> COMMUNITY GOVERNANCE
-> TERMS CHANGE
-> WORK ACCEPTS NEW BOUNDARY / OBLIGATION / LIMIT.

---

# V. Material / Environmental Standing

## Definition

A physical process or condition whose behavior is not authored by the work.

Examples:
- weather;
- tide;
- growth;
- decay;
- temperature;
- erosion;
- astronomical event;
- acoustic property;
- biological process;
- measured physical constraint.

## What it can do

Material conditions can:
- constrain timing;
- alter form;
- prevent an event;
- leave trace;
- introduce irreversible change;
- force adaptation.

## Strength

HIGH MATERIAL STANDING.

LOW INTENTIONAL / RELATIONAL STANDING.

## Central caution

DO NOT ANTHROPOMORPHIZE CONSTRAINT INTO CONSENT.

The weather does not "refuse."

A river does not "agree."

Material standing is real, but it is not personhood.

## Best use in handoff

PLANNED FORM
-> MATERIAL CONDITION
-> FORM MUST ALTER
-> TRACE PRESERVED.

---

# VI. Event Standing

## Definition

An external historical or public event not caused for the sake of the work.

Examples:
- election result;
- court judgment;
- public policy change;
- strike;
- market closure;
- institutional decision;
- natural disaster;
- public protest;
- unexpected historical event.

## What it can do

An event can:
- invalidate premise;
- change context;
- create moral obligation;
- make continuation inappropriate;
- force pause;
- redirect the work.

## Strength

HIGH HISTORICAL STANDING.

## Ethical caution

DO NOT TURN SUFFERING INTO A FORMAL DEVICE.

A tragedy is not valuable because it gives the work an Outside.

If real harm enters the field, the work may acquire an obligation to:
- stop;
- change;
- withdraw;
- redirect benefit;
- preserve silence.

The work does not outrank the event.

## Best use in handoff

WORK-IN-PROGRESS
-> EXTERNAL EVENT
-> ORIGINAL FORM LOSES WARRANT
-> REVISION / SUSPENSION / OBLIGATION.

---

# VII. Successor Standing

## Definition

A later reader, performer, editor, custodian, community, or inheritor who receives the deposit after the originating author has withdrawn.

## What successors can do

They can:
- reinterpret;
- reject;
- recover excluded material;
- create new relations;
- change status;
- perform differently;
- preserve;
- neglect;
- revive.

## Strength

VERY HIGH SUCCESSIONAL STANDING.

But it is delayed.

It cannot perform the immediate handoff unless the work is designed to remain genuinely open across time.

## Best use

DEPOSIT
-> AUTHOR WITHDRAWS
-> FUTURE RECEIVER
-> NEW STANDING.

This is the deepest final Outside because the author cannot control which future receiver arrives.

---

# VIII. External Data / Measurement Standing

## Definition

Data generated or maintained outside the work from independently governed measurement or observation.

Examples:
- census data;
- weather station readings;
- public-health statistics;
- audited financial data;
- independently maintained scientific measurements;
- election returns.

## What it can do

Data can:
- contradict the model;
- expose tail conditions;
- force recalculation;
- prevent narrative convenience.

## Strength

MEDIUM TO HIGH EVIDENTIARY STANDING.

## Caution

DATA
IS NOT
THE THING IT MEASURES.

A dataset can preserve an external constraint while still compressing:
- people;
- context;
- minority positions;
- measurement error.

Use provenance and uncertainty.

## Best use

MODEL
-> PREDICTION / CLAIM
-> INDEPENDENT DATA
-> DELTA
-> REVISION.

# IX. What Does NOT Qualify by Itself

## Randomness

Randomness can create unpredictability.

It does not create standing.

RNG != OTHER.

## Another AI model

A separately hosted model may be operationally independent from the author's system.

But it remains synthetic response.

MODEL_B != ACTUAL OTHER merely because MODEL_A does not control it.

It can function as:
- constraint;
- rival model;
- comparison.

It cannot alone satisfy the Actual Other requirement.

## Generated persona

No.

A persona can have consistency.

It does not have independent provenance or jurisdiction.

## Simulated population

No, not as substitute for a real population.

It can be a model to test.

It cannot stand in for the people whose distribution it simulates.

## Anonymous crowd output

Not automatically.

Aggregation can erase the very local standing the handoff is meant to preserve.

Crowd participation requires:
- consent where appropriate;
- provenance;
- aggregation rules;
- protection against extraction;
- preservation of minority / tail positions.

## Web search output

Not by itself.

Search is mediation.

The standing belongs, if anywhere, to the underlying source.

## "The public"

Too abstract.

The public is not one Other.

# X. Independent Standing Test

Before allowing something to occupy the handoff seam, ask:

## 1. External provenance
DID THIS EXIST OUTSIDE THE WORK?

## 2. Separate jurisdiction
DOES IT OPERATE UNDER RULES, INTERESTS, OR CONDITIONS THE WORK DOES NOT OWN?

## 3. Contradiction
CAN IT MAKE THE WORK WRONG?

## 4. Refusal
IF IT IS AN AGENT OR GROUP, CAN IT SAY NO?

## 5. Non-substitutability
COULD THE WORK REPLACE IT WITH A SIMULATION WITHOUT LOSING THE ESSENTIAL OPERATION?

If yes, independent standing is weak.

## 6. Consequence
CAN ITS RESPONSE ACTUALLY CHANGE WHAT THE WORK MAY DO NEXT?

## 7. Persistence
DOES IT CONTINUE TO EXIST OUTSIDE THE WORK'S USE OF IT?

## 8. Trace
CAN THE BEFORE / AFTER DIFFERENCE BE PRESERVED?

## 9. Ethical jurisdiction
IF A PERSON OR COMMUNITY IS INVOLVED, DO THEY RETAIN CONTROL OVER THEIR OWN PARTICIPATION?

## 10. Non-instrumentality
WOULD THEIR REFUSAL STILL COUNT AS A SUCCESSFUL ENCOUNTER?

This last question is decisive.

If NO:

THE WORK NEEDS THEM
AS A PROP.

# XI. Strongest Candidate Classes for the Irreducible Handoff

The strongest immediate candidates are:

## A. Independently custodied source
Low interpersonal risk.
High provenance.
Can materially correct the model.

## B. Independent institution / governed process
Can refuse and create consequence.
Strong jurisdictional seam.

## C. Consenting human collaborator
Strongest relational standing.
Requires highest ethical discipline.

## D. Community-governed contribution
Potentially strongest social standing.
Must be co-governed, not extracted.

## E. Material condition
Strong nonhuman constraint.
Useful where physical consequence matters.

## F. Future successor
Strongest final standing.
Best suited to the deposit / succession layer.

No single class needs to carry the entire work.

A mature realization may use different classes at different seams.

---

# XII. Current Finding

The Irreducible Handoff should not be built around:

"FIND A REAL PERSON TO MAKE IT AUTHENTIC."

That would violate its own law.

The stronger principle is:

> **THE HANDOFF MUST PASS TO A JURISDICTION THAT EXISTS FOR REASONS OTHER THAN COMPLETING THE WORK.**

That jurisdiction may belong to:
- a person;
- a community;
- an institution;
- a source;
- a material process;
- history;
- a future receiver.

The handoff is irreducible when:

THE WORK CANNOT SUBSTITUTE
A MORE CONVENIENT REPRESENTATION
WITHOUT CHANGING
THE NATURE OF THE ENCOUNTER.
