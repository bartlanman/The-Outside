# Attentional Formation Fields — Before Biography

## Status

Pre-biography formation research.

This document does **not** assign:
- childhood;
- parents;
- profession;
- class position;
- trauma;
- diagnosis;
- age;
- gender;
- hometown;
- plot.

It asks:

> **WHAT KINDS OF LIVED CONDITIONS COULD TRAIN THE CURRENT PERCEPTUAL FIELDS WITHOUT REDUCING EITHER PERSON TO A SYMBOLIC JOB OR A SINGLE BACKSTORY CAUSE?**

Current perceptual tendencies:

### A
**CONTINUITY / PATTERN / LANGUAGE / OBLIGATION**

### B
**PRESENT / PARTICULAR / ACTION / POSSIBILITY**

The purpose is not to explain the characters completely.

It is to identify the kinds of consequence that could have taught their attention where to go.

---

# I. Core Principle — Attention Is Trained by Consequence

People do not usually wake up valuing:
- chronology;
- present salience;
- exact language;
- practical action;
- promise;
- exception;

as abstract philosophies.

They learn what deserves attention because some kinds of noticing repeatedly matter.

Working law:

> **ATTENTION FOLLOWS CONSEQUENCE.**

A person may become highly sensitive to continuity because:
- forgetting created damage;
- promises mattered;
- records protected people;
- instability made sequence precious.

Another may become highly sensitive to present fit because:
- old assumptions caused damage;
- quick adaptation mattered;
- embodied reality changed faster than rules;
- survival depended on noticing what was different this time.

This keeps perceptual style grounded in life.

# II. Formation Field 1 — Repeated Consequence

## What it trains

A tendency toward:
- pattern;
- recurrence;
- memory;
- causal sequence.

A person learns:

> **THE PAST IS USEFUL BECAUSE IT HAPPENS AGAIN.**

Possible lived environments:
- recurring family crises;
- cyclical financial strain;
- chronic illness management;
- repeated institutional failure;
- seasonal work;
- maintenance-heavy systems;
- repeated conflict patterns.

## Gift

Recognizes what others dismiss as isolated.

## Risk

Can overgeneralize.

Can mistake:
THIS HAS HAPPENED BEFORE

for:

THIS IS WHAT THIS ALWAYS IS.

## Strongest affinity

A.

# XIV. Do Not Reduce Formation to Job

A profession can train attention.

It should not explain a person.

Bad design:

A is an accountant, therefore A sees patterns.

B is an artist, therefore B sees possibility.

This is symbolic casting.

Better:

A's life has repeatedly rewarded:
- remembering;
- exact wording;
- follow-through;
- long-horizon consequence.

B's life has repeatedly rewarded:
- noticing change;
- acting under uncertainty;
- present-state judgment;
- improvisation.

Their work may reflect those capacities.

Or contradict them.

Working rule:

> **A JOB MAY REHEARSE A PERCEPTUAL HABIT. IT SHOULD NOT FUNCTION AS ITS SYMBOLIC DEFINITION.**

---

# XV. Do Not Reduce Formation to Trauma

Another danger is monocausal psychology.

Bad design:

A had an unreliable parent, therefore A needs continuity.

B had a controlling parent, therefore B needs freedom.

Human formation is more distributed than that.

Prefer:

**TEMPERAMENT
+
REPEATED CONSEQUENCE
+
SOCIAL ROLE
+
MATERIAL RESPONSIBILITY
+
CHANCE
+
RELATIONSHIP HISTORY**

No single event should explain the whole person.

Working law:

> **BACKSTORY SHOULD COMPLICATE ATTENTION, NOT SOLVE IT.**

# XVI. Strongest Formation Logic for A

A's perceptual field becomes plausible if several conditions overlap:

1. **Repeated consequence**
   - patterns really have mattered.

2. **Custodial responsibility**
   - someone had to remember.

3. **Language with consequence**
   - wording and promise were not trivial.

4. **Long-horizon obligation**
   - choices had delayed effects.

5. **Material memory**
   - objects / records carried what people forgot.

This produces someone for whom:

> **CONTINUITY IS NOT NOSTALGIA. IT IS A FORM OF CARE.**

A may have learned that a life stays intact because someone:
- remembers;
- keeps;
- calls;
- pays;
- shows up;
- notices recurrence;
- does not let yesterday vanish simply because today feels different.

This makes A lovable before it makes A dangerous.

---

# XVII. Strongest Formation Logic for B

B's perceptual field becomes plausible if several conditions overlap:

1. **Rapidly changing conditions**
   - fixed plans often failed.

2. **Misrecognition by stable categories**
   - history became an inaccurate container.

3. **Practical action under pressure**
   - adaptation mattered more than explanation.

4. **Escape from overdetermined futures**
   - possibility had to be actively preserved.

5. **Body as evidence**
   - present-state signal mattered.

This produces someone for whom:

> **REVISION IS NOT FLIGHT. IT IS A FORM OF SURVIVAL.**

B may have learned that a life stays alive because someone:
- notices;
- adjusts;
- acts;
- releases;
- refuses obsolete terms;
- tries another route.

This makes B lovable before it makes B dangerous.

# XVIII. Why These Two Formation Histories Could Fit Together

A's world teaches:

**WITHOUT CONTINUITY, PEOPLE GET HURT.**

B's world teaches:

**WITHOUT REVISION, PEOPLE GET TRAPPED.**

These are both true.

That is why the pair can create a marriage rather than an allegory.

Each initially possesses something the other needs.

A can give B:
- reliable ground;
- remembered continuity;
- evidence that someone stayed.

B can give A:
- permission to improvise;
- present vitality;
- evidence that change need not equal collapse.

Their relationship may have genuinely expanded both capacities.

The eventual crisis should therefore not reveal that attraction was mistaken.

It should pressure a real complementarity.

# XIX. Formation Should Appear Indirectly

The novel should not explain these formation fields early.

They should surface through:
- competence;
- irritation;
- jokes;
- objects;
- what each does automatically;
- what each considers obvious;
- stories told by others;
- what each notices under stress.

Example:

We should not be told:
A learned to value continuity because they were the responsible one.

We should see:
A knows which medication is about to run out without checking.

We should not be told:
B learned to adapt because plans used to fail.

We should see:
B can reorganize an entire bad evening in thirty seconds and make everyone think that was the plan.

The formation is inferred before it is narrated.

# XX. Attentional Formation Is Not Moral Destiny

A history that trained a useful attention does not permanently authorize that attention.

This extends the earlier law:

> **A RELATIONAL VIRTUE CAN BECOME A MISRECOGNITION WHEN IT CONTINUES AFTER ITS JURISDICTION HAS CHANGED.**

Formation explains why the habit deserves respect.

It does not prove the habit remains right.

A's past may justify vigilance.

It does not entitle A to imprison B in history.

B's past may justify revision.

It does not entitle B to erase obligations.

The work must preserve both distinctions.

---

# XXI. Strongest New Discovery — Each Person's Blind Spot May Once Have Been Their Intelligence

This is deeper than:

strength becomes weakness.

The same act of attention that once detected reality can later misread reality because the field changed.

A learned:

PATTERNS SAVE YOU.

Then encounters a moment where:
THE EXCEPTION IS REAL.

B learned:

THE PRESENT CAN OVERRIDE THE OLD MODEL.

Then encounters a moment where:
THE OLD PATTERN IS STILL OPERATING.

Therefore:

> **A BLIND SPOT MAY BE AN INTELLIGENCE APPLIED BEYOND THE CONDITIONS THAT TAUGHT IT.**

This is stronger than assigning flaws.

It means the novel can honor why each person sees as they do.

# XXII. Formation and the Model-Era Milieu

The contemporary model environment may resonate differently with both perceptual formations.

## For A

Models can offer:
- archive;
- pattern;
- exact retrieval;
- cross-time comparison;
- linguistic analysis.

A may find this powerful.

But models can also:
- overfit pattern;
- make recurrence look more deterministic than it is;
- produce smooth historical accounts.

A's intelligence can be amplified into A's danger.

## For B

Models can offer:
- rapid updating;
- personalized reframing;
- alternative language;
- possibility generation.

B may find this liberating.

But models can also:
- validate novelty too quickly;
- generate endless alternate selves;
- turn every current feeling into a plausible new narrative.

B's intelligence can be amplified into B's danger.

This is important.

The contemporary medium should not align with only one character.

> **THE MODEL SHOULD TEMPT EACH PERSON IN A DIFFERENT WAY.**

Do not assign exact uses yet.

## B3 — Highly reliable in action + skeptical of verbal permanence

Produces:

- dislike of identity claims;
- ability to show up while refusing narrative capture.

Potentially strongest because it complicates the assumption that B is less committed.

# XXIV. Preferred Direction

The most promising formation design is **crossed rather than pure**.

### A should contain a hidden capacity for rupture.

A cannot merely be continuity.

Some part of A should:
- crave surprise;
- act impulsively in one domain;
- have made at least one consequential break;
- understand possibility from inside.

### B should contain a deep capacity for continuity.

B cannot merely be change.

Some part of B should:
- keep something for years;
- honor a difficult promise;
- maintain a ritual;
- remain loyal where leaving would be easier.

This is essential.

Otherwise their marriage would never have lasted long enough to become the relational field we need.

# XXVI. Current Edge

The attentional fields are now sufficiently grounded to permit the first cautious move into biography.

But do not build complete biographies yet.

Next ask:

> **WHAT MINIMUM BIOGRAPHICAL FACTS WOULD ACTUALLY CHANGE THE PROSE?**

Candidate categories:
- approximate life stage;
- length / phase of relationship;
- one or two material competencies;
- one social world each moves through;
- class / economic pressure;
- one bodily fact;
- one family relation outside the marriage.

The test is:

> **IF A BIOGRAPHICAL FACT DOES NOT CHANGE WHAT THE PERSON NOTICES, WANTS, FEARS, OR CAN DO IN A SCENE, DO WE NEED IT YET?**

Next object:

**MINIMUM CHARACTER GROUNDING — NOT FULL BACKSTORY.**
