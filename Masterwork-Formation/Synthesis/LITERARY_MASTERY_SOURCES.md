# Evidence Discipline

The Literary Mastery Audit is interpretive criticism.

It should not be confused with the historical evidence status used in formation dossiers.

Use these labels when necessary:

- **DIRECT TEXTUAL OBSERVATION** — feature visible in text consulted.
- **PROJECT INTERPRETATION** — critical synthesis about why the feature matters.
- **TRANSLATION-LIMITED OBSERVATION** — judgment may belong partly to translator's English.
- **DOSSIER-SUPPORTED INTERPRETATION** — based on prior project reading / evidence.
- **OPEN** — requires further primary-text consultation.

The goal of the audit is not to certify greatness.

It is to identify what our previous instrument could not see.
