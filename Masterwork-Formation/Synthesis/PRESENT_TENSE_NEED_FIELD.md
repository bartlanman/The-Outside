# Present-Tense Need Field

## Status

Pre-plot motivational research.

This document does **not** establish:
- inciting incident;
- specific family role;
- plot sequence;
- marriage outcome;
- character names;
- exact jobs;
- exact location;
- AI use.

It asks:

> **WHAT DOES EACH PERSON NEED RIGHT NOW, IN A WAY THAT MAKES THE OTHERS MATTER?**

Current relational field:

**PARTNER A
⇄
PARTNER B
⇄
FAMILY OTHER**

Current perceptual tendencies:

### A
**CONTINUITY / PATTERN / LANGUAGE / OBLIGATION**

### B
**PRESENT / PARTICULAR / ACTION / POSSIBILITY**

The third person remains:
**A PARTICIPANT IN CONSEQUENCE, NOT A COMMENTATOR ON THE THEME.**

---

# I. Core Rule — Need Must Create Relation

A literary need is not just a private preference.

It becomes useful when:

1. the person cannot satisfy it alone;
2. another person's standing matters;
3. action has a cost;
4. delay changes something;
5. satisfying the need threatens another legitimate need.

Working law:

> **A NEED BECOMES NARRATIVE PRESSURE WHEN ANOTHER PERSON HAS STANDING IN ITS SATISFACTION.**

---

# II. A's Present-Tense Need

A should not simply need:

"for things to stay the same."

That would flatten continuity into conservatism.

The stronger need is:

> **A NEEDS THE SHARED PAST TO RETAIN CONSEQUENCE WHILE THE PRESENT CHANGES.**

Or in ordinary human terms:

> **WHAT WE BUILT HAS TO MATTER TO WHAT HAPPENS NEXT.**

A does not necessarily oppose change.

A needs:
- prior promises;
- sacrifices;
- shared decisions;
- carried responsibilities;
- versions of both people that genuinely existed;

not to become irrelevant merely because B's present account has changed.

## Surface forms this need might take

A may want:
- a decision not to be rushed;
- an old commitment acknowledged;
- B to admit that the change costs A something;
- a practical obligation completed before terms change;
- continuity preserved in one area while another changes.

Do not select one yet.

## Emotional core

A fears:

> **THAT THE LIFE THEY CARRIED WILL BE RECLASSIFIED AS A MISTAKE SIMPLY BECAUSE THE PRESENT HAS MOVED.**

A wants not nostalgia, but **standing for prior reality**.

## Literary risk

A can become prosecutorial if every memory becomes evidence.

Protect A by showing:
- present desire;
- humor;
- genuine openness in some domains;
- moments where A chooses change first.

---

# III. B's Present-Tense Need

B should not simply need:

"freedom."

That would flatten possibility into escape.

The stronger need is:

> **B NEEDS PRESENT CHANGE TO ACQUIRE PRACTICAL STANDING BEFORE IT IS FULLY EXPLAINABLE OR APPROVED.**

Or in ordinary human terms:

> **WHAT IS TRUE FOR ME NOW HAS TO BE ALLOWED TO COUNT.**

B may not yet possess:
- the perfect language;
- a final plan;
- a coherent account of why the change occurred.

But B needs room for the present to alter action.

## Surface forms this need might take

B may want:
- to stop one routine;
- begin something new;
- revise a shared plan;
- delay or refuse a prior commitment;
- try a change before defending it as permanent.

Do not select one yet.

## Emotional core

B fears:

> **THAT NOTHING NEW CAN BECOME REAL UNTIL IT SURVIVES A TRIAL CONDUCTED BY THE OLD SELF.**

B wants not permission to erase history, but **standing for emergence**.

## Literary risk

B can become self-exonerating if every current desire is treated as transformation.

Protect B by showing:
- deep loyalty;
- one promise B keeps despite personal cost;
- moments where present feeling is wrong;
- affection for parts of the shared past.

---

# IV. The Family Other's Present-Tense Need

The family Other must not need:

"for A and B to fix their marriage."

That makes the third person a function.

The third person needs something **independent of the marriage's self-understanding**.

The strongest generic form is:

> **THE FAMILY OTHER NEEDS A CONCRETE CHANGE IN RELATIONSHIP, CARE, OR SUPPORT THAT REQUIRES A AND B TO ACT BEFORE THEY HAVE AGREED ON WHAT THEIR OWN CHANGE MEANS.**

The exact role remains open.

This can fit:

## Aging parent
Needs:
- care;
- transport;
- housing decision;
- medical coordination;
- financial support;
- increased independence or reduced independence.

## Adult / near-adult child
Needs:
- separation;
- support;
- privacy;
- housing;
- money;
- permission to fail;
- recognition as no longer a child.

The forms differ.

The deeper narrative function does not:

> **THE THIRD PERSON IMPOSES A REALITY CLOCK.**

Their need exists for reasons other than the couple's internal process.

---

# V. The Three Time Scales

The need field becomes especially generative because each position has a different relation to time.

## A — Historical Time

A asks:

> **WHAT DOES WHAT ALREADY HAPPENED REQUIRE OF US NOW?**

A's evidence comes from:
- accumulation;
- sequence;
- prior commitments;
- consequences already borne.

## B — Emergent Time

B asks:

> **WHAT IS HAPPENING NOW THAT THE OLD ACCOUNT CANNOT YET EXPLAIN?**

B's evidence comes from:
- changed salience;
- present sensation;
- exception;
- new desire;
- current conditions.

## Family Other — Consequential Time

The third person asks, implicitly or explicitly:

> **WHAT ARE YOU GOING TO DO?**

Their need creates:
- deadline;
- practical action;
- dependency;
- consequence.

This may be the strongest structural result of the need field:

**PAST CLAIM
⇄
PRESENT EMERGENCE
⇄
EXTERNAL CONSEQUENCE**

---

# VI. Why This Creates Motion Without Plot

The field now moves because no one can simply wait for perfect understanding.

A wants:
the past to retain standing.

B wants:
the present to acquire standing.

The family Other needs:
action.

Therefore:

> **THEY MUST ACT WHILE THE MEANING OF THE ACTION IS STILL CONTESTED.**

That is narrative pressure.

It does not yet tell us:
- what they do;
- who wins;
- what breaks;
- whether anyone leaves.

It simply creates the condition where interpretation and consequence can no longer be separated.

---

# VII. Need Is Not Position

Each person should contain internal contradiction.

## A may also need change

A may privately need:
- relief;
- adventure;
- sexual renewal;
- a different future;
- release from always being the carrier.

This prevents A from becoming continuity incarnate.

## B may also need continuity

B may privately need:
- the marriage to remain;
- a ritual preserved;
- financial safety;
- one promise kept;
- A's memory of something B cannot hold alone.

This prevents B from becoming change incarnate.

## Family Other may need both

The third person may need:
- help and independence;
- memory and release;
- continuity and transition.

No role should be pure.

---

# VIII. Present-Tense Need Candidate A — Recognition of Cost

One especially strong A-need is:

> **ACKNOWLEDGE THAT YOUR CHANGE COSTS ME SOMETHING BEFORE YOU ASK ME TO BLESS IT.**

Why it works:
- not anti-change;
- not controlling by definition;
- rooted in consequence;
- creates emotional and material stakes.

Risk:
can sound morally superior.

Use only through scenes, never thesis.

---

# IX. Present-Tense Need Candidate B — Provisional Permission

One especially strong B-need is:

> **LET ME TRY TO LIVE DIFFERENTLY BEFORE I CAN PROVE WHAT THIS CHANGE MEANS.**

Why it works:
- emergence is often inarticulate;
- creates risk without requiring total rupture;
- fits altered salience.

Risk:
can excuse irresponsibility.

The work must let consequences test it.

---

# X. Present-Tense Need Candidate Family Other — Practical Reconfiguration

The strongest generic third need is:

> **I NEED OUR RELATIONSHIP TO CHANGE IN PRACTICE, NOT JUST IN DESCRIPTION.**

For an aging parent:
perhaps more help, less help, or a different kind.

For an adult child:
perhaps more independence, less parental authority, or material support under new terms.

Why it works:

The third person may be undergoing their own revision.

This prevents the couple from monopolizing change.

---

# XI. Deep Triad

The three strongest needs can currently be compressed as:

### A
> **DO NOT MAKE OUR PAST MEANINGLESS.**

### B
> **DO NOT MAKE MY PRESENT WAIT FOR PERFECT EXPLANATION.**

### FAMILY OTHER
> **DO NOT MAKE YOUR SELF-UNDERSTANDING MORE IMPORTANT THAN WHAT I ACTUALLY NEED FROM YOU.**

These are authorial compressions.

They are **not dialogue**.

---

# XII. Mutual Misrecognition

Each person will initially misread the others' needs.

## A may hear B as:
"Our history no longer matters."

But B may mean:
"History cannot have total jurisdiction."

## B may hear A as:
"You are not allowed to change."

But A may mean:
"Change does not erase the costs already distributed."

## A and B may hear the family Other as:
"One more burden / complication."

But the third person may be undergoing a transformation equally real.

## Family Other may hear A and B as:
"Self-absorbed."

But the marriage may actually be carrying structural strain the third person depends on them to survive.

This creates reciprocal incompleteness.

---

# XIII. Need and Love

The strongest field preserves love as an active complication.

A may still want B's future.

B may still want A beside them in that future.

The problem is not absence of love.

It is:

> **THEY NO LONGER AGREE ON WHAT LOVE IS ENTITLED TO PRESERVE OR CHANGE.**

That is richer than:
love vs freedom.

---

# XIV. Need and Recognition

A wants recognition of:
**cost already borne.**

B wants recognition of:
**life currently emerging.**

The family Other wants recognition of:
**their independent need.**

Therefore the novel's recognition problem is now plural.

No one simply wants to be understood.

Each wants a different reality to be granted standing.

Working law:

> **CONFLICT BEGINS WHEN DIFFERENT REALITIES REQUIRE STANDING AT THE SAME TIME.**

---

# XV. Need and the Contemporary Milieu

The model-era pressure can later enter precisely because each need can be made legible differently.

A may be helped by systems that:
- reconstruct history;
- compare patterns;
- preserve records;
- articulate accumulated cost.

B may be helped by systems that:
- generate new language;
- reframe;
- simulate options;
- validate emerging patterns.

The family Other may use the same systems for:
- practical coordination;
- independent self-understanding;
- their own account of family history.

Important:

> **NO ONE SHOULD OWN THE MODEL.**

The medium belongs to the milieu, not to a side.

It can help each person become more articulate and therefore potentially **more difficult to reconcile**.

That is historically interesting.

---

# XVI. New Discovery — Legibility Can Increase Conflict

The earlier project identified the Legibility Advantage.

The need field reveals a complication:

Better self-description does not necessarily produce agreement.

It may produce:
- sharper claims;
- clearer boundaries;
- more coherent self-accounts;
- stronger incompatible needs.

Therefore:

> **INCREASED LEGIBILITY CAN MAKE RELATIONAL DIFFERENCE MORE, NOT LESS, CONSEQUENT.**

A better model of each person may make compromise harder if each becomes more certain that their own account is complete.

This is a significant contemporary pressure.

---

# XVII. New Discovery — The Story May Begin Before Anyone Is Wrong

This is perhaps the strongest literary result.

No initiating betrayal is required.

No secret is required.

No villain is required.

The field can begin because:

- A's need is legitimate;
- B's need is legitimate;
- the family Other's need is legitimate;
- the three needs cannot all be satisfied under the existing arrangement.

Therefore:

> **NARRATIVE CAN BEGIN WHEN LEGITIMATE NEEDS OUTGROW THE FORM THAT USED TO HOLD THEM.**

This may be the bridge from architecture into story without engineering a plot.

---

# XVIII. Anti-Plot Rules

Do not yet decide:
- what exact change B wants;
- what exact cost A has borne;
- whether the family Other is parent or child;
- what deadline exists;
- who takes action first;
- whether the marriage is threatened explicitly.

Do not manufacture:
- affair;
- terminal diagnosis;
- hidden debt;
- dramatic revelation;
- sudden catastrophe.

The field already moves.
