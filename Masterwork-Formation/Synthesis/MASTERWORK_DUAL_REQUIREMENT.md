# Masterwork Dual Requirement

## Status

Constitutional correction after the Literary Mastery Audit.

The Masterwork Formation project previously developed a strong constitutional architecture for a future work.

That architecture remains valid.

It is now explicitly incomplete.

---

# I. The Missing Dimension

The earlier research could answer:

- Is the work answerable?
- Is authorial jurisdiction bounded?
- Can independent standing change the account?
- Does the work preserve remainder?
- Can it survive succession?
- Does the form perform the problem?

It could not yet answer:

> **IS THIS MAGNIFICENT TO READ?**

That question cannot be treated as decoration.

---

# II. Dual Requirement

A candidate future masterwork now requires two independent forms of strength.

## A. FORMAL / CONSTITUTIONAL LIFE

The work must remain:
- answerable;
- correctable;
- bounded;
- open to contact;
- developmental;
- depositable;
- refusable.

## B. LITERARY LIFE

The work must possess:
- voice;
- rhythm;
- scene;
- character;
- desire;
- sensory particularity;
- emotional modulation;
- surprise;
- memorability;
- reader hunger.

Neither category can be inferred from the other.

---

# III. Working Equation

Do not use this as a literal score.

Use it as a protection:

**CANDIDATE MASTERWORK
=
LITERARY LIFE
x
FORMAL NECESSITY
x
ANSWERABILITY
x
SUCCESSION CAPACITY**

The multiplication matters conceptually.

If any dimension collapses toward zero, the others cannot simply compensate.

---

# IV. Central Laws

> **THE FUTURE MASTERWORK MUST BE BOTH ALIVE AND ANSWERABLE.**

> **THE ARCHITECTURE MAY GOVERN THE WORK, BUT IT CANNOT BE THE ONLY REASON THE READER TURNS THE PAGE.**

> **THE FORMAL OPERATION MUST BECOME LITERARY LIFE.**

> **A CHARACTER MUST BE ALLOWED TO EMBARRASS THE THEORY.**

> **A WORLD MUST CONTAIN LIFE THE THESIS DID NOT NEED.**

> **THE READER MUST WANT SOMETHING BEFORE THE FORM CAN CONSEQUENTIALLY WITHHOLD, ALTER, OR RETURN IT.**

---

# V. New Deposit Test Addition

Before release, add:

## Literary survival test

If the reader:
- rejects The Outside;
- never learns the Meaning Field;
- never sees the architecture;
- cannot diagram the formal operation;

does the work still contain:
- a voice worth hearing;
- people worth following;
- scenes worth remembering;
- language worth returning to;
- images that survive;
- desire strong enough to carry the reader?

If no:

the architecture has not yet become literature.
