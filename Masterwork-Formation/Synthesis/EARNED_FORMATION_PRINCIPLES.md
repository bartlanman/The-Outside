# Earned Formation Principles

These principles are working principles, not universal laws.

The file began with the first three pilot dossiers — Dante, Proust, Borges — and has now been extended by the completed authorship-spine audit across Homeric tradition, Biblical corpus, Shakespeare, Whitman, Eliot, Calvino, Hesse, Tolkien, and Le Guin.

## 1. Form can do epistemic work

A masterwork's form may do more than carry an idea.

It can place the receiver in an operation where knowledge becomes possible only through participation.

Working principle:

THE FORM SHOULD NOT MERELY CONTAIN THE QUESTION.

THE FORM CAN MAKE THE QUESTION HAPPEN TO THE RECEIVER.

---

## 2. The receiver can be part of the machinery

Receiver activity can be constitutive of the work's operation.

A receiver may have to move, wait, remember, forget, infer, compare, revise, doubt, recognize, perform, or re-enter.

Working principle:

ASK NOT ONLY WHAT THE WORK MEANS.

ASK WHAT THE WORK REQUIRES THE RECEIVER TO DO.

---

## 3. Originality is a transformation of inheritance

Major works are not intelligible as creation without inheritance.

Their originality may appear in selection, recombination, transformation, refusal, recontextualization, and new relations among received forms.

Working principle:

DO NOT STUDY GENIUS AS ABSENCE OF SOURCES.

STUDY WHAT THE WORK DOES TO WHAT IT RECEIVES.

---

## 4. Historical pressure may become formal pressure

A work need not reproduce its historical moment literally.

Historical pressure may alter architecture, genre, scale, epistemic stance, receiver relation, revision, and conditions of authority.

This must be demonstrated separately in each dossier.

---

## 5. Surface style is downstream of operation

Visible features become important because they perform work.

Working principle:

TO CREATE A CONTEMPORARY EQUIVALENT, PRESERVE THE OPERATION RATHER THAN THE COSTUME.

---

## 6. There is no single masterwork formation process

The dossiers resist a universal compositional recipe.

Working principle:

THE RESEARCH INSTRUMENT MUST DETECT FORMATION MODE.

IT MUST NOT PRESCRIBE ONE.

---

# Authorship-Spine Promotions

The following principles were promoted after the full authorship-spine synthesis audit.

## 7. Distributed authorship requires more precise responsibility

Support:
Homeric tradition, Biblical corpus, Shakespeare, Eliot, Tolkien, Le Guin.

Working principle:

DISTRIBUTED AUTHORSHIP
DOES NOT MEAN
OWNERLESS AUTHORSHIP.

When agency is distributed, responsibility must be located more precisely, not dissolved.

---

## 8. Relation itself can be authored

Support:
Biblical corpus, Eliot, Proust, Le Guin.

Selection, adjacency, sequence, boundary, recontextualization, and later positioning can change what a work becomes.

Working principle:

TO PLACE THINGS IN CONSEQUENTIAL RELATION
CAN ITSELF BE AN AUTHORIAL ACT.

---

## 9. Formal control does not entitle total meaning control

Support:
Dante, Shakespeare, Calvino, Tolkien.

A work can be tightly shaped while leaving judgment, participation, remainder, and future interpretation outside total authorial sovereignty.

Working principle:

FORMAL CONTROL
DOES NOT ENTITLE
TOTAL MEANING CONTROL.

---

## 10. Authorship and realization may be different offices

Support:
Homeric performance, Shakespearean theater, Biblical communal realization, Calvino's receiver operation.

Working principle:

AUTHORSHIP LOCUS
!=
REALIZATION LOCUS.

A performer, reader, community, or participant can be essential to the experienced work without becoming identical to the textual author.

---

## 11. Real contact must be able to change the account

Support:
Whitman, Proust, Eliot, Hesse, Le Guin.

If the author can absorb every encounter without changing form, warrant, boundary, interpretation, or right to speak, the encounter has not demonstrated independent standing.

Working principle:

REAL CONTACT
MUST BE ABLE
TO CHANGE THE ACCOUNT.

---

## 12. Participation requires standing outside the designer's model

Support:
Calvino, Shakespeare, Tolkien, Hesse.

Working principle:

A PARTICIPANT MUST RETAIN
SOME STANDING
THE DESIGNER DID NOT AUTHOR.

Responsiveness alone does not establish agency.

---

## 13. Constitutive participation is not automatically coauthorship

Support:
Calvino, Shakespeare, Homeric performance, Dante and Proust receiver operations.

Working principle:

CONSTITUTIVE PARTICIPATION
!=
COAUTHORSHIP.

A receiver may complete an operation without owning the fixed textual material.

---

## 14. Internal coherence is not sufficient warrant

Support:
Borges, Hesse, Dante's totalization risk, Tolkien's possession risk.

Working principle:

COHERENCE
!=
CONTACT
!=
SUFFICIENCY.

A system can be coherent and valuable while remaining self-certifying or hermetic.

---

## 15. Large-scale relation must retain a route to particular responsibility

Support:
Hesse, Tolkien, Le Guin.

Working principle:

THE TEST OF LARGE-SCALE RELATION
MAY ARRIVE
AS RESPONSIBILITY TO THE PARTICULAR.

The Game returns to Tito.
The quest returns to the Shire.
The Archmage-scale world returns to household, care, and vulnerable people.

---

## 16. Authored constraints can later constrain the author

Support:
Tolkien, Le Guin, Proust, Calvino.

Working principle:

A CONDITION MAY BEGIN
AS AN AUTHORIAL CHOICE
AND LATER BECOME
A LIMIT ON AUTHORIAL CHOICE.

Prior publication, maps, languages, chronology, character history, and formal rules can create structured resistance to arbitrary revision.

---

## 17. Revision can be relational rather than erasive

Support:
Le Guin, Proust, Whitman, Biblical corpus formation.

Working principle:

REVISION MAY CHANGE
THE STANDING OF AN EARLIER STATE
WITHOUT DESTROYING
THE EARLIER STATE AS EVIDENCE.

This preserves developmental truth.

---

## 18. Deposit is often iterative

Support:
Homer, Biblical corpus, Shakespeare, Whitman, Eliot, Tolkien, Le Guin.

Working principle:

DEPOSIT
IS OFTEN
A RECURRING TRANSFER OF CUSTODY.

Performance, edition, translation, collection, archive, recovered draft, and later re-reading can all become new deposits.

The author's preferred final state does not control succession.
