# Earned Formation Principles

These principles have survived the first three pilot dossiers — Dante, Proust, Borges — across significantly different periods, archives, formal methods, and authorial situations.

They are working principles, not universal laws.

## 1. Form can do epistemic work

A masterwork's form may do more than carry an idea.

It can place the reader in an operation where knowledge becomes possible only through participation.

Dante:
position -> traverse -> changed perception.

Proust:
duration -> misrecognition -> return -> recognition.

Borges:
formal authority -> trust -> instability -> warrant test.

Working principle:

THE FORM SHOULD NOT MERELY CONTAIN THE QUESTION.

THE FORM CAN MAKE THE QUESTION HAPPEN TO THE READER.

---

## 2. The reader can be part of the machinery

Reader response is not always something that happens after the work is complete.

In these pilots, the reader's activity is constitutive of the work's operation.

The reader must:
- move,
- wait,
- forget,
- infer,
- revise,
- doubt,
- recognize.

Working principle:

ASK NOT ONLY WHAT THE WORK MEANS.

ASK WHAT THE WORK REQUIRES THE READER TO DO.

---

## 3. Originality is a transformation of inheritance

None of the three pilot works is intelligible as creation without inheritance.

Their originality appears in:
- selection,
- recombination,
- transformation,
- refusal,
- recontextualization,
- new relations among received forms.

Working principle:

DO NOT STUDY GENIUS AS ABSENCE OF SOURCES.

STUDY WHAT THE WORK DOES TO WHAT IT RECEIVES.

---

## 4. Historical pressure may become formal pressure

A work need not reproduce its historical moment literally.

Historical pressure may alter:
- architecture,
- genre,
- scale,
- epistemic stance,
- reader relation,
- revision,
- conditions of authority.

This principle must be demonstrated separately in each dossier.

It is a research instruction, not permission to invent causal connections.

---

## 5. Surface style is downstream of operation

Visible features become historically important because they perform work.

Examples:

Dante's traversal:
not medieval scenery, but positional cognition.

Proust's syntactic delay:
not long sentences, but resistance to premature closure.

Borges's scholarly surface:
not fake footnotes, but a test of how authority is produced.

Working principle:

TO CREATE A CONTEMPORARY EQUIVALENT, PRESERVE THE OPERATION RATHER THAN THE COSTUME.

---

## 6. There is no single masterwork formation process

The pilots already resist a universal compositional recipe.

Dante:
architectural passage.

Proust:
recursive growth and return.

Borges:
proliferation, selection, compression, and destabilization.

Working principle:

THE RESEARCH INSTRUMENT MUST DETECT FORMATION MODE.

IT MUST NOT PRESCRIBE ONE.
