## Current threshold compression

> **CULTURE CAN NOW CONVERSE WITH A MODEL OF CULTURE.**

The unresolved distinction is:

> **IS THE MODEL A MIRROR, A MEDIUM, AN AGENT, AN OTHER, OR SOME NEW RELATION AMONG THESE?**

The Outside research gives two constitutional protections:

> **RESPONSE != OTHER**

and:

> **REPRESENTATION != INDEPENDENT STANDING**

The future masterwork may need to make these distinctions culturally perceptible before ordinary language has stabilized around them.
