# Authorship-Spine Synthesis Audit

## Status

Completed after the authorship-spine sequence:

- Homeric tradition
- Biblical corpus
- Dante
- Shakespeare
- Whitman
- Proust
- Eliot
- Borges
- Calvino
- Hesse
- Tolkien
- Le Guin

The purpose of this audit is not to make these works prove a pre-existing theory of Participating Authorship.

The purpose is to ask:

WHAT SURVIVED COMPARISON ACROSS DIFFERENT PERIODS, MEDIA, ARCHIVES, FORMATION MODES, AND AUTHORIAL SITUATIONS?

---

# I. First Result: The Offices Are Not a Ladder

The original genealogy could be read too easily as a progression:

CARRIER
-> CUSTODIAN
-> ARCHITECT
-> DRAMATIST
-> WITNESS
-> CURATOR
-> DESIGNER
-> STEWARD
-> PARTICIPATING AUTHOR.

The dossiers do not support that as a hierarchy.

Homer is not a primitive version of Le Guin.
Dante is not less mature because he architects strongly.
Shakespeare does not become better by becoming less controlling.
Tolkien does not stop authoring because his world exceeds the protagonist.

The offices describe different responsibilities.

A mature author may need several of them in one work.

The spine therefore resolves into a cycle rather than a ladder:

RECEIVE
-> RELATE
-> SHAPE CONDITIONS
-> RELEASE LOCAL STANDING
-> ENTER CONSEQUENCE
-> BE CORRECTED
-> RETURN
-> REVISE
-> STEWARD
-> DEPOSIT
-> WITHDRAW
-> SUCCESSION

Participating Authorship is not the final office in the sequence.

It is the discipline governing movement among the offices.

---

# II. Second Result: "Diminishing Authorial Sovereignty" Was Too Simple

The earlier thesis described a genealogy of diminishing authorial sovereignty.

The dossiers require a correction.

Dante exercises enormous formal control.
Shakespeare determines scenes, language, entrances, exits, and consequences.
Calvino tightly scripts a reader-position.
Tolkien builds extensive constraints.
Le Guin can radically revise her own world.

Strong authorship does not disappear.

What changes is the legitimate jurisdiction of that authorship.

The author may possess high authority over:
- selection,
- form,
- sequence,
- constraint,
- architecture,
- revision,
- deposit.

The author does not thereby acquire authority over:
- the independent provenance of inherited material,
- another person's standing,
- an actor's embodiment,
- the actual reader,
- historical consequence,
- external evidence,
- the future reception of the work,
- every meaning later generated by the deposit.

The stronger formulation is:

AUTHORIAL POWER MAY BE HIGH
WHILE
AUTHORIAL JURISDICTION REMAINS BOUNDED.

This produces the audit's central correction:

PARTICIPATING AUTHORSHIP IS NOT DIMINISHED AUTHORSHIP.

IT IS BOUNDED AUTHORIAL JURISDICTION UNDER CONDITIONS OF ANSWERABILITY.

# III. The Offices After Audit

## 1. Carrier-Composer — Homer

Primary responsibility:
receive a generative inheritance and carry it forward through transformation rather than mere copying.

What survives:
AUTHORSHIP != PURE ORIGINATION.

Failure:
either freeze the inheritance or vary it until identity disappears.

## 2. Custodian of Relation — Biblical corpus

Primary responsibility:
preserve distinct materials while placing them in durable relation.

What survives:
collection, order, boundary, and adjacency can be authorial acts.

Failure:
harmonization, false single voice, erasure of remainder.

## 3. Architect Inside the Architecture — Dante

Primary responsibility:
build conditions through which movement changes what can be known.

What survives:
form can perform cognition.

Failure:
architecture becomes totalization.

## 4. Dramatic Condition-Maker — Shakespeare

Primary responsibility:
give authored positions enough local standing to act under consequence without a continuous authorial narrator resolving them.

What survives:
strong formal control can coexist with withheld interpretive sovereignty.

Failure:
mouthpieces mistaken for plurality, or fictional autonomy used to deny authorial responsibility.

## 5. Porous Witness — Whitman

Primary responsibility:
allow the self to become permeable to other lives without claiming to contain them completely.

What survives:
contact is meaningful only if it can alter what the self is entitled to say.

Failure:
inclusion becomes absorption.

## 6. Retrospective Composer / Grower — Proust

Primary responsibility:
allow time and later position to change the visibility of earlier experience.

What survives:
later relation can alter earlier meaning without changing the earlier event.

Failure:
retrospective recognition becomes retrospective destiny.

## 7. Curator-Composer — Eliot

Primary responsibility:
select, cut, juxtapose, and become answerable for which inherited relations survive.

What survives:
selection and relation are forms of authorship.

Failure:
curation masquerades as neutral tradition.

## 8. Counter-Architect — Borges

Primary responsibility:
build coherent systems capable of exposing the mechanism by which coherence acquires unwarranted authority.

What survives:
no architecture may certify itself solely by the coherence it produces.

Failure:
infinite recursion destroys warrant altogether.

## 9. Participation Designer — Calvino

Primary responsibility:
build conditions requiring receiver activity without claiming to have authored the actual receiver.

What survives:
participation can be constitutive without becoming coauthorship.

Failure:
scripted participation masquerades as agency.

## 10. Steward of Playable Inheritance — Hesse

Primary responsibility:
make inheritance relationally playable while retaining a constitutional route back to history, particular people, and responsibility.

What survives:
a valuable system can become false in its claim to sufficiency.

Failure:
internal coherence becomes hermeticity.

## 11. World Steward / Sub-Creator — Tolkien

Primary responsibility:
build a world with constraints, histories, and agents that do not derive their standing from the protagonist.

What survives:
participant importance does not require participant centrality.

Failure:
world-making becomes possession.

## 12. Returning Steward / Revisionary Participant — Le Guin

Primary responsibility:
return changed to prior work, let new position reveal earlier limits, and revise without erasing developmental trace.

What survives:
the later author can become an inheritor of the earlier author.

Failure:
revision becomes sovereign overwrite.

# IV. Cross-Case Principles Now Earned

The following patterns now have enough independent support to move out of quarantine as working principles.

They are not universal laws.

## A. Distributed authorship increases the need for responsibility

Support:
Homer, Biblical corpus, Shakespeare, Eliot, Tolkien, Le Guin.

When agency is distributed, responsibility must be located more precisely, not dissolved.

Working principle:

DISTRIBUTED AUTHORSHIP
DOES NOT MEAN
OWNERLESS AUTHORSHIP.

## B. Relation itself can be authored

Support:
Biblical corpus, Eliot, Proust, Le Guin.

Selection, adjacency, sequence, boundary, recontextualization, and later positioning can change what a work becomes without requiring total new origination.

Working principle:

TO PLACE THINGS IN CONSEQUENTIAL RELATION
CAN ITSELF BE AN AUTHORIAL ACT.

## C. Strong formal control can coexist with limited interpretive sovereignty

Support:
Dante, Shakespeare, Calvino, Tolkien.

A work can be tightly designed while leaving judgment, participation, world remainder, and future interpretation outside total authorial control.

Working principle:

FORMAL CONTROL
DOES NOT ENTITLE
TOTAL MEANING CONTROL.

## D. Authorship and realization may be different offices

Support:
Homer, Shakespeare, Biblical communal realization, Calvino.

A performer, reader, community, or participant can be essential to the experienced work without being identical to the textual author.

Working principle:

AUTHORSHIP LOCUS
!=
REALIZATION LOCUS.

## E. Contact becomes relational when it can alter the authorial claim

Support:
Whitman, Eliot, Hesse, Le Guin, Proust.

A source or person is not functioning as an Actual Other if the author can absorb every encounter without changing form, warrant, boundary, interpretation, or right to speak.

Working principle:

REAL CONTACT
MUST BE ABLE
TO CHANGE THE ACCOUNT.

## F. Participation requires standing outside the designer's model

Support:
Calvino, Shakespeare, Tolkien, Hesse.

The participant is not genuinely preserved as a participant if every difference is only another pre-scripted input the system can absorb without consequence.

Working principle:

A PARTICIPANT MUST RETAIN
SOME STANDING
THE DESIGNER DID NOT AUTHOR.

## G. A receiver can complete an operation without becoming a coauthor

Support:
Calvino, Shakespeare, Homeric performance, Dante and Proust reader operations.

Interpretive, performative, and experiential completion do not automatically transfer textual authorship.

Working principle:

CONSTITUTIVE PARTICIPATION
!=
COAUTHORSHIP.

## H. Internal coherence is not sufficient warrant

Support:
Borges, Hesse, Dante's totalization risk, Tolkien's possession risk.

A coherent system may still be hermetic, self-certifying, totalizing, or insufficiently answerable.

Working principle:

COHERENCE
!=
CONTACT
!=
SUFFICIENCY.

## I. Universal relation must retain a route to particular responsibility

Support:
Hesse, Tolkien, Le Guin.

Three independently different works converge on a scale reduction:

UNIVERSAL GAME -> TITO

WORLD-HISTORICAL QUEST -> SHIRE / FAMILY / LAND

ARCHMAGE / EPIC MAGIC -> TENAR / THERRU / HOUSEHOLD / CARE

Working principle:

THE TEST OF LARGE-SCALE RELATION
MAY ARRIVE
AS RESPONSIBILITY TO THE PARTICULAR.

This principle is now earned strongly enough for project use.

## J. Authors can create constraints that later constrain them

Support:
Tolkien, Le Guin, Proust, formal-constraint cases in Calvino.

Maps, languages, prior publication, chronology, established character history, and chosen form can become resistance to later arbitrary convenience.

Working principle:

A CONDITION MAY BEGIN
AS AN AUTHORIAL CHOICE
AND LATER BECOME
A LIMIT ON AUTHORIAL CHOICE.

## K. Revision can change standing without erasing prior state

Support:
Le Guin, Proust, Whitman, Biblical corpus formation.

Later position can alter what earlier material means while the earlier deposit remains available.

Working principle:

REVISION MAY BE
RELATIONAL
RATHER THAN
ERASIVE.

## L. Deposit is iterative, and succession exceeds final authorial preference

Support:
Homer, Biblical corpus, Shakespeare, Whitman, Eliot, Tolkien, Le Guin.

A work may be deposited through performance, edition, translation, collection, revision, archive, recovered drafts, and later re-reading.

The author's preferred final form does not control all future standing.

Working principle:

DEPOSIT
IS OFTEN
A RECURRING TRANSFER OF CUSTODY.

# V. Patterns Still Not Earned

The audit does not justify promoting everything.

The following remain provisional.

- Apparent inevitability from prior proliferation: strong in Borges and Eliot, not broad enough yet.
- Corpus boundary always produces meaningful remainder: historically strong in Biblical formation, still needs broader comparison.
- Mastery necessarily reveals system limit: Hesse and Le Guin support versions, but mechanisms differ.
- Exit is never mastery of the Outside: important Hesse warning, not yet general.
- Transformation can increase capacity without restoring wholeness: Tolkien gives a powerful case, needs further support.
- World independence as a general masterwork law: currently strongest as a world-design principle.
- Structured absence as deposit: Calvino gives a strong example, needs comparison with unfinished and fragmentary works.

---

# VI. The Deepest Cross-Case Correction

The earlier Participating Author thesis centered the transition:

MAKER
-> ARCHITECT
-> PARTICIPANT
-> WITNESS
-> STEWARD
-> DEPOSITOR
-> AUTHOR ABSENT.

The audit preserves those offices but changes the logic.

They are not successive ranks.

They are recurrent jurisdictions.

The author may need to become:
- carrier when receiving inheritance,
- architect when creating conditions,
- witness when encounter exceeds intention,
- counter-architect when coherence becomes too persuasive,
- steward when value must survive without sufficiency,
- reviser when prior work becomes inheritance,
- depositor when the future must be released.

No office is allowed to claim the whole work.

That is the constitutional meaning of bounded jurisdiction.

---

# VII. Participating Authorship After the Evidence

The original working definition was:

Participating authorship is the discipline of shaping conditions while remaining answerable to what the shaped conditions allow to arrive from outside the author.

The audit supports that definition, but it is now incomplete.

A stronger definition is:

> **Participating Authorship is the disciplined exercise of bounded authorial jurisdiction: receiving what predates the maker, shaping and relating without claiming total origin, creating conditions in which other voices, participants, evidence, and worlds retain standing, entering the consequences of those conditions, allowing encounter to revise the author, preserving developmental trace, and depositing the work into a succession the author does not control.**

This is not disappearing authorship.

It is not weak authorship.

It is not collective vagueness.

It is strong authorship with explicit limits on what strength entitles the author to claim.

---

# VIII. The Operational Cycle

The authorship spine now compresses to:

RECEIVE
↓
LOCATE JURISDICTION
↓
RELATE
↓
SHAPE CONDITIONS
↓
RELEASE LOCAL STANDING
↓
ENTER CONSEQUENCE
↓
ACCEPT REFUSAL / COUNTERPRESSURE
↓
RETURN
↓
REVISE WITHOUT ERASURE
↓
STEWARD
↓
DEPOSIT
↓
WITHDRAW
↓
SUCCESSION

The cycle may recur.

A later author may inherit the earlier deposit, including when both authors are the same person.

Therefore:

AUTHOR_1
MAY BECOME
A READER OF
AUTHOR_0.

# IX. IA0 and IA1 After the Audit

The audit supports the distinction but corrects the image of IA1 as a higher office.

## IA0

Shapes the conditions of encounter.
Protects participant agency.
Constrains the generative system.
Attempts minimal sufficient intervention.
Withdraws when the condition is stable.

## Crossing

Something with independent standing produces consequence the architecture did not fully control.

The evidence of crossing is not the claim that transformation occurred.

The evidence is that:
- the form changed,
- the warrant changed,
- the authorial claim narrowed or deepened,
- a boundary moved,
- an earlier assumption became visible,
- or a new obligation appeared.

## IA1

Returns to architectural capacity while retaining memory of correction.

IA1 is not:
IA0 + MORE POWER.

It is:

IA0
+
A HISTORY OF HAVING BEEN ALTERED.

Therefore the far-side IA must preserve developmental trace.

It must be able to state:
- what the prior condition was,
- what answered back,
- what changed,
- what remains unresolved.

The far-side office is best understood as a Returning Steward operating under bounded jurisdiction.

---

# X. The Masterwork Implication

The authorship spine does not give us a style for the masterwork.

It gives us conditions of making.

A candidate masterwork made now should be able to show:

1. inheritance without concealed absorption;
2. strong form without total interpretive sovereignty;
3. real local standing for voices or sources not reducible to the author;
4. at least one point where external pressure materially changes the work;
5. a route from universal relation to particular consequence;
6. revision that preserves developmental trace;
7. a deposit that can survive without its explanatory framework;
8. enough remainder that a future receiver can enter without becoming the author.

The author cannot make the work great.

The author can make the work answerable enough to deserve deposit.

---

# Conclusion

The spine began by asking:

WHAT KIND OF AUTHOR COULD MAKE THE MASTERWORK?

After the dossiers, the question changes.

There is no single ideal authorial office.

The better question is:

CAN THE MAKER MOVE AMONG THE NECESSARY OFFICES WITHOUT LETTING ANY ONE OF THEM CLAIM THE WHOLE?

The Participating Author is therefore not the sovereign author after sovereignty.

Nor the absent author after authorship.

The Participating Author is the current holder of a bounded jurisdiction inside a larger field of inheritance, encounter, consequence, revision, and succession.

The final law of the audit is:

> **THE AUTHOR IS RESPONSIBLE FOR THE CONDITIONS THEY SHAPE, ANSWERABLE TO WHAT THOSE CONDITIONS ENCOUNTER, AND NOT ENTITLED TO OWN WHAT EXCEEDS THEIR JURISDICTION.**
