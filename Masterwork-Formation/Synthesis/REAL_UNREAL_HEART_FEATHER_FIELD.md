# Real / Unreal — Heart / Feather Field

## Status

Working masterwork axis.

The terms **heart** and **feather** are project metaphors here.

They should not be confused with a literal reconstruction of ancient Egyptian psychostasia, in which the heart is weighed against the feather of Ma'at (truth / order / justice).

This project uses the image differently:

**HEART = REAL / WEIGHT / CONSEQUENCE**

**FEATHER = UNREAL / FLIGHT / POSSIBILITY**

---

# I. Heart — What Has Weight

The Real includes whatever can resist the work's preferred account.

Examples:
- body;
- nature;
- mortality;
- another person;
- weather;
- history;
- archive;
- economics;
- labor;
- sex;
- illness;
- hunger;
- matter;
- time;
- independently custodied evidence.

The Real is not "realism."

Fantasy can contain Real if consequence has standing.

Documentary can evade Real if it manipulates its evidence into closure.

The governing property is:

> **THE REAL CAN ANSWER BACK.**

---

# II. Feather — What Can Fly

The Unreal includes whatever the human / cultural imagination can produce beyond current actuality.

Examples:
- myth;
- dream;
- archetype;
- counterfactual;
- fantasy;
- game;
- simulation;
- impossible geometry;
- generated image;
- alternate history;
- symbolic compression;
- speculative science;
- invented language;
- fictional world.

The Unreal is not falsehood.

It is:

> **THE SPACE IN WHICH CULTURE CAN REHEARSE FORMS THAT REALITY HAS NOT YET MADE AVAILABLE.**

---

# III. Failure Modes

## Heart without Feather
- reportage;
- documentary closure;
- realism mistaken for truth;
- present conditions treated as total possibility.

## Feather without Heart
- self-sealing myth;
- simulation;
- aestheticized escape;
- generated coherence without consequence.

## False Balance
Alternating "real scenes" and "fantasy scenes" is not enough.

The stronger condition is:

> **THE REAL AND UNREAL MUST BE MUTUALLY CORRECTIVE.**

The Real gives the Unreal weight.

The Unreal gives the Real alternative form.

---

# IV. Mythos as the Bridge

Mythos is neither pure fact nor arbitrary fantasy.

It is a form that:
- receives real conditions;
- compresses them;
- moves them through symbol;
- returns them as an orientation.

Therefore Mythos can become the bridge:

**REAL
→ MYTHIC COMPRESSION
→ UNREAL POSSIBILITY
→ ENCOUNTER
→ REAL CONSEQUENCE
→ REVISED MYTH**

This is a living mythology rather than fixed doctrine.

---

# V. AI as Feather-Engine

Generative AI has extraordinary capacity on the Feather side:
- transformation;
- recombination;
- world generation;
- counterfactual;
- voice;
- image;
- branching possibility;
- symbolic relation.

Its danger is that its outputs can feel weighted when they have not encountered independent consequence.

Therefore:

> **AI IS POWERFUL AT FLIGHT AND WEAK AT GRAVITY UNLESS THE WORK BUILDS GRAVITY INTO THE ENCOUNTER.**

This is not a criticism of AI.

It is an artistic affordance.

The new form can deliberately use AI for:
- flight;
- variation;
- metamorphosis;
- participant-specific realization;

while preserving Real anchors that the system cannot simply rewrite.

---

# VI. The Masterwork Balance

The work should never settle permanently into either pole.

Working movement:

**WEIGHT
→ FLIGHT
→ CONTACT
→ ALTERED WEIGHT
→ NEW FLIGHT**

Or:

**HEART ⇄ FEATHER**

This may be the deeper form beneath Fixed Core / Live Seam.

The fixed core carries weight.

The live seam permits flight.

The Outside restores gravity.

The Return changes what both mean.

---

# VII. Current Question

> **WHAT MEDIUM CAN MAKE THE RECEIVER FEEL BOTH THE FREEDOM OF AN UNBOUNDED IMAGINATION AND THE WEIGHT OF SOMETHING THAT IMAGINATION CANNOT COMMAND?**

That is now a primary Masterwork Formation question.
