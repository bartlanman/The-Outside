# Masterwork Deposit Test

## Purpose

This test does not certify greatness.

It tests whether a work is mature enough to deserve deposit under the Masterwork Formation research.

Use after substantial formation, not as a checklist for generating the work.

---

## A. Making

### 1. Inheritance
Can the work's major inheritances be recovered?

- PASS: inheritance remains legible and transformed.
- FAIL: the work depends on concealed absorption or false originality.

### 2. Jurisdiction
Can the author state what belongs to authorial decision and what does not?

- PASS: strong authorship with explicit limits.
- FAIL: making is treated as ownership of source, participant, evidence, or future meaning.

### 3. Formal operation
Can the work state what its form makes possible that explanation alone could not?

- PASS: form performs the problem.
- FAIL: style or complexity is primarily decorative.

### 4. Consequential relation
Do relations among materials change their standing?

- PASS: adjacency, sequence, boundary, or interaction produces consequence.
- FAIL: accumulation without relational work.

### 5. Structured resistance
Does the work contain constraints that prevent arbitrary convenience?

- PASS: accepted constraints can force cost, refusal, or revision.
- FAIL: anything can change whenever the author needs it to.

---

## B. Encounter

### 6. Actual counterpressure
Did anything with independent standing materially change the work?

- PASS: the change can be named.
- FAIL: all "outside" pressure was staged or confirmatory.

### 7. Local standing
Can another voice, source, participant, or agent resist reduction to the central system?

- PASS: at least some local standing survives.
- FAIL: plurality is cosmetic.

### 8. Particular consequence
Where does the large claim become a real cost, body, person, place, obligation, or repair?

- PASS: the work becomes answerable to the particular.
- FAIL: meaning remains entirely abstract.

### 9. Developmental trace
Can important revisions be distinguished from claims that the work always knew what it now knows?

- PASS: correction remains visible.
- FAIL: revision silently rewrites the work's own past.

### 10. Remainder
What important tension remains unresolved because closure would exceed warrant?

- PASS: remainder is identifiable and honest.
- FAIL: forced synthesis or decorative ambiguity.

---

## C. Deposit

### 11. Explanatory independence
Would the work retain life if the author could never explain the framework again?

- PASS: the work can stand.
- FAIL: the framework is life support.

### 12. Refusability
Can a future receiver reject the author's framework and still encounter something alive?

- PASS: the deposit does not require conversion.
- FAIL: reception is pre-scripted.

### 13. Succession
Can the work tolerate a future use, interpretation, relation, or standing the author did not plan?

- PASS: succession is real.
- FAIL: only authorized reception counts.

---

# Release Question

If several conditions fail, continue formation.

If the work passes all thirteen, do **not** conclude that it is a masterwork.

Ask only:

> **HAS THIS WORK BECOME ANSWERABLE ENOUGH TO RELEASE?**

The author makes the deposit.

The future decides whether anything grows.
