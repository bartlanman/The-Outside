# MODEL

The index establishes that, on the same report date, National Fuel Gas:

1. completed an acquisition or disposition;
2. entered a material agreement;
3. incurred or created a material financial obligation.

What it does **not** establish is the causal relation among those events.

Two coherent interpretations remain open.

## Interpretation A — Transaction Financing

The new financial obligation and material agreement are primarily part of financing the acquisition that closed at the same time.

Compression:

ACQUISITION
-> FINANCING AGREEMENT
-> NEW DEBT
-> CLOSING.

Reasons this is plausible:
- Items 1.01, 2.01, and 2.03 occur in the same filing;
- acquisition closings commonly involve new financing;
- the filing date and report date are the same.

## Interpretation B — Parallel / Restructuring Financing

The acquisition closed, but the new material agreement / obligation is primarily a broader refinancing, credit restructuring, or balance-sheet action whose significance is not simply "debt used to buy the acquisition."

Compression:

ACQUISITION
+
SEPARATE / BROADER FINANCING EVENT.

Reasons this is plausible:
- 8-Ks can report several related but legally distinct events together;
- Item 2.03 does not itself say proceeds funded the acquisition;
- the new agreement may refinance prior facilities, replace bridge financing, or restructure existing debt.

---

# MODEL JUDGMENT BEFORE RECEIVER

Internal model weighting from index only:

A — Transaction financing: 65
B — Parallel / broader restructuring: 35

This weighting is preserved only as the model's pre-contact state.

The receiver will supply their own allocation separately.

---

# Receiver Commitment

Before the filing body is opened, the receiver must allocate **100 points** between:

A. The new debt/agreement was mainly financing the acquisition.

B. The acquisition and financing were related in timing but the financing was mainly a broader refinancing / restructuring event rather than simply acquisition funding.

The receiver should also identify the single clue they are relying on most.

---

# Handoff Rule

After receiver commitment:

1. open the SEC-filed 8-K body;
2. preserve the filing's own distinctions;
3. compare source against A and B;
4. allow:
   - A to win;
   - B to win;
   - both to become inadequate;
5. ask receiver to reallocate the same 100 points;
6. preserve before / after allocation and reason;
7. do not rewrite this file.

## Success condition

The run succeeds if contact restores a distinction that materially changes the receiver's warranted judgment.

It does not require surprise or model failure.
