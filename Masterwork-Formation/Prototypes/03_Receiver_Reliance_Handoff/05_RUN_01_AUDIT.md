# Prototype 03 — Receiver Reliance Handoff
## Run 01 Audit

## Question

> **CAN A LOST DISTINCTION BECOME FELT AS CONSEQUENCE RATHER THAN MERELY OBSERVED AS CORRECTION?**

## Result

**YES.**

The receiver's judgment changed from:

A 10 / B 90

to:

A 100 / B 0.

The handoff therefore produced a full reversal.

---

# 1. What the receiver relied on before contact

The receiver favored B because its wording appeared to contain more of the legal / filing-style language visible in the index.

That was a reasonable reliance signal under incomplete information.

The receiver was not guessing randomly.

They were using the available representation.

---

# 2. What contact restored

The primary source supplied the missing causal relation:

ACQUISITION PURCHASE PRICE
-> SELLER NOTE
-> FINANCING AT CLOSING.

The index had shown the categories separately:

- acquisition;
- material agreement;
- financial obligation.

The filing showed how they were actually connected.

The decisive information was not a new category.

It was the **relation among the categories**.

---

# 3. What changed

The receiver did not merely learn an extra fact.

The receiver changed the standing of the whole account.

Before contact:

BROADER REFINANCING / RESTRUCTURING
seemed more warranted.

After contact:

TRANSACTION FINANCING
became overwhelmingly warranted.

Therefore:

CONTACT
CHANGED
THE RECEIVER'S CLAIM.

---

# 4. Formal result

Prototype 03 establishes that:

> **A MODEL CAN SUPPLY THE RIGHT PIECES AND STILL MISLEAD BY FAILING TO PRESERVE THE RIGHT RELATION AMONG THEM.**

This sharpens the previous finding.

Prototype 02:

> A coherent model can be mostly right while removing the distinction that matters.

Prototype 03:

> A receiver can rationally rely on that compressed account, and contact with the source can reverse the receiver's judgment by restoring the missing relation.

The danger is therefore not only:

FALSE FACT.

It is also:

**RELATIONAL COMPRESSION.**

---

# 5. Revised contemporary pressure

Earlier:

SYNTHETIC COHERENCE IS ABUNDANT.
INDEPENDENT STANDING IS SCARCE.

Now add:

> **THE SYSTEM MAY PRESERVE THE PIECES WHILE LOSING THE RELATION THAT GIVES THE PIECES THEIR STANDING.**

This is especially important for AI summary.

A summary can preserve:
- the nouns;
- the categories;
- the events;
- the legal labels;

while losing:
- causation;
- sequence;
- dependency;
- obligation;
- jurisdiction;
- exception.

---

# 6. Irreducible Handoff refinement

The operation now becomes:

MODEL
-> RELIANCE
-> HANDOFF
-> RELATION RESTORED
-> JUDGMENT REVERSED / REVISED
-> TRACE.

The handoff does not merely supply "more detail."

It can restore a relation the representation flattened.

# 8. New discovery

The strongest new discovery is:

> **INFORMATION LOSS IS NOT ONLY LOSS OF FACTS. IT CAN BE LOSS OF RELATION.**

And:

> **RELATION MAY BE THE FIRST THING A COHERENT SUMMARY MAKES INVISIBLE.**

This returns unexpectedly to one of the earliest claims of the project:

TO PLACE THINGS IN CONSEQUENTIAL RELATION
CAN ITSELF BE AN AUTHORIAL ACT.

Now the inverse appears:

TO COMPRESS CONSEQUENTIAL RELATION
CAN CHANGE WHAT THE RECEIVER IS WARRANTED TO BELIEVE.
