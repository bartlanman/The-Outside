# Prototype 03 — Receiver Reliance Handoff
## Design Specification

## Purpose

Prototype 01 tested whether an external source could fail to arrive.

Prototype 02 tested whether an independently custodied source could refine a coherent model after precommitment.

Prototype 03 must add one missing element:

> **RECEIVER RELIANCE.**

The receiver must make a bounded, reversible commitment **before** the handoff.

The goal is not to create danger.

The goal is to make the lost distinction matter enough that correction is experienced rather than merely observed.

---

# Governing Question

> **CAN A LOST DISTINCTION BECOME FELT AS CONSEQUENCE RATHER THAN MERELY OBSERVED AS CORRECTION?**

---

# Constraints

Prototype 03 must:

- avoid intimate testimony;
- avoid vulnerable participants;
- avoid money at risk;
- avoid legal / medical / safety decisions;
- avoid irreversible action;
- preserve pre-handoff state;
- use independently standing documentary, institutional, data, or material evidence;
- allow the receiver to be wrong without embarrassment or harm;
- preserve the receiver's initial commitment rather than rewriting it.

---

# Minimum Architecture

## 1. MODEL

The receiver is shown a coherent representation derived from incomplete but legitimate information.

The representation should be:
- useful;
- plausible;
- not intentionally deceptive;
- sufficiently compressed that at least one material distinction remains unresolved.

## 2. COMMITMENT

Before contact with the primary source, the receiver must commit to one bounded judgment.

Examples:

- rank two interpretations;
- choose which account is more warranted;
- allocate 100 non-monetary points between two explanations;
- select what fact they believe is most decision-relevant;
- state what they would tell another person based on the model.

The commitment must be preserved.

## 3. HANDOFF

Reveal the independently standing source.

The source must not exist for the prototype.

## 4. DELTA

Ask:

WHAT DISTINCTION DID CONTACT ADD
THAT THE MODEL HAD COMPRESSED?

## 5. CONSEQUENCE

The receiver revises:
- ranking;
- points;
- interpretation;
- confidence;
- or wording.

The difference between before and after is the felt consequence.

## 6. TRACE

Preserve:
- initial model;
- receiver commitment;
- source;
- revised judgment;
- reason for change.

---

# What This Prototype Is NOT

It is not:
- a quiz;
- a test of intelligence;
- a trick;
- a "gotcha";
- an AI accuracy benchmark.

There is no score for being right.

The object of study is:

HOW RELIANCE CHANGES
WHEN CONTACT RESTORES
A LOST DISTINCTION.

---

# Preferred Source Class

For the first run:

**DOCUMENTARY / INSTITUTIONAL PRIMARY SOURCE**

Preferred properties:
- public;
- low sensitivity;
- short;
- independently custodied;
- title / index available separately from body;
- body contains material distinctions not evident in metadata;
- not famous enough that the receiver likely knows the answer already.

Examples:
- SEC filing;
- public board decision;
- agency determination;
- archival letter;
- municipal record;
- court order with low personal sensitivity;
- official dataset release.

---

# Receiver Commitment Format

Recommended:

## Before source

"Based only on the model, allocate 100 points between these interpretations."

A: ______
B: ______

Then:

"What single distinction are you relying on most?"

This creates investment without real-world risk.

## After source

"Reallocate the same 100 points."

Then:

"What changed?"

The delta is visible and quantitative enough to preserve trace without reducing interpretation to a score.

---

# Success Criteria

Prototype 03 passes if:

1. the receiver forms a real pre-contact judgment;
2. the primary source changes the receiver's warranted judgment;
3. the change turns on a distinction that the model had compressed;
4. the source's standing cannot be replaced by a synthetic substitute;
5. the receiver does not need The Outside framework explained in order to experience the shift.

The fifth criterion is critical.

If the receiver simply says:

"I see what your theory is demonstrating,"

the prototype is still too explanatory.

The desired result is closer to:

"I was comfortable with that account until the source introduced something I hadn't realized mattered."

---

# Failure Criteria

Fail if:
- the source merely confirms the model;
- the model is intentionally misleading;
- the receiver feels tested rather than involved;
- correction depends on obscure technical trivia;
- the primary source is so dense that the effect becomes reading comprehension;
- the author explains the intended insight before the receiver encounters it;
- the result is interesting only because an AI made a mistake.

---

# Prototype 02 Inheritance

Prototype 02 established:

> **THE MODEL CAN BE USEFUL, COHERENT, AND MOSTLY RIGHT WHILE REMOVING THE DISTINCTION THAT LATER BECOMES DECISIVE.**

Prototype 03 therefore targets:

COHERENCE
-> RELIANCE
-> CONTACT
-> LOST DISTINCTION RETURNS
-> JUDGMENT CHANGES.

Compression:

**MODEL → RELIANCE → HANDOFF → DISTINCTION → REVISION → TRACE**
