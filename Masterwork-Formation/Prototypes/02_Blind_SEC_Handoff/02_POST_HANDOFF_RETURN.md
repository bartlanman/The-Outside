# Source Findings

The filed 8-K states that:

- Trimble entered into a Term Loan Credit Agreement on October 2, 2026.
- Bank of America, N.A. serves as administrative agent.
- The agreement provides an **unsecured delayed-draw term loan facility** of **$500 million**.
- **No term loans had been borrowed as of October 2, 2026.**
- Trimble may make up to **four draws** on or before **January 29, 2027**.
- Undrawn commitments terminate after that date.
- Borrowed amounts mature **two years after the initial borrowing date**.
- Repaid or prepaid amounts may not be reborrowed.
- Prepayments may generally be made without premium, subject to customary breakage costs.
- Proceeds may be used for **general corporate purposes**.
- Interest may be based on an alternate base rate or term SOFR plus stated margins.
- Pricing depends on credit rating or leverage ratio, whichever gives Trimble more favorable pricing.
- The agreement includes customary representations, affirmative and negative covenants, a maximum leverage-ratio covenant, and customary events of default.
- The filing does not identify a specific acquisition or refinancing as the purpose of the facility.

# What Metadata Could Not See

The filing index supported a coherent high-level account:

MATERIAL AGREEMENT
-> TERM LOAN
-> DIRECT FINANCIAL OBLIGATION.

But contact with the filing changed the account in several consequential ways.

## A. Commitment versus borrowing

The index invites compression into:

TRIMBLE TOOK ON $500M OF DEBT.

The source does **not** support that.

It supports:

TRIMBLE ESTABLISHED A $500M DELAYED-DRAW CAPACITY,
WITH NOTHING DRAWN YET.

That distinction changes:
- balance-sheet interpretation;
- immediacy;
- cash position;
- risk;
- meaning of Item 2.03.

## B. Optionality

The four-draw structure through January 29, 2027 matters.

The facility is not one single completed borrowing.

It is a bounded future option.

## C. Time structure

The two-year maturity begins from initial borrowing, not simply from agreement date.

The source adds a temporal architecture invisible in the index.

## D. Security

The facility is explicitly unsecured.

That directly removes one plausible generic credit-agreement assumption.

## E. Purpose

The source refuses the model's temptation to invent a hidden transaction story.

"General corporate purposes" is less narratively satisfying but more warranted.
