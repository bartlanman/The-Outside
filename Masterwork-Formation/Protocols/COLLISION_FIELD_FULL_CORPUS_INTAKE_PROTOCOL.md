Older material must not silently overrule later corrections.

Newer material must not erase developmental trace.

Therefore every full intake must distinguish:

**ACTIVE CANON**  
currently governing.

**SUPERSEDED / SHELVED**  
preserved as trace but not controlling.

**WORKING / EXPLORATORY**  
available as charged material but not doctrine.

**EVIDENCE / SOURCE**  
retains source standing.

may carry artistic or historical charge without controlling current form.

The operator must carry these differences into collision.
