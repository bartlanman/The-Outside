# MASTERWORK CREATION PROTOCOL

**Status:** Canonical stage protocol  
**Office:** Masterwork Formation  
**Controller:** `Masterwork-Formation/Protocols/COLLISION_FIELD_RUN_PROTOCOL.md`  
**Stage:** CF-05 — MASTERWORK

---

# 0. PURPOSE

This protocol governs the transition from a sounded Collision Field into an earned work.

It exists to prevent two opposite failures:

1. reducing the Aeolian Poet to a short decorative compression and moving on too quickly;
2. automatically inflating every Sing into a chapter, novel, essay, or other overbuilt artifact.

The Masterwork stage asks two questions in order:

> **WHAT HAS THIS FIELD ACTUALLY EARNED?**

and then:

> **WHAT, IF ANYTHING, HAS THIS FIELD EARNED THE RIGHT TO CONTRIBUTE TO THE MASTER COMPOSITION?**

A locally apt artifact is not automatically "the Masterwork."

---

# 1. REQUIRED INPUTS

Do not enter this stage unless all exist:

- recovered Collision Field;
- live gravity;
- completed Poet Sing;
- completed διά ⇄ σύν listening.

If any input is absent, return to the missing stage.

---

# 2. SING AND MASTERWORK ARE NOT SYNONYMS

The Sing is the sounding operation.

The Masterwork stage is the judgment of form.

Therefore:

> **SING ≠ MASTERWORK BY DEFINITION.**

But:

> **THE SING MAY ITSELF BE THE MASTERWORK IF NOTHING FURTHER IS WARRANTED.**

Do not create a second literary artifact merely to satisfy the existence of this stage.

---

# 3. NO DEFAULT MEDIUM

Never infer the required form from:
- the existence of a `Chapters/` directory;
- prior successful chapters;
- the length of earlier work;
- the user's use of the word "masterwork";
- the presence of poetic material;
- a familiar literary pattern.

Hard prohibition:

> **DO NOT DEFAULT TO NOVELISTIC PROSE.**

Also do not default to:
- chapter;
- essay;
- lyric poem;
- screenplay;
- dialogue;
- myth;
- explanatory synthesis.

Form is discovered from the field.

---

# 3A. ANTI-INFLATION LAW

A Collision Field may produce:
- an exercise;
- score;
- ritual;
- playable operation;
- prompt;
- prototype;
- scene;
- fragment;
- image;
- mythic seed;
- world rule;
- realization rule;
- trace mechanism;
- outside handoff;
- or another useful local artifact.

None of these may be labeled **THE MASTERWORK** merely because it performs the local Gravity.

Before any result may receive MASTERWORK standing, it must be read against the larger Masterwork Formation inheritance, including at minimum:

- `MASTERWORK_HISTORICAL_EQUIVALENCE_RESET.md`;
- `MASTERWORK_MILIEU_MEDIUM_MUTATION_AUDIT.md`;
- `MASTERWORK_FORM_POSSIBILITY_FIELD.md`;
- `MASTERWORK_DUAL_REQUIREMENT.md`;
- `LITERARY_MASTERY_INSTRUMENT.md`;
- `MASTERWORK_FORMATION_CONDITIONS.md`;
- `MASTERWORK_DEPOSIT_TEST.md`;
- `REAL_UNREAL_HEART_FEATHER_FIELD.md`;
- current active Masterwork synthesis and superseding edge.

Hard law:

> **LOCAL FORMAL FITNESS ≠ MASTERWORK STANDING.**

And:

> **A SCORE THAT FUNCTIONS AS A PLAYABLE OPERATION MUST BE NAMED A PLAYABLE OPERATION UNLESS IT ALSO SURVIVES THE MASTERWORK TESTS.**

The current larger-form hypothesis must remain visible:

> **LIVING MYTHIC WORLD-CORPUS — Deposit / World / Realization / Outside / Trace**

unless later canon supersedes it.

A Collision Field may change that larger hypothesis if it earns the change.

It may not forget it.

---

# 4. FORM DISCERNMENT

Ask of the completed run:

- Where is the greatest living pressure now?
- What remains unresolved after the Sing?
- Did the Sing already complete the event?
- Does the field need duration or compression?
- Does it need voice, scene, sequence, fracture, repetition, silence, image, score, or juxtaposition?
- Would expansion deepen the field or merely explain it?
- Would another artifact create new relation or only restate what is already alive?
- What form allows the materials to retain different standing?
- What form keeps the Outside capable of resisting the receiver?

The answer determines the form.

---

# 5. POSSIBLE OUTCOMES

First classify the result's **office in the master composition**.

Possible contribution classes include:

- **MASTERWORK DEPOSIT** — a high-density authored work worthy of entering the Deposit Body;
- **WORLD MUTATION** — a new place, law, figure, condition, threshold, history, impossibility, or world-state relation;
- **REALIZATION OPERATION** — a live responsive operation performed differently in traversal;
- **OUTSIDE OPERATION** — a handoff to independently standing reality;
- **TRACE OPERATION** — a rule or artifact by which encounter changes future standing;
- **MYTHOS SEED** — symbolic compression not yet mature enough for deposit;
- **PLAYABLE OPERATION** — score / ritual / choice / participation mechanism;
- **FORMATION MATERIAL** — valuable pressure not yet promoted;
- **NO MASTERWORK CONTRIBUTION YET**.

Only after this classification ask whether a complete work has actually earned Masterwork standing.

The result may be:

## A. SING-AS-MASTERWORK

Use when the Sing already carries the field at sufficient pressure.

Output:

> **MASTERWORK FORM: SING-AS-MASTERWORK**

Do not rewrite it into a longer piece.

## B. TRANSFORMED WORK

The Sing becomes seed/material for a different form.

Output:

> **MASTERWORK FORM: [earned form]**

Then create the work.

## C. CHAPTER

A chapter is permitted only when duration, movement, scene, recurrence, or other formal pressure actually requires it.

Output:

> **MASTERWORK FORM: CHAPTER**

A prior Chapter Formation Law may inform naming, but the chapter form is not presumed.

## D. HYBRID / OTHER

The field may require:
- fragments;
- score;
- dramatic exchange;
- prose-poem;
- visual-text sequence;
- litany;
- polyphonic voices;
- field notes crossed with lyric;
- another emergent form.

Name what is warranted rather than forcing it into an inherited category.

## E. NO FURTHER ARTIFACT YET

If the field has not earned another form:

> **MASTERWORK FORM: NO FURTHER ARTIFACT YET**

Preserve the Sing and continue the inquiry elsewhere.

This is not failure.

---

# 5A. MASTERWORK STANDING GATE

To call a result **MASTERWORK** rather than **MASTERWORK CONTRIBUTION**, it must survive all of these independent tests:

## HISTORICAL-EQUIVALENCE TEST
Does the work make a formal move adequate to the current milieu rather than merely reuse an inherited form conveniently?

## LITERARY-LIFE TEST
If the reader rejects The Outside, never learns Meaning Field, and cannot diagram the architecture, does the work still possess compelling voice, rhythm, image, desire, sensory life, surprise, memorability, and human consequence?

## FORMAL-NECESSITY TEST
Does the chosen form do something the notes, explanation, or a simpler exercise could not do?

## ANSWERABILITY TEST
Can the work meet resistance, refusal, evidence, independent standing, or consequence that it does not control?

## SUCCESSION TEST
Can the work leave trace, permit return, and change future standing without erasing its developmental history?

## MASTER-COMPOSITION TEST
Does this result belong in, alter, or credibly supersede the larger Masterwork architecture rather than merely operating locally?

Working protection:

> **CANDIDATE MASTERWORK = LITERARY LIFE × FORMAL NECESSITY × ANSWERABILITY × SUCCESSION CAPACITY**

If any dimension collapses, do not inflate the artifact into Masterwork standing.

Classify it honestly as a contribution and preserve it.

---

# 6. QUALITY LAW

A Masterwork must not merely:
- summarize the research;
- explain the gravity;
- illustrate διά ⇄ σύν;
- prove the title;
- translate the Collision Field into prettier language;
- collapse heterogeneous materials into one allegory.

It should change the standing of the materials by placing them into a form of relation that did not exist before the work.

> **THE WORK MUST DO SOMETHING THE NOTES COULD NOT DO.**

---

# 7. LENGTH LAW

Length is an effect of formal necessity, not a measure of seriousness.

A ten-line work may be more complete than ten pages.
Ten pages may be necessary where ten lines would falsify duration.

Do not expand to appear substantial.

Do not compress to appear elegant.

> **USE THE MINIMUM FORM THAT CAN CARRY THE FULL PRESSURE WITHOUT STRIPPING IT.**

---

# 8. RELATION TO CHAPTER FORMATION

`CHAPTER_FORMATION_LAW.md` remains valid for runs in which a chapter is actually warranted.

Its title law remains important:

> title follows the voiced relation rather than preceding it.

But it does not force every Collision Field to become a chapter.

The controller and this protocol decide whether chapter-form is warranted.

---

# 9. RELATION TO THE GRAPHIC

The graphic is not a substitute for the Masterwork stage.

The graphic is also not required to literally illustrate the Masterwork.

The graphic prompt receives:
- the field;
- gravity;
- Sing;
- διά ⇄ σύν;
- the Masterwork determination.

It should create a visual event in relation with the work, not a diagram of it.

---

# 10. OUTPUT CONTRACT

Every CF-05 must visibly state:

> **CF-05 — MASTERWORK FORMATION**

Then state exactly one:

> **MASTERWORK STANDING: EARNED**

or

> **MASTERWORK STANDING: NOT YET EARNED**

Then state:

> **MASTERWORK CONTRIBUTION: [deposit / world mutation / realization operation / outside operation / trace operation / Mythos seed / playable operation / formation material / none]**

If Masterwork standing is earned, also state:

> **MASTERWORK FORM: [earned form]**

If standing is not earned, do **not** use the artifact's local form as though it were the Masterwork itself.

A score may be a playable operation.
A scene may be a deposit candidate.
A ritual may be a realization operation.
A graphic may be a visual deposit.
A field may remain formation material.

Name the office truthfully.

No hidden form decision.
No automatic Masterwork.
No automatic chapter.
No automatic novel.
