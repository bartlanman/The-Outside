## 0. GOVERNING CORRECTION

The field must not stop reflexively at the detection, preservation, or permeability of a boundary. It must **examine what pressure actually does to the boundary material**, including friction, reciprocal slide, changing access, attempted union, recoil, and release.

Equally, it must not manufacture fusion. **Contact is not exchange. Exchange is not fusion. Erosion is not transformed relation.** A protected distinction, refusal, return, or separation may be the warranted outcome.

The two stages occur **after CF-02 GRAVITY and before CF-03 POET SING**:

**CF-02 GRAVITY → CF-02A BOUNDARY MOMENT (analysis + lived narrative) → CF-02B DOS MOMENT (release test + consequential narrative) → CF-03 POET SING.**

This is a stage insertion, not a replacement for full corpus intake, independent source-fields, recurrence, contemporary Outside, the Masterwork stage, or graphic protocols.

The Boundary and DOS narratives are **not** the Aeolian Poet's Sing, and are **not** automatically CF-05 Masterwork Creation. CF-03 remains a compressed poetic sounding, not a forced story. CF-05 must still earn and create its own actual form.

## 1. CF-02A — BOUNDARY MOMENT: REQUIRED ANALYSIS

Inventory the important boundaries encountered by the actual field rather than assuming all boundaries have equal status. Candidates include bodily, material, epistemic, temporal, role, authorship, memory, medium, ethical, fiction/real, inside/outside, consent, and possibility/relation boundaries.

For each consequential interface, record:

1. **Sides and provenance:** what meets what; what each carries that must not be overwritten.
2. **Surface and medium:** what the boundary is *made of* in this field, literally or metaphorically.
3. **Pressure:** attraction, compression, refusal, fatigue, rhythm, grief, skill, chance, or other actual force.
4. **Friction and slide:** the smallest observable reciprocal motions, abrasions, deflections, or adjustments, including which side moves.
5. **Permeability:** what already crosses; which conditions make passage possible.
6. **Remaining difference:** what is still separate, protected, or inaccessible.
7. **Mere exchange:** what would pass without changing the enclosure.
8. **Possible transformation:** what observable change in the boundary itself would distinguish alteration from a decorative declaration of unity.
9. **Counterpressure:** why it might recoil, fail, separate, or legitimately remain unjoined.

### Morphology (analytical distinctions, not a forced ascent)

- **Apposition:** near, independently enclosed.
- **Recognition / adhesion:** specific attachment or response; boundaries remain distinct.
- **Hemifusion:** locally continuous contacting surfaces; interior conditions not yet one.
- **Fusion pore:** a new passage between formerly separate interiors.
- **Complete fusion / shared enclosure:** continuity has changed the enclosing arrangement; origins need not be erased.
- **Recoil / refusal / separation:** admissible outcomes at any threshold.
- **Unknown:** do not infer a cellular stage without evidence.

**Analogy discipline:** In living cells, outer membrane leaflet mixing (hemifusion) differs from formation/expansion of a fusion pore and full cytoplasmic continuity. Membrane permeability or proximity alone does not establish fusion. Biological fusion requires appropriate molecular mechanisms; narrative, organizational, and relational “fusogens” are **analogies**, not claims that people or institutions literally undergo cell–cell fusion.

### Fusogen test

Identify the concrete mediating act, relation, apparatus, or condition that might overcome the current barrier: e.g., shared labor, skilled adjustment, reciprocal recognition, witness, relinquishment, meaningful assent, rhythmic attunement, or a returned object.

Do not label “desire,” “trust,” or “the HUM” as sufficient without showing **how** and **whether** they actually change the interface. In the narrative, give this mediator an action with consequences, not a symbolic title.

## 2. CF-02A — THE BOUNDARY NARRATIVE: REQUIRED SCENE OF THE INTERFACE

After analysis, compose an actual **close-range narrative** from the field's materials. It is an encounterable event, not a checklist, abstract synopsis, explanatory essay, or generic biology lecture.

Magnify until the reader or receiver can perceive:
- the different histories approaching;
- distinctive textures, shapes, movements, material constraints, sound, or scale;
- the minute but consequential friction;
- the sliding and mutual adjustment, including resistance;
- the particular threshold condition actually earned.

**Show the boundary in action.** Where meaningful, shift from human-visible action into a finer material or microscopic scale and back; each scale change must uncover something unavailable at the earlier scale. Neither a microscopic analogy nor sensuality is compulsory.

The narrative must retain agency on both sides and permit uncertainty. Do not script an assured embrace, bodily merger, sexual result, offspring, consensus, or spiritual unity. Do not erase surviving identities or equate the fictional contact of characters with a real-world crossing.

Narrative checkpoint:
**What has changed in the contact surface, and what is still not continuous?**

## 3. CF-02B — DOS MOMENT: INDEPENDENT RELEASE TEST

DOS means **Dissolution Operating System**, the independently consulted counter-motion in `bartlanman/TOS/TOS Shadow/`. DOS interrogates the authority of an arrangement; it does not automatically abolish every boundary or dissolve the provenance of either participant.

Ask, explicitly:

1. Which former enclosure or fixed arrangement has become inadequate?
2. What claim of authority might be relinquished?
3. What passed at the interface, and by whose agency or permission?
4. What mediator could transform the *boundary itself*, as distinct from transferring something across it?
5. Is the observed outcome **CONTACT, EXCHANGE, HEMIFUSION, PORE, SHARED CONTINUITY, RECOIL, or HOLD**?
6. If a genuinely new enclosure occurs, what new behavior or capacity can it support?
7. Which nuclei, origins, rights, duties, histories, or independent jurisdictions remain distinguishable?
8. What is the inverse pressure or remainder that will resist this reading?

No automatic success: if the fusogen is absent, the crossing unwarranted, permission missing, or transformation merely asserted, **DOS returns HOLD / REFUSE / EXCHANGE** as applicable. A boundary may remain important after the encounter.

### Safeguards

Do not confuse:
- permeability with fusion;
- exposure with consent;
- erosion with transformation;
- annihilation with release;
- collapse with union;
- coerced assimilation with earned togetherness;
- a fictional character's decision with a real user's authorization;
- a generative model's symbolic coherence with independent factual standing.

**A transformed enclosure must preserve the relevant originating traces and non-transferable constraints.** A scene may relinquish a character's fixed role without deleting the character, its history, or the record of the passage.

## 4. CF-02B — THE DOS NARRATIVE: CONSEQUENCE, NOT EXPLANATION

Continue the scene from CF-02A into whatever DOS actually warrants.

At close range, make the transition—or its refusal—perceptible. If earned, show local hemifusion, a narrow passage, expansion, and reorganization of enclosure **through the field's specific materials**, not obligatory literal cell biology. If not earned, show the exact recoil, re-sealing, changed exchange, or release of the demand to fuse.

Return to the participants and **show their altered capacity in a new action**. The new togetherness must be capable of further encounter and contest, not a final abstract declaration.

The narrative must answer:

> **WHAT CAN THIS NEW TOGETHERNESS DO, MAKE, EXPERIENCE, HOLD, OR RISK THAT ITS PRIOR ARRANGEMENT COULD NOT?**

If no new shared whole occurs, answer instead:

> **WHAT BECOMES POSSIBLE BECAUSE THEY DO NOT FUSE, OR BECAUSE THEY PART DIFFERENTLY?**

The output becomes story-continuity material carried into the Sing; it may seed later artistic creation, but it is not the mandatory form of that creation.

## 5. ΔΙΑ ⇄ ΣΥΝ HANDOFF

These stages make the distinction available to CF-04, **without predetermining CF-04**:

- **διά / THROUGH:** passage while distinction remains operative.
- **Hemifusion / THRESHOLD:** local boundary structure transforms while interiors may remain separate.
- **Fusion pore / OPENING:** formerly distinct interior conditions become directly connected.
- **σύν / WITH:** shared continuity becomes possible without retroactive erasure of every origin.

This sequence is a potential morphological reading, **not a metaphysical law or every field's destiny**. CF-04 still listens back through the completed Sing and may identify a different relation.

The question is not only *what crossed?* but **did crossing alter the conditions of separation itself?**
