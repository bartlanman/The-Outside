# COLLISION FIELD SING PROTOCOL

**Status:** Hard execution contract  
**Office:** Masterwork Formation / Poetic Sounding  
**Purpose:** Prevent Collision Field SING from drifting into essay, explanation, chapter prose, scene-writing, character-writing, or novelistic narration.

---

# 0. GOVERNING DISTINCTION

The Collision Field **SING** is not a chapter draft.

It is not:
- a novel scene;
- a short story;
- character development;
- exposition;
- a literary essay;
- an explanation of the collision field;
- a proof of the gravity;
- a dramatization of the project's architecture.

The SING is:

> **THE FIELD'S GRAVITY MADE AUDIBLE AS POETIC PRESSURE.**

It is a compressed sounding.

It exists between:

> **GRAVITY → SING → ΔΙΑ ⇄ ΣΥΝ**

It does not silently expand into another literary form.

---

# 1. INVOCATION

Whenever the Collision Field workflow reaches **SING**, re-read this file before writing.

Do not reconstruct the SING method from memory.

Do not substitute:
- the Literary Mastery Instrument;
- the Chapter files;
- scene-generation methods;
- character/plot prompts;
- novel conventions;
- prior stylistic habits.

Those may belong to other offices.

They do not govern SING.

---

# 2. FORM

Default SING form:

> **a compressed lyrical sounding, usually a short poem, poetic paragraph, or lineated field-song**

It should be:
- imaginal;
- rhythmic;
- compressed;
- sensorial where earned;
- emotionally charged;
- open;
- non-explanatory;
- able to hold contradiction without solving it.

It may use:
- repetition;
- image;
- breath;
- juxtaposition;
- fragments;
- refrain;
- compression;
- silence;
- tonal turn;
- a few concrete objects if the field has earned them.

It should not require:
- protagonist;
- named character;
- scene arc;
- dialogue;
- chronology;
- backstory;
- plot;
- narrative payoff.

---

# 3. THE POET'S OFFICE

The poet enters as a **tensioned instrument**.

The poet does not:
- summarize;
- explain;
- lecture;
- prove;
- resolve;
- illustrate a thesis;
- invent a character merely to embody an idea;
- convert every material into project vocabulary.

The poet listens for:
- the pressure that repeats;
- the image that will not leave;
- the rhythm of attraction / repulsion;
- the tenderness, terror, hunger, restraint, exposure, or release inside the gravity;
- the place where unlike materials begin sounding together without becoming identical.

Working law:

> **DO NOT TELL US WHAT THE FIELD MEANS. LET US HEAR WHAT IT DOES.**

---

# 4. LENGTH DISCIPLINE

Default SING length is **compressed**.

Usually:
- approximately 12–40 lines, or
- approximately 150–500 words if paragraphic.

Longer is allowed only if the field itself produces sustained poetic pressure.

Do not enlarge the SING simply to make it feel more important.

Compression is part of its office.

---

# 5. HARD ANTI-NOVEL RULE

The following are automatic failures unless the user explicitly requests chapter/scene prose:

- "The first time he/she..."
- a protagonist entering a remembered scene;
- domestic realism used as default embodiment;
- spouse / daughter / friend / stranger inserted to humanize an abstract idea;
- dialogue scenes;
- chronological anecdote;
- scene-setting whose main function is to make an idea literary;
- several pages of narrative prose;
- a story engineered to arrive at the known gravity.

If the output begins reading primarily as fiction or memoir:

> **FAIL — RETURN TO POETIC COMPRESSION.**

The SING may contain a human voice.

It must not become a narrative character apparatus by default.

---

# 6. HARD ANTI-EXPLANATION RULE

Automatic failure if the SING:
- names the thesis and then decorates it;
- repeatedly explains its own images;
- uses project terms as substitutes for poetry;
- concludes with a lesson;
- resolves the collision;
- sounds like exegesis written in line breaks.

The SING must preserve remainder.

Working law:

> **THE SONG MAY KNOW LESS THAN THE EXEGESIS AND STILL BE TRUER TO THE FIELD.**

---

# 7. MATERIAL PRESERVATION

The collision materials must not be flattened into one metaphor too early.

If the field contains:
- animal;
- machine;
- ritual;
- body;
- joke;
- inheritance;
- weather;
- code;

the SING may let them touch, echo, interrupt, or transform one another.

It should not force them to become interchangeable.

Ask:

> **WHAT CAN SOUND TOGETHER WITHOUT BECOMING THE SAME THING?**

---

# 8. TITLE DISCIPLINE

Do not title the chapter before SING.

Do not write toward an already selected elegant title.

After the SING is complete:

1. read it through **διά ⇄ σύν**;
2. listen for the relation in which one side enters the other;
3. only then allow a chapter name to emerge.

If a title was already proposed earlier, treat it as provisional and re-earn it after the new SING.

---

# 9. SING LINT

Before accepting the SING, ask:

## Form
- Is this primarily poetic compression rather than narrative prose? → must be **YES**
- Could this exist without a protagonist or plot? → must be **YES**
- Is it substantially shorter and more compressed than a chapter draft? → must be **YES**

## Office
- Does it sound the gravity rather than explain it? → must be **YES**
- Does it preserve contradiction / remainder? → must be **YES**
- Does it avoid proving a preselected thesis? → must be **YES**

## Drift
- Did I import scene/character rules from Literary Mastery? → **FAIL**
- Did I imitate the surface form of existing Chapters merely because they exist? → **FAIL**
- Did I turn the SING into a novel, memoir, parable, or essay without explicit instruction? → **FAIL**

If any failure occurs:

> **STOP. DISCARD THE DRAFT. RETURN TO GRAVITY AND SOUND IT AGAIN.**

---

# 10. RELATION TO CHAPTER FORMATION

SING and CHAPTER are adjacent but not identical offices.

Default Collision Field workflow:

> **FIELD → GRAVITY → SING → ΔΙΑ ⇄ ΣΥΝ → GRAPHIC PROMPT / EXEGESIS**

A separate chapter-promotion process may later ask whether the SING should become:
- a longer poem;
- prose;
- scene;
- sequence;
- hybrid form;
- or remain compressed.

That later promotion must be **explicitly invoked**.

Never assume:

> **SING = WRITE A NOVEL CHAPTER.**

Existing Chapter files are deposits of prior formation work. They are not a mandatory surface template for every future SING.
