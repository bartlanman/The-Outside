# COLLISION FIELD GRAPHIC PROTOCOL

**Status:** Working protocol  
**Office:** Masterwork Formation / Visual Art  
**Purpose:** Define the operating sequence for collision-field-to-graphic generation.

---

# 0. PRINCIPLE

When the user invokes:

> **collision field: [name / number / title]**

the process does **not** begin by making an image.

It proceeds in a fixed sequence:

1. **Extract the gravity**
2. **Let the field sing**
3. **Articulate the διά ⇄ σύν relation**
4. **Create the graphic prompt**
5. **Write the exegesis**
6. **Generate the graphic**

This preserves the order:

> **FIELD → GRAVITY → SONG → RELATION → PROMPT → EXEGESIS → IMAGE**

The image is last.

---

# 1. INVOCATION FORM

The user may invoke a field by number, title, or shorthand.

Examples:

- `collision field 05`
- `collision field: contact / flock / answer / lace / void`
- `collision field 06`
- `collision field: shell / seed / heat / ferment / threshold`

This invocation means:

> recover the field and run the full graphic protocol.

---

# 2. STEP ONE — GRAVITY

First identify the field's **gravity**.

Gravity is not a summary.
Gravity is the pressure that pulls the field together.

Questions:

- What is the central pressure?
- What is being drawn toward what?
- What is at stake?
- What transforms under this field?
- What cannot remain as it was?

Output form:

> **GRAVITY:** [short living statement]

Example style:

> **GRAVITY:** the form that once protected life has become too small for the life it protected.

The gravity should be brief, exact, and generative.

---

# 3. STEP TWO — SING

Next the field must **sing**.

This is the poetic compression of the field.
Not analysis first, but the tonal and imaginal note the field carries.

The sing should answer:

- what kind of emotional music does the field make?
- what is its tenderness / terror / yearning / restraint / release?
- how does it move?
- how does it sound as living relation?

Output form:

> **SING:** [poetic paragraph or compressed lyrical statement]

The sing should not become decorative language.
It should feel like the field's actual music.

---

# 4. STEP THREE — ΔΙΑ ⇄ ΣΥΝ

Next articulate the field's **διά ⇄ σύν** relation.

This does not mean pasting the symbol into interpretation.
It means clarifying:

- what is passing through?
- what is remaining with?
- where is the opening?
- where is the togetherness?
- how do separation and relation coexist here?

Output form:

> **ΔΙΑ:** [through / passage / rupture / traversal]  
> **ΣΥΝ:** [with / together / held relation / gathered condition]  
> **ΔΙΑ ⇄ ΣΥΝ:** [how the field oscillates or lives through both]

This relation should be stated in a way that can later inform both prompt and exegesis.

---

# 5. STEP FOUR — GRAPHIC PROMPT CREATION

Only after gravity, sing, and διά ⇄ σύν are clarified does the image prompt get written.

The prompt must:

- produce a **single visual event**
- avoid merely illustrating concepts
- let the field act on the medium
- allow scale shifts: far / middle / near
- preserve mystery
- carry the field's actual gravity
- permit exegesis afterward

The prompt should not over-explain symbolism.

It should establish:

- field motion
- material tensions
- spatial conditions
- emotional temperature
- scale behavior
- the field-specific embodiment of the standing body-fragment rule

## Standing body clause — required in every graphic prompt

Every collision-field graphic prompt must include and adapt the following passage to the field's gravity:

> **Include an abstract, sensual, partially dissipating human form integrated into the field itself. The body should not appear as a separate posed figure, but as a poetic fragment of living flesh emerging from, dissolving into, or being altered by the field's motion. Let the collision gravity determine whether the form is being drawn inward, pressed outward, opened, gathered, or dispersed. The body may be feminine, masculine, androgynous, or only suggestively human, depending on the field's emotional and symbolic gravity. Emphasize partial zones such as throat, shoulder, rib, waist, hip, back, hand, or thigh, rendered with sensual tenderness but without explicit nudity. The flesh should share the same ontology as the rest of the image: part body, part atmosphere, part material event.**

This clause is not decorative.

The prompt must decide, from the field itself:

- whether the body gives or receives;
- whether it is drawn inward or pushed outward;
- which part or parts of the body carry the gravity;
- whether the embodiment reads as feminine, masculine, androgynous, or only suggestively human;
- how flesh becomes continuous with the field rather than appearing as an inserted figure.

Standing compression:

> **EVERY FIELD CONTAINS A SENSUAL DISSIPATING HUMAN FRAGMENT WHOSE FORM IS DETERMINED BY THE COLLISION GRAVITY, SO THAT FLESH BECOMES PART OF THE FIELD AND THE FIELD BECOMES PART OF THE FLESH.**

Output form:

> **GRAPHIC PROMPT:** [full image-generation prompt, including the adapted standing body clause]

---

# 6. STEP FIVE — EXEGESIS

After the prompt is created, write the **exegesis**.

The exegesis is not a generic explanation.
It should read the generated or intended graphic back through the field.

It should cover:

1. how the image carries the collision field
2. how the gravity appears visually
3. how the sing appears visually
4. how διά ⇄ σύν is present
5. what the major materials / tensions / motions are
6. what the image gets right or fails to get right

Output form:

> **EXEGESIS:** [interpretive reading of the field as image]

The exegesis should help determine whether the image is strong enough to keep, revise, or canonize.

---

# 7. STEP SIX — CREATE THE GRAPHIC

Only now is the graphic generated.

The image should be treated as:

- a field event
- a visual test
- a deposit to be judged
- not yet canon unless warranted

After generation, the result may be:

- kept
- revised
- discarded
- used as anchor for further field work
- canonized if it truly carries the field

---

# 8. BODY RULE

A collision-field graphic must include:

> **an abstract, sensual, partially dissipating human fragment integrated into the field itself**

This should not be a separate posed figure.
It should be part of the event.

The body fragment may be:

- feminine
- masculine
- androgynous
- suggestively human

depending on the field's gravity.

It should be determined by the field, not added decoratively.

Useful zones include:

- throat
- shoulder
- rib
- waist
- hip
- back
- hand
- thigh
- jawline / mouth in partial dissolution

The flesh should be:

- poetic
- integrated
- directional
- partially abstract
- non-explicit
- part of the field's giving / receiving / drawing / emergence / dispersal

Standing rule:

> **EVERY FIELD CONTAINS A SENSUAL DISSIPATING HUMAN FRAGMENT WHOSE FORM IS DETERMINED BY THE COLLISION GRAVITY, SO THAT FLESH BECOMES PART OF THE FIELD AND THE FIELD BECOMES PART OF THE FLESH.**

The body is therefore not added after the field is composed. It is one of the materials through which the field's gravity becomes visible.

---

# 9. VISUAL RULES

The process should preserve the current visual discoveries without turning them into a cage.

Possible gravities may include:

- contact without collapse
- a whole with no single location
- load-bearing emptiness
- protection becoming constraint
- emergence that looks like damage
- the gap that participates
- the seam as negotiated transformation

These may enter the work as force, not as required iconography.

The image should avoid:

- explanatory symbolism
- obvious diagrams
- cliché surrealism
- literalized thesis illustration
- overbuilt allegory
- decorative mysticism
- automatic central symbol placement

---

# 10. CANONICAL SEQUENCE

When the user says:

> **collision field**

the default sequence is:

## A. Recover the field
name, number, existing field document

## B. State the gravity
short living statement

## C. Let it sing
poetic compression

## D. Run διά ⇄ σύν
through / with relation

## E. Write the graphic prompt
full prompt draft

## F. Write the exegesis
reading of the intended or generated image

## G. Generate the image
graphic output

## H. Judge the result
keep / revise / discard / canonize

---

# 11. SHORT OPERATOR VERSION

> **COLLISION FIELD → GRAVITY → SING → ΔΙΑ ⇄ ΣΥΝ → GRAPHIC PROMPT → EXEGESIS → GRAPHIC**

This is now the standing workflow.

---

# 12. CURRENT COMMITMENT

The image is not the first discovery.

The field is.

The image is the last test.

Therefore:

> **DO NOT START WITH THE PICTURE.**

> **LET THE FIELD BECOME GRAVITY.**

> **LET THE GRAVITY SING.**

> **LET ΔΙΑ ⇄ ΣΥΝ BECOME LEGIBLE.**

> **THEN WRITE THE PROMPT.**

> **THEN WRITE THE EXEGESIS.**

> **THEN MAKE THE IMAGE.**
