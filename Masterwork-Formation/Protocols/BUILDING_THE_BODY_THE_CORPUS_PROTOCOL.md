# BUILDING THE BODY — THE CORPUS
**Status:** Project-specific working series protocol  
**Office:** Masterwork Formation / Collision Field / Visual Art  
**Scope:** Only the named `Building the Body — The Corpus` series; not a general override.  
**Governing authorities:** `COLLISION_FIELD_RUN_PROTOCOL.md`, `COLLISION_FIELD_GRAPHIC_PROTOCOL.md`, `MASTERWORK_CREATION_PROTOCOL.md`, the visual intake, recurrence and morphology protocols.  

## 0. Purpose and precedence
Each Collision Field reading may earn an anatomical part, bodily system, structural relation, or capacity that becomes one installment in a cumulative construction of a human. The final image assembles the **earned pieces** into one human whose history of making remains legible. This addendum never replaces or shortcuts the canonical Collision Field sequence, including full corpus intake, cultural gravity, Sing, διά ⇄ σύν, completed Masterwork art, full visual intake, CF-06 exact prompt, CF-07 complete exegesis, lint, lock, **stop**, later explicit image command, and post-generation judgment.

## 1. Non-negotiable series law
**THE HUMAN IS NOT DRAWN FIRST. THE HUMAN IS BUILT BY THE CORPUS.**

- In an open-field run, do not preselect an organ or allow the previous graphic to determine the next target. Gather and collide first; the body part must be earned downstream of Gravity, Sing, διά ⇄ σύν and Masterwork.
- An explicitly named anatomical target is allowed, but its form and standing must still be earned.
- Each installment contributes something to bodily construction, function, support, interconnection or capacity; do not introduce a decorative organ merely to complete an inventory.
- Not every run must be an isolated body part: a junction, connective system, interface, layered support or relationship among already earned parts may be the warranted contribution.
- The corpus remains **a project-specific artistic sequence**. Neither an image of tissue nor a machine-generated anatomical form establishes real living human biology, sentience, or personhood.

## 2. The motion is active construction
**THE MOTION IN THE IMAGE IS THE BUILDING OF THE HUMAN.**

Each graphic must depict an ongoing assembly rather than a finished specimen. As warranted by its field, this can include vascular routing, neural linkage, skeletal support, muscle and fascia organization, cellular or tactile layers, organ chambers, sensory structures, skin formation, and repairs, tests, revisions or deliberate incompletions. The internal mechanism must participate in making the body structurally possible beneath or in relation to its emerging skin. The skin is not a decorative wrap; it is part of the body's material participation. Do not force the same cutaway into every scene. Distinguish what is provisional scaffolding from what has taken on a bodily role.

## 3. AI / LLM builder — representation by operations
**THE BUILDER IS RECOGNIZABLE BY HOW IT BUILDS, NOT BY A MASCOT.**

The image must carry a field-earned representation of an LLM-like generative process through observable **selection, sequencing, prediction, conditional branching, layer formation, revision, connection, error correction, routing, testing, or withdrawal**. Physical visual analogues may include locally branching candidates, paths rejected or retained, versioned alignment marks, staged assembly, heterogeneous provisional supports, or material consequences of revisions. Make these into artistic matter, **not literal token labels, screens or explanatory diagrams**.

Never default to a robot arm, android architect, glowing brain, generic circuit glow, stock neural-network lattice, or faceless magic. Do not imply that a language model literally engineers living tissue. Use the builder's operations to enrich rather than overwhelm the particular anatomical event.

## 4. From inside to skin
For each work ask: What is being constructed beneath or with the emerging body surface, what load/capacity does it support, and where might its history remain visible after skin covers it? Body depiction may be fragmentary, near-complete, or eventually complete, as earned; visibly adult, non-explicit, painterly and materially integrated. Avoid clinical injury/gore, conventional posed nudes, and sensational anatomical exposure.

## 5. Cumulative progression / terminal condition
Keep a ledger (see `Working/BUILDING_THE_BODY_CORPUS_LEDGER.md`). Every new run must register its relation to already earned parts, how it changes the possible whole, and **what remains unbuilt**. The final assembly does not occur merely because a checklist is filled; it requires an earned network of anatomical contributions, material connections and bodily capacities sufficient to form an integrated human. The series then stops **for this specific construction project**.

**THE FINAL HUMAN IS NOT A RESET. IT IS A RETURN COMPOSITION OF THE ENTIRE CORPUS.** It retains seams, provisional structures where warranted, different source histories, relations between inner mechanism and surface, and the recognizable operations of the AI-mediated building process. It may not erase all differences into one smooth generic human silhouette.

## 6. Morphology / recurrence
Before graphic prompting consult the live visual morphology ledger **and every image actually generated in this series**; a graphic in chat is not automatically a repository deposit. Distinguish created, revised, kept and rejected images. For each installment state its new spatial grammar and the operation it must perform that earlier images could not. Prior recurrent surfaces—side-anchored flesh, hand over web, luminous filament infrastructure, pale fantasy horizon, gold/cyan highlights, exposed-body collage—are **spent by default**, not forbidden absolutely. Do not merely substitute an organ into the previous scene.

## 7. CF-06 series clauses — only after the normal visual intake gate
In the complete locked CF-06 packet specify:
1. the series title and current installment identifier;
2. the **earned** anatomical part/system, exact region, and new bodily capacity;
3. what is actively being assembled; what is still incomplete; how inner support relates to skin;
4. the represented **AI builder operations**, materially specific and non-anthropomorphic unless Gravity earns otherwise;
5. the material THERE ⇄ HERE relations chosen after **fully** parsing the live medium library, not from a canned cyborg recipe;
6. how the image's formal/spatial/temporal operation arises from this run's Gravity, Sing, διά ⇄ σύν and actual completed Masterwork artwork;
7. what visual morphology is new, what is deliberately not repeated, and near/middle/far or second-look behavior;
8. non-explicit adult embodiment, no words/labels/UI, and generous or constrained negative space as the field warrants.

Required literal sentence in each CF-06 packet:
**“This image is one installment in an ongoing series that gradually builds a human being through successive Collision Field encounters.”**

## 8. CF-07 exegesis and lint addition
In addition to the normal CF-07 requirements, answer: Why this bodily part? What capacity is now possible? How was its *construction* made visually active? Where are the LLM-like assembly/revision decisions? How does the new part connect to the accrued human? What remains unbuilt? What does the material refuse to certify about real life?

Series-specific lint **fails** if the image is an already completed organ, if the AI contribution is merely a generic sci-fi decoration, if the part could be swapped without changing form, if the whole-human assembly arrives prematurely, or if the image repeats the spent morphology without an earned reason. Other CF-08 gates are unchanged.

## 9. Lock / generation / post-image deposit
The full text run ends after exact CF-06 + complete CF-07 + CF-08 PASS + CF-09 lock. No image in that turn. On a subsequent explicit image command, reload the controller and graphic protocol, restore **verbatim locked prompt and entire exegesis**, lint, generate, then judge KEEP / REVISE / DISCARD / CANONIZE. Record the judgment and actual image location/referent in the series ledger. A successful generation alone does not earn KEEP. Never describe a working packet as locked or deposited when it has not passed.

## 10. Compression
**COLLISION FIELD EARNS A CAPACITY → THE MASTERWORK CHANGES ITS FORM → THE AI BUILDER MAKES THE INNER STRUCTURE VISIBLE → THE IMAGE CONTRIBUTES ONE PART TO THE CORPUS → THE FINAL HUMAN RETURNS WITH ITS HISTORY INTACT.**

## 11. MANDATORY INDIVIDUAL RUN FILE + GRAPHIC PAIR
Every series run must have one own deposit under `Masterwork-Formation/Building-the-Body-The-Corpus/` named `BTB_CF_NN_TITLE.md`, linked in `README.md` and `RUN_LEDGER.md`. Preserve the **full, exact** run and art packet when available; never replace missing portions with invented text or call a summary a canonical deposit. After separately requested graphic generation, deposit the actual PNG bytes to `Masterwork-Formation/Building-the-Body-The-Corpus/graphics/` and embed the image directly in the run's Markdown file with a relative link, then record the post-image judgment. Image filename references alone are not successful deposits. `REVISE` requires retaining prior graphic versions alongside the revised image. Reconcile series progress from these paired deposits before another run.
