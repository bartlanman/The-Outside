# THERE ⇄ HERE — GRAPHIC MATERIAL PROTOCOL

**Status:** Working subordinate protocol — mandatory consultation, optional material deployment.
**Authority:** `Masterwork-Formation/Protocols/COLLISION_FIELD_RUN_PROTOCOL.md` controls stage order; `COLLISION_FIELD_GRAPHIC_PROTOCOL.md` controls CF-06 to judgment.
**Library:** `Masterwork-Formation/Libraries/MEDIUM_LIBRARY_THERE_HERE.md`

## Purpose
Allow a Collision Field to find material operations and inversions in the historical movement among organic, cultivated, crafted, industrial, synthetic, digital, and generated media. The purpose is not to illustrate a progress ladder or require nature-versus-machine symbolism.

## Mandatory placement
After the active field has produced its gravity, Sing, διά ⇄ σύν, and Masterwork contribution, and **before drafting CF-06**, fully read the Medium Library. This is part of the visual intake / pre-prompt material decision, not a new stage inserted into the run controller. Consult the full current library even for revisions of existing prompts. Never infer the library from memory or headings.

## Required full-library parse and material assay
1. **Read every entry** in every material family, the operations, historical trajectories, and cautions. No abbreviated shortlist in place of intake.
2. **Recover the actual field output**, not just the target word: gravity, poetic motion, διά ⇄ σύν, Masterwork formation/contribution, and existing exegesis for a revision.
3. **Scan across families** for possible collisions, including remote or counterintuitive ones. Identify more than the most obvious pairing; preserve candidate differences before narrowing.
4. **Test each candidate** for genuine field fit: What historical process produced each material? What can it actually do? What does it resist? What mark or property could not have originated there? How does this material alter the form, not just its decoration?
5. **Choose sparingly**: one strong material collision may be enough. Record selected materials, discarded obvious pairings, and why the field earned the selection.
6. **Permit no pairing** when the field does not warrant it. Full parsing is mandatory; using a THERE/HERE substitution is not.
7. **Run morphology comparison** against the live visual ledger. Reject repetition of wood-to-metal, skin-to-silicone, cyborg, or any other familiar solution when not independently earned.

Required pre-prompt output:
- **LIBRARY INTAKE:** FULL / BLOCKED
- **FIELD-TO-MATERIAL DELTA:** what material possibility emerged from this particular output
- **CANDIDATES CONSIDERED:** distinct families and their resistance
- **SELECTED / WITHHELD:** selected pairing(s), or NO MATERIAL PAIR EARNED
- **FORMAL CONSEQUENCE:** what changes in composition, material behavior, space, scale, or second reading

A partial read is **BLOCKED**. Do not claim FULL unless the entire live library was consulted.

## THERE ⇄ HERE is reversible
THERE is not always nature and HERE is not always technology. Synthetic media can acquire histories, become predecessors, or fail to replace organic ones. Do not flatten medical, biological, cultural, or historical differences into literal equivalences.

## Surreal pressure and painterly medium
Distinguish **metamorphosis** (a thing becomes another) from **impossible provenance** (a thing bears evidence of an event that could not have occurred). Neither is compulsory. Favor unresolved, materially specific contradictions over automatically harmonious transformations when the field calls for a snag. The uncanny may arise from an ordinary object in an impossible relation.

Specify the **artistic medium** separately from the **depicted materials**: oil, fresco, ink, charcoal, engraving, collage, print, textile, digital paint, or a deliberate hybrid. Do not mistake photorealistic simulation of paint texture for materially considered painting. If painterly labor is warranted, direct visible compositional decisions, marks, revision, edge handling, underlayers, and pigment behavior. Avoid generic glow and fantasy resolution when not earned.
