# COLLISION FIELD RUN PROTOCOL

**Status:** Canonical execution controller — hard contract  
**Office:** Masterwork Formation  
**Authority:** This file owns the order of operations for every full Collision Field run.  
**Invocation:** `collision field`, `run collision field`, `collision field [number/title/name]`, or clear equivalent.

---

# 0. GOVERNING LAW

A Collision Field run is not a loose collection of related operations.

It is a staged execution.

> **NO STAGE MAY BE SKIPPED, SILENTLY INFERRED, MERGED INTO ANOTHER STAGE, OR REORDERED.**

If a subordinate file conflicts with this controller, this controller governs the sequence.

Subordinate protocols govern the quality and internal method of their own stage; they do not own the run order.

Canonical run:

> **RELOAD CANON → RECOVER FIELD → CULTURAL GRAVITY DRAW → GRAVITY → POET SING → ΔΙΑ ⇄ ΣΥΝ → MASTERWORK → GRAPHIC PROMPT → EXEGESIS → LINT → PROMPT LOCK → STOP**

Image phase:

> **USER IMAGE COMMAND → RELOAD CANON → RECOVER LOCKED PROMPT VERBATIM → LINT AGAIN → GENERATE GRAPHIC → JUDGE**

---

# 0A. TARGET EMERGENCE RULE

A Collision Field does **not** require a preassigned target.

Two invocation modes are valid:

## A. EXPLICIT-TARGET INVOCATION

If the user explicitly names a field, number, title, word, object, or target, recover that target and run the field around it.

## B. OPEN-FIELD INVOCATION

If the user says only `collision field`, `run the collision field`, or another untargeted equivalent:

> **DO NOT CHOOSE A TARGET IN ADVANCE.**

Do not infer a target from:
- the immediately preceding conversation;
- the most recent Collision Field;
- the highest numbered Collision Field in the repository;
- repository recency;
- a recently discussed word, image, title, or concept;
- remembered user interest;
- whatever material is easiest to retrieve.

Instead begin with charged available material and let the Collision Field perform its own discovery through the active formation motion:

> **GATHER → COLLIDE → PROLIFERATE → TRANSFORM → NOTICE → FOLLOW → RETURN**

The field must gather across:
- remainder banks;
- open threads;
- discoveries;
- unresolved tensions;
- prior Collision Fields;
- masterwork deposits;
- The Outside / Masterwork Formation;
- TOS as a live source-field;
- Meaning Field as a live source-field;
- current cultural pressure;
- fresh contemporary outside material through current web research.

For an open-field run, TOS, Meaning Field, and the contemporary Outside are not optional enrichment. They are required masses before Gravity can be finalized.

The field must follow `Masterwork-Formation/Protocols/COLLISION_FIELD_CULTURAL_GRAVITY_PROTOCOL.md`.

The operator must preserve plurality long enough for attraction to become observable.

Only when one material, relation, question, image, contradiction, or pressure begins **pulling other material toward it without erasing their difference** may it be named as the emergent target / gravity-center of that run.

Hard law:

> **THE FIELD MAY CHOOSE ITS OWN TARGET.**

And:

> **AN UNTARGED COLLISION FIELD MUST NOT HAVE ITS TARGET SELECTED BY RECENCY, MEMORY, CONVENIENCE, OR OPERATOR PREFERENCE.**

If no gravity-center earns itself, the run may remain unresolved rather than manufacturing a target.

The target is therefore either:
1. **explicitly supplied by the user**, or
2. **earned by the field after collision begins**.

Never silently substitute one mode for the other.

# 2A. CF-01A — CULTURAL GRAVITY DRAW

Before Gravity may be named, invoke:

`Masterwork-Formation/Protocols/COLLISION_FIELD_CULTURAL_GRAVITY_PROTOCOL.md`

This stage introduces live mass from:
- TOS;
- Meaning Field;
- fresh contemporary web research;
- and permits that new material to alter, resist, redirect, or seed the recovered field.

The web is not used merely to verify or decorate an emerging thesis.

It is used to find material capable of changing the field.

The required motion includes the possibility of:
- **MAGNETO** — new mass reorganizes attraction;
- **FEEDING FLOW** — new energy extends the field into new territory;
- **INVERSE FLOW** — counterforce slows or redirects premature collapse;
- **SPIRAL ENERGY** — recurrence returns with changed capacity rather than looping;
- **SEED / NEW OTHER** — charged material admitted before its relevance is fully legible.

Output header:

> **CF-01A — CULTURAL GRAVITY DRAW**

This stage must include a visible **FIELD DELTA**: what changed because the new material entered.

If no meaningful delta occurred, continue gathering.

> **NO FIELD DELTA → NO CULTURAL GRAVITY YET.**

If the web is unavailable or explicitly forbidden, mark this stage BLOCKED and do not claim current cultural gravity.

# 3. CF-02 — GRAVITY

Identify the pressure that is pulling the recovered field together.

Gravity is not:
- a summary;
- a doctrine;
- a theme label;
- a thesis imposed before the field speaks.

Gravity is the live attraction / pressure that makes the materials begin to organize without erasing their difference.

Gravity must be downstream of CF-01A.

It is not admissible merely because internal project material coheres.

Before accepting Gravity, verify:
- TOS altered or charged the field;
- Meaning Field altered or charged the field;
- fresh contemporary Outside material entered;
- at least one outside mass resisted, redirected, or remained partly untranslated;
- at least one seed of the new Other was admitted;
- a FIELD DELTA is visible.

If those conditions are absent, return to CF-01A.

Output header:

> **CF-02 — GRAVITY**

# 4. CF-03 — POET SING

The Aeolian Poet enters as a tensioned instrument.

This stage must follow `FOLLOW_AEOLIAN_POET.md`.

The Sing is not:
- an explanation;
- a summary;
- an essay;
- a chapter by default;
- narrative fiction by default;
- a decorative paragraph;
- a proof of a predetermined title.

The poet sounds the field.

The poet creates adjacency rather than forcing synthesis.

The Sing must be substantial enough for the field's pressure to become audible, but its length and form are earned by the field.

> **SING ≠ MASTERWORK BY DEFINITION.**

The Sing is the sounding operation.

It may later be judged sufficient to stand as the Masterwork, but that judgment belongs to CF-05.

Output header:

> **CF-03 — POET SING**

# 5. CF-04 — ΔΙΑ ⇄ ΣΥΝ

Only after the Sing exists, listen back through:

# **διά ⇄ σύν**

- what passes through?
- what gathers?
- what becomes possible only through passage?
- what becomes perceptible only because something temporarily holds?
- where does holding become constraint?
- where does passage become dispersal?
- what relation was audible only after the poet sounded the field?

Do not apply διά ⇄ σύν as a prefabricated doctrine.

Read it from the completed Sing.

If a name/title emerges, it must be earned here rather than imposed upstream.

Output header:

> **CF-04 — ΔΙΑ ⇄ ΣΥΝ**

Now invoke:

`Masterwork-Formation/Protocols/MASTERWORK_CREATION_PROTOCOL.md`

The system must decide what form, if any, the field now warrants.

> **DO NOT DEFAULT TO A NOVEL, CHAPTER, ESSAY, POEM, SCRIPT, OR ANY OTHER PRESELECTED FORM.**

- the Sing itself is sufficient and becomes the Masterwork;
- the Sing becomes the seed of a different work;
- a chapter is warranted;
- a short lyric, dramatic scene, sequence, score, hybrid, visual-text object, or other form is warranted;
- no additional literary artifact is warranted yet.

Never manufacture a second artifact merely because the checklist contains a Masterwork stage.

Output header:

> **CF-05 — MASTERWORK**

The stage must state:

> **MASTERWORK FORM:** [earned form]

and then create only what is warranted.

---

# 9. CF-08 — LINT

Run the complete lint defined by the graphic protocol.

This lint must be visible.

Output exactly one of:

> **CF-08 — LINT: PASS**

or

> **CF-08 — LINT: FAIL**

If FAIL:
- state the failure;
- revise CF-06;
- update CF-07 if the revision changes the intended reading;
- lint again.

Do not proceed until PASS.

---

# 10. CF-09 — PROMPT LOCK / STOP

After PASS, freeze the exact generation prompt.

Output:

> **CF-09 — GRAPHIC PROMPT LOCKED**

The locked prompt is immutable unless the user explicitly asks to revise it.

Do not paraphrase it later.
Do not "improve" it at generation time.
Do not reconstruct it from memory.

Then:

> **STOP.**

No image generation occurs in the same turn as the full textual run.

---

# 11. IMAGE PHASE

The image phase begins only after an explicit user command such as:
- image;
- graphic;
- create it;
- generate it.

Before generation:

1. re-read this controller;
2. re-read `COLLISION_FIELD_GRAPHIC_PROTOCOL.md`;
3. recover the exact locked prompt from CF-09;
4. run the graphic lint again;
5. if unchanged and PASS, generate from the locked prompt.

No new concepts may be introduced between lock and generation.

---

# 12. POST-GENERATION JUDGMENT

After generation, judge the image against the locked prompt and graphic protocol.

Allowed judgments:

> **KEEP**  
> **REVISE**  
> **DISCARD**  
> **CANONIZE**

Generation does not equal success.

A protocol violation must not be quietly accepted.
