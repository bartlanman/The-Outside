## 0. GOVERNING LAW

**THE TATTOO'S LOCATION IS NOT DECORATIVE. THE BODY MUST ENACT THE SYMBOL.**

In a sevenfold Craft Sublime Collision Field, a tattoo is a specific location on a specific body whose motion, pressure, breath, friction, posture, and vulnerability become available to the event. The symbol is not sufficient by appearing, glowing, or decorating the character.

**BODY GEOGRAPHY → CONTEXTUAL PRESSURE → EMBODIED ACTION → BOUNDARY TRANSFORMATION → THERE/HERE SYMBOL BECOMING → RELATIONAL CONSEQUENCE → POSSIBLE UNIFIED CRAFT SUBLIME.**

For each *actually participating* character, the story must show a motivated physical act, its consequence for another participant, and the manner in which the inherited tattoo constructs its assigned contemporary emblem component. If a needed contribution is missing, record NOT YET ENACTED / HOLD rather than inventing it.

This protocol adds a specialized narrative enactment within CF-02A and CF-02B. It never replaces the full Collision Field controller, independent DOS draw, CF-03 compressed Sing, CF-05 creative work, or the later image phase.

**Active character source:** Elias = CRAFT; Tessa = SUBLIME; Elias ⇄ Tessa is the primary Craft Sublime relationship. Eleanor = Living Ledger custodian, not Tessa's replacement. Other characters keep their independent histories and agency.

## 2. CF-02A — THE EIGHT-POINT EMBODIED-GEOGRAPHY CHECK

For each consequential participant, record AND make narratively perceptible:

1. **ANATOMICAL LOCATION** — Exact body zone and front/back/paired orientation from the character map.
2. **LOCAL CAPACITY** — Plausible movement, exertion, breath, resonance, weight shift, touch or sensation involving that anatomical zone.
3. **CONTEXTUAL TRIGGER** — What specifically happens in *this Collision Field* that requires this movement?
4. **EMBODIED ACTION** — What does the character do or refuse to do? An observable choice, not a symbol recitation.
5. **BOUNDARY CONSEQUENCE** — What pressure, friction, reciprocal slide, permeability, adhesion, hemifusion, recoil or changed relation results, and whose action caused it?
6. **THERE → HERE** — Which lines of the inherited tattoo become WHICH structural lines of its unique Craft Sublime emblem part? Is this bodily ink, felt motion, projected design, crafted inscription, or another explicitly established narrative representation?
7. **RELATIONAL CONTRIBUTION** — What does another participant newly perceive, do, receive, risk, or refuse because of this bodily act?
8. **UNIFIED CONTRIBUTION** — How could that earned change enter the shared architecture? Mark ACTUAL / PARTIAL / HOLD; do not claim fusion by default.

**Boundary Narrative:** Write the actual close-range scene. Perceive distinctive surfaces and minute actions, not simply an explanatory table. A tattoo need not be unveiled: posture, a covered torso's breath, muscular effort, or the bearing of weight can enact its geography. In the next sentence, show a second participant responding so that the act changes the encounter.

**CF-02A question:** What changed at the shared interface, and what remains separately enclosed?

## 5. CF-02B — DOS MOMENT / EMBODIED RELEASE

DOS continues from the **specific boundary condition earned in CF-02A**. It does not arrive as a deus ex machina that strips all living or ethical boundaries.

For each participating character, stage:
- Which previous enclosure/role claims inappropriate authority?
- How does that particular anatomical region participate in the pressure of release?
- What voluntary action, assent, resistance or refusal alters the interface?
- What has actually transformed: selective passage, local outer joining, new interior pore, shared enclosure, or mere rupture?
- What happens to the recognizable ancient motif in its HERE form, and how does this change another participant's capacity?
- What origins, identities, records and permissions remain legitimately distinct?

**DOS Narrative:** Show the concrete release in the *same ongoing scene* as the Boundary Narrative, then return to the bodies and show the first behavior available only under the new arrangement. If genuine fusion does not occur, narrate recoil/resealing/separation and the newly possible relation that remains. Keep characters identifiable; the unified emblem is a working architecture, never a compulsory offspring, person, or relationship.

**CF-02B question:** What can the new togetherness now DO, RECEIVE, MAKE, EXPERIENCE or RISK that it previously could not? If no fusion, what became possible through the difference that remained?
