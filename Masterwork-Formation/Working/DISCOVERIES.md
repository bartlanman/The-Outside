# Working Discoveries

## 001 — Evidentiary survivability
A masterwork dossier must record how much of the making process can actually be recovered.

Unequal archives must not become unequal certainty.

Origin: Dante pilot.

## 002 — Architecture as cognition
A work can make structure epistemically active by forcing the participant to move through positions before later relations become visible.

Origin: Dante pilot.

## 003 — Historical-equivalent creation
The target is not surface modernization.

The research must distinguish:

SURFACE
-> TECHNIQUE
-> FUNCTION
-> CONTEMPORARY STRUCTURAL EQUIVALENT.

Origin: method development before Dante; reinforced by Dante pilot.


## 004 — The form can discover its own form
A work may begin with one intended genre or structure and, through repeated encounter with its material, become another kind of work.

Origin: Proust pilot.

Caution:
Do not romanticize drift. The distinction between fruitful emergence and undisciplined accumulation must remain testable.

## 005 — Archive abundance can reveal instability rather than certainty
A rich manuscript archive does not necessarily clarify a single authorial intention. It can reveal competing openings, revisions, deferred decisions, reused material, and changing architecture.

Origin: Proust pilot.

## 006 — External interruption can become internal form
A work can be materially changed by historical events the author did not create.

Origin: Proust / World War I.

Possible relevance:
A strong work may survive the Outside not by resisting change, but by revising without losing identity.
