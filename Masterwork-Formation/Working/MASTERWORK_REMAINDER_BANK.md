# Masterwork Remainder Bank

## Purpose

This file holds charged material that is not yet selected, resolved, or promoted.

Nothing here is doctrine.

Nothing here must become useful immediately.

The purpose is to prevent artistic excess from being deleted merely because it does not fit the current candidate form.

Working law:

> **TODAY'S EXCESS MAY BE TOMORROW'S FORM.**

## Handling Rule

Do not:
- rank;
- score;
- eliminate;
- convert immediately into requirements.

Do:
- revisit;
- collide;
- annotate when another field unexpectedly touches one;
- preserve contradictions;
- record when one item begins attracting unrelated material.

When an item begins exerting repeated cross-domain gravity, move it into a **Follow** document without deleting it here.
