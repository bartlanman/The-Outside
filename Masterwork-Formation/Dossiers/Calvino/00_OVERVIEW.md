# Calvino — Overview

## Unit of analysis

Primary work:
Italo Calvino, *If on a winter's night a traveler* (1979).

Supporting theoretical texts and contexts:
- "Cybernetics and Ghosts" (1967)
- "The Written World and the Unwritten World" (1983)
- Oulipo
- Calvino's late interviews on structure, constraint, and the reader

Secondary comparison texts where useful:
- *The Castle of Crossed Destinies*
- *Invisible Cities*
- *Mr. Palomar*

## Existing role in The Outside constellation

- Calvino as primary masterwork of TRAVERSE
- reader enters the labyrinth
- authorship as design of participation
- formal precedent for a work whose operation cannot be completed through passive reading alone

## Spine question

Can an author design a work that genuinely requires reader participation without merely scripting the reader's apparent freedom in advance?

## Why Calvino matters after Borges

Borges destabilizes:
SOURCE
AUTHOR
TEXT
SYSTEM
INTERPRETATION.

Calvino turns destabilization into an experiential device.

The Reader does not merely contemplate recursion.

The Reader is made into a character whose effort to read becomes the plot.

This creates a new authorship problem:

DESIGNING CONDITIONS FOR PARTICIPATION.
