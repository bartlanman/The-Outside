## Evidence cautions

Do not state:
- that Middle-earth existed fully before Tolkien wrote the story;
- that Tolkien planned every detail in advance;
- that deep lore automatically produces a world;
- that the Ring is the atomic bomb;
- that *The Scouring of the Shire* is a one-to-one postwar allegory;
- that invented languages alone create narrative depth;
- that a world literally becomes independent of its author.

Distinguish:

WORLD DEPTH
from
LORE QUANTITY.

Distinguish:

SUB-CREATION
from
POSSESSION.

Distinguish:

PARTICIPANT IMPORTANCE
from
PARTICIPANT CENTRALITY.

Distinguish:

RETURN
from
RESTORATION.
