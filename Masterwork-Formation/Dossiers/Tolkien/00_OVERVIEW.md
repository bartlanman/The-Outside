# Tolkien — Overview

## Unit of analysis

Primary work:

Supporting formation field:
- *The Hobbit*
- *The Silmarillion* / legendarium
- invented languages
- maps and chronologies
- "On Fairy-Stories"
- selected letters
- the posthumous *History of Middle-earth* materials edited by Christopher Tolkien

## Existing role in The Outside constellation

- Tolkien as witness for WORLD
- a world that precedes and exceeds the participant
- return that does not restore the participant to the same home
- participant not identical with protagonist-center or author-center
- stewardship and relinquishment after heroic action

## Spine question

How does an author create a world with enough independent depth that the protagonist travels through only a small part of it, the reader senses histories that exceed the plot, and return reveals that home continued changing without the traveler?

## Why Tolkien matters after Hesse

Hesse gives us:

SYSTEM
-> MASTERY
-> HERMETIC RISK
-> EXIT.

Tolkien gives us something different:

WORLD
-> PARTICIPANT
-> JOURNEY
-> RETURN
-> WORLD CONTINUES.

Middle-earth is not primarily a system the protagonist must leave.

It is a world the protagonist never fully possesses.
