## Evidence cautions

Do not state:
- that Castalia is simply Hesse's utopia;
- that Castalia is merely a coded portrait of Nazi Germany;
- that Hesse rejects intellectual or monastic life;
- that the Glass Bead Game has a fully recoverable rule system;
- that Knecht's resignation supplies a complete political or educational program;
- that leaving Castalia guarantees authentic life;
- that every non-Castalian tradition functions only as a symbol of "the Outside."

Distinguish:

VALUE OF THE GAME
from
SUFFICIENCY OF THE GAME.

Distinguish:

PRESERVATION OF CULTURE
from
SELF-PRESERVATION OF THE INSTITUTION.

Distinguish:

REPRESENTING ANOTHER TRADITION
from
CONTAINING IT.

Distinguish:

EXIT
from
MASTERY OF WHAT LIES OUTSIDE.
