# Proust — Overview

## Work
Marcel Proust, *À la recherche du temps perdu* / *In Search of Lost Time*

## Existing role in The Outside constellation
- Primary masterwork of TRAJECTORY
- Major witness in the genealogy of authorship: retrospective recognition
- Important case for memory, return, altered perception, and later positions changing earlier visibility

## Pilot question
What conditions allowed Proust to turn lived time, remembered time, social observation, literary inheritance, and repeated revision into a form in which later experience changes what earlier experience can mean?

## Why Proust is the second pilot
Dante tested a low-survivability archive: almost no direct draft history survives.

Proust is the opposite.

The surviving archive includes notebooks, cahiers, typescripts, corrected galleys, proofs, paperoles, correspondence, and abandoned or transformed material. The dossier can therefore examine actual compositional development rather than infer decisions only from the finished work.

## Working hypothesis
Proust's transferable operation may not be:

WRITE LONG SENTENCES ABOUT MEMORY.

It may be:

ALLOW EXPERIENCE TO REMAIN UNFINISHED UNTIL LATER POSITIONS MAKE RELATIONS VISIBLE THAT THE EARLIER SELF COULD NOT YET SEE.

A second, compositional version may be:

LET THE WORK KEEP CHANGING LONG ENOUGH FOR ITS FORM TO DISCOVER WHAT ITS MATERIAL HAS BECOME.
