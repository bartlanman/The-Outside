## Evidence cautions

Do not state as fact:
- that every major role was written specifically for Burbage;
- that a surviving quarto or Folio always represents Shakespeare's final intention;
- that every textual difference is authorial revision;
- that Hand D is certainly Shakespeare;
- that every play is solely authored by Shakespeare;
- that characters possess literal independence from their author.

Distinguish:
TEXTUAL AUTHORSHIP
from
THEATRICAL REALIZATION
from
COLLABORATION
from
TEXTUAL TRANSMISSION
from
LATER EDITORIAL STABILIZATION.
