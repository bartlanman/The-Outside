## Evidence cautions

### The 1938 accident story
Borges later framed "Pierre Menard" as a test of whether he could still write after serious illness.

Do not state that the accident literally created Borges's first fiction. He had written narrative prose before it.

### Political allegory
Do not reduce "Tlön" or "The Library of Babel" to one political allegory.

Historical pressure may become formal pressure without creating one-to-one symbolism.

### Fake references
When analyzing Borges's sources, verify whether citations are:
- real
- altered
- misquoted
- invented
- composite

Do not assume bibliographic appearance equals historical existence. That mistake would reproduce the very operation under study.
