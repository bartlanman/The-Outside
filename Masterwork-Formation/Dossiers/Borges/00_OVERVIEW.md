# Borges — Overview

## Primary corpus
Jorge Luis Borges, selected fictions centered on *Ficciones* and its immediate precursors:
- "Pierre Menard, autor del Quijote"
- "Tlön, Uqbar, Orbis Tertius"
- "La biblioteca de Babel"
- "El jardín de senderos que se bifurcan"
- "Las ruinas circulares"
- related essay-fiction and later revisions

## Existing role in The Outside constellation
- necessary counter-masterwork beside Calvino at TRAVERSE
- epistemic shadow of FIELD
- warning against hermetic systems
- witness to destabilized authorship
- permanent Guardian against self-certifying architecture

## Pilot question
What conditions led Borges to build fictions in which books, authors, archives, citations, worlds, and readers become mutually unstable — and what formal decisions make that instability epistemically productive rather than merely clever?

## Why Borges is the third pilot
Dante tests strong architecture.
Proust tests open growth through revision and return.
Borges tests whether architecture, archive, and interpretation can begin producing the evidence by which they justify themselves.

## Working hypothesis
Borges's transferable operation is not:

WRITE LABYRINTHS, LIBRARIES, MIRRORS, AND FAKE SCHOLARSHIP.

It may be:

BUILD A SYSTEM COHERENT ENOUGH TO SEDUCE THE READER INTO BELIEF, THEN REVEAL THAT COHERENCE ITSELF DOES NOT WARRANT THE SYSTEM'S CLAIM.

A second formulation:

MAKE THE READER FEEL HOW EASILY AUTHORITY CAN BE MANUFACTURED BY FORM.
