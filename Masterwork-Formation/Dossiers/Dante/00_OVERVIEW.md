# Dante — Overview

Primary work: The Divine Comedy

Existing roles in The Outside:
- analogue for The Great Ledger
- alternative model of Trajectory
- example of authorship as architecture

Pilot question:

What conditions made Dante's particular act of architectural authorship necessary, and which parts of that act remain transferable after his medieval cosmology is gone?

Working hypothesis to test, not assume:

Dante's most transferable operation may not be BUILD A COMPLETE WORLD.

It may be:

MAKE STRUCTURE SOMETHING THE PARTICIPANT MUST MOVE THROUGH IN ORDER TO KNOW WHAT THE STRUCTURE MEANS.

Current compression:

INHERITANCE
-> multiple authorities and traditions

PRESSURE
-> political fracture + exile + intellectual inheritance + vernacular ambition

PROBLEM
-> how can fragmented human existence become intelligible within an order larger than its immediate position?

DECISION
-> do not merely explain the order; make a traveler traverse it

FORM
-> world + journey + position + consequence + retrospective narration

AUTHORIAL OFFICE
-> ARCHITECT, but an architect who also places a version of himself inside the architecture

RISK
-> architecture becomes totalization

SURVIVING OPERATION
-> POSITION -> TRAVERSE -> CHANGED CAPACITY -> RECOGNITION

CONTEMPORARY QUESTION
-> can we build an architecture powerful enough to create passage without claiming the right to close the cosmos?
