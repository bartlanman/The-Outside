# Eliot — Read

## The central authorship finding

Eliot gives us a form of authorship that is easy to underestimate because modern culture still privileges origination.

But *The Waste Land* demonstrates:

TO SELECT
IS TO AUTHOR.

TO CUT
IS TO AUTHOR.

TO JUXTAPOSE
IS TO AUTHOR.

TO LEAVE UNTRANSLATED
IS TO AUTHOR.

TO MAKE ONE FRAGMENT ANSWER ANOTHER
IS TO AUTHOR.

The work is not original because its materials lack predecessors.

It is original because of the relations produced among inherited materials.

## Pound makes the point unavoidable

The manuscript record prevents us from telling a simple heroic-author story.

Pound removed large amounts of material.

Eliot accepted much of the intervention.

The final poem is therefore not merely Eliot expressing himself.

It is Eliot allowing his work to be altered by another intelligence.

That yields a powerful Participating Author precedent:

AUTHORSHIP CAN INCLUDE
THE DISCIPLINE OF LETTING ANOTHER PERSON
CHANGE WHAT YOU THOUGHT THE WORK WAS.

This differs from distributed origination.

Eliot remains primary author.

But primary authorship is not absolute sovereignty.

## The editor as Actual Other

Pound has actual standing because he can say:

NO.

CUT THIS.

THIS DOES NOT BELONG.

And Eliot can accept the refusal.

That is very close to our Guardian / Outside architecture.

The editor does not merely validate.

The editor applies counter-pressure.

## Eliot versus the Biblical corpus

Biblical corpus formation:
LOCAL WORKS
-> DURABLE RELATION.

Eliot:
INHERITED FRAGMENTS
-> EXTRACTION
-> NEW RELATION.

The first preserves local textual wholes.

The second creates a new whole partly by cutting material away from earlier wholes.

So curation has greater intervention.

That raises greater responsibility.

## Eliot versus Whitman

Whitman risks:
I CONTAIN THE MANY.

Eliot risks:
I SELECT THE TRADITION.

Whitman's shadow is absorption.

Eliot's shadow is canonical sovereignty.

Both can appear inclusive while preserving central authorial power.

This is a useful pair.

## "Tradition" is itself authored

Eliot's famous argument is more radical than "read old books."

A new work changes how previous works stand in relation.

That means tradition is not merely inherited.

It is continually reconstructed by succession.

But there is a dangerous reciprocal truth:

THE PERSON WHO DEFINES "TRADITION"
HAS POWER OVER WHAT COUNTS AS THE PAST.

So "Tradition and the Individual Talent" gives us both:
- a powerful relational theory,
- and a warning about canon-making authority.

## The Notes reveal the provenance problem

The Notes encourage readers to follow sources.

That can be useful.

But it can produce an illusion:

IF I HAVE FOUND THE SOURCE,
I HAVE SOLVED THE POEM.

No.

Source is provenance.

Meaning lies in:
SOURCE
+
NEW CONTEXT
+
RELATION
+
RECEIVER.

This distinction is going to matter enormously in AI systems that can produce citations instantly.

## The future reopens the workshop

The 1971 publication of the drafts changes *The Waste Land* again.

Readers can now see:
- what was cut,
- where Pound intervened,
- how large the possible poem once was,
- how constructed the final compression really is.

So the deposit has layers:

POEM
then
FORMATION RECORD.

The future therefore gains access to the remainder of authorship.

That is important.

A masterwork may leave behind not only its finished deposit but its excluded possibilities.

## Provisional office

CURATOR-COMPOSER.

The good version:
selects inherited materials and creates warranted new relation.

The failure-state:
CANON-MAKER WHO HIDES THE FACT OF SELECTION.

The seam is:

CURATION
<->
CANONICAL SOVEREIGNTY.

## Contemporary pressure

Generative AI makes Eliot newly relevant.

Generation is cheap.

Retrieval is cheap.

Quotation is cheap.

Stylistic recombination is cheap.

Therefore the scarce authorial act may increasingly be:

SELECTION
+
RELATION
+
REFUSAL
+
ACCOUNTABILITY FOR THE CORPUS IMPLIED BY THOSE CHOICES.

This pushes Participating Authorship away from "who generated the sentences?"

toward:

WHO DECIDED WHAT DESERVED TO STAND TOGETHER?

WHO ACCEPTED THE CUT?

WHO PRESERVED THE SOURCE'S DIFFERENCE?

WHO IS ANSWERABLE FOR THE RELATION?

## Working extraction

ELIOT:

INHERITANCE
-> FRAGMENT
-> CURATION
-> EDITORIAL REFUSAL
-> JUXTAPOSITION
-> RELATIONAL FIELD
-> READER RECONSTRUCTION
-> REWEIGHTED TRADITION.

AUTHORSHIP FINDING:

THE AUTHOR MAY BE MOST AUTHORIAL
NOT WHEN ORIGINATING THE MOST MATERIAL,
BUT WHEN TAKING RESPONSIBILITY
FOR WHICH RELATIONS SURVIVE.
