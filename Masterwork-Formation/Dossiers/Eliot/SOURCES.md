## Evidence cautions

Do not state:
- that Pound "wrote" *The Waste Land*;
- that Eliot's breakdown explains the poem;
- that every allusion has one fixed function;
- that the Notes provide a complete key;
- that Eliot's "tradition" is a neutral or universal canon;
- that impersonality removes authorial selection power.

Distinguish:
SOURCE
from
NEW RELATION.

Distinguish:
EDITORIAL COLLABORATION
from
COAUTHORSHIP.

Distinguish:
PROVENANCE
from
INTERPRETATION.
