## Evidence cautions

Do not state:
- that Le Guin literally discovered objective facts about an independently existing Earthsea;
- that later Earthsea simply corrects false earlier books;
- that feminism alone explains the later novels;
- that every late revision is automatically superior;
- that Roke's later backstory was secretly planned in 1968;
- that authorial change automatically counts as progress.

Distinguish:

REVISION
from
ERASURE.

Distinguish:

NEW POSITION
from
RETROACTIVE CLAIM THAT THE OLD POSITION NEVER EXISTED.

Distinguish:

DEEPER HISTORY
from
ARBITRARY RETCON.

Distinguish:

AUTHORIAL FALLIBILITY
from
AUTHORIAL ABDICATION.
