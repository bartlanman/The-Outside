## Competing / qualifying view

### Jerome Moran, "The Composition and Transmission of the Homeric Poems: A Summary"
*Journal of Classics Teaching*.

Use:
- oral hypothesis
- composition / textualization summary
- argument that overall unity points toward a major individual poet for each surviving epic rather than indefinite multiple successive authorship

This is important because the dossier must not confuse "oral tradition" with a settled claim of collective authorship.

Tenth-century manuscript.

Important for scholia and ancient textual scholarship.

## Evidence cautions

Do not state as settled:
- the identity or biography of Homer,
- exact dating of a singular composition event,
- exact mechanism of first writing,
- a simple Peisistratean "recension" that created the epics,
- pure collective authorship,
- pure singular authorship independent of tradition.

Preserve the distinction between:
ORAL-TRADITIONAL BACKGROUND — strongly supported
and
SPECIFIC AUTHORSHIP MODEL — still contested.
