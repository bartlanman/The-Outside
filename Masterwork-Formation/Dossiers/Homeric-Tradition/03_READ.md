# Homeric Tradition — Read

## The hidden assumption Homer breaks

The first three dossiers still assumed something like:

AUTHOR
-> WORK
-> READER.

Homer breaks this.

A more historically plausible model is:

TRADITION
-> PERFORMER
-> PERFORMANCE / COMPOSITION
-> AUDIENCE
-> REPERFORMANCE
-> TEXTUALIZATION
-> EDITOR / TRANSMITTER
-> READER.

The important point is not that "there was no Homer."

The evidence does not entitle us to solve that question so simply.

The important point is:

THE MASTERWORK WE HAVE CANNOT BE EXPLAINED ONLY BY A PRIVATE AUTHORIAL ACT WE CAN RECOVER.

That changes the authorship-spine project.

## Carrying is creative

Transmission sounds passive in a modern textual model.

But oral tradition complicates it.

If performance can involve recomposition, then carrying the work forward requires active making.

The singer receives:
- story,
- formula,
- meter,
- scenes,
- names,
- inherited meanings.

The singer does not merely copy them.

He makes a present performance through them.

This gives us a stronger authorship equation:

AUTHORSHIP
!=
ORIGINATION.

It may sometimes be:

RESPONSIBLE TRANSFORMATION OF INHERITANCE.

That feels highly relevant to AI-era authorship.

## Tradition as a condition, not merely a source

Dante had Virgil.

Borges had libraries.

Proust had Ruskin and French literature.

But in the Homeric case the inheritance is not simply "material consulted before writing."

The tradition is the medium in which composition is possible.

That distinction matters.

SOURCE
is something you draw from.

GENERATIVE TRADITION
is something that partly supplies the grammar of making.

This may become an important column later.

## Receiver enters earlier than expected

The most important methodological discovery is that reception may occur during formation.

An oral performer makes for a present audience.

Audience competence matters because formula, traditional reference, character, story, and variation are meaningful partly through recognition.

So the old linear sequence:

MAKE
-> DEPOSIT
-> RECEIVE

does not always hold.

Here it can be:

RECEIVE
-> MAKE
-> RECEIVE
-> REMAKE.

That is a major correction.

## Deposit becomes iterative

There is no obvious singular moment corresponding to:
"the author finished the manuscript."

The tradition survives through repeated deposits:
- memory,
- performance,
- reperformance,
- transcript,
- script,
- edited text,
- manuscript,
- edition.

Therefore:

DEPOSIT
MAY BE
A RECURRING OPERATION.

This may connect strongly to our Succeeding Ledger and future-reader work.

## Identity without fixity

The deepest formal problem may be:

HOW DOES SOMETHING REMAIN ITSELF WHILE CHANGING?

That is not only a Homeric textual question.

It is also a living-system question.

Too much fixity:
tradition becomes dead repetition.

Too much variation:
identity dissolves.

The seam is:

IDENTITY
<-> VARIATION.

We should not yet call this an earned masterwork law.

But it is likely to matter.

## Authorial office

"CARRIER" is correct but insufficient.

The stronger provisional office is:

CARRIER-COMPOSER.

The carrier does not claim to originate the inheritance.

But the carrier is responsible for the form it takes now.

That distinction may be one of the strongest pieces of the Participating Author we have yet found.

## Contemporary relevance

Generative AI makes the old fantasy of solitary origination increasingly difficult to maintain.

A contemporary work may include:
- inherited human language,
- prior literature,
- AI generation,
- research sources,
- collaborators,
- audience response,
- revisions,
- external evidence.

The Homeric lesson is not:
"therefore nobody authors anything."

It is almost the opposite.

When origination is distributed, responsibility must become more precise.

The question shifts from:

WHO CREATED EVERY ELEMENT?

to:

WHO WAS ANSWERABLE FOR WHAT WAS RECEIVED, RECOMPOSED, ADMITTED, ALTERED, AND DEPOSITED?

That is Participating Authorship territory.
