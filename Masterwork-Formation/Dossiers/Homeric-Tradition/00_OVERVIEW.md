# Homeric Tradition — Overview

## Unit of analysis

Primary works:
- *Iliad*
- *Odyssey*

But the dossier cannot begin with "Homer the individual author" as though that identity were securely recoverable.

The actual unit of analysis is:

HOMERIC TRADITION
+
THE ILIAD AND ODYSSEY AS CRYSTALLIZED MASTERWORKS.

## Existing role in The Outside constellation

- *Odyssey* as primary analogue for the Turning Ledger
- Homeric tradition as authorship-as-carrying / transmission
- major precedent for a work with strong formal identity whose origins exceed the modern model of private individual authorship

## Spine question

How can a masterwork possess durable formal identity when composition, performance, reception, transmission, and textual stabilization are distributed across a tradition rather than securely attributable to one recoverable author's private act?

## Why this case matters

Dante, Proust, and Borges still allowed the research instrument to center a historically identifiable maker.

Homer does not.

The surviving evidence strongly supports an oral-traditional background, but major questions remain contested:
- whether one exceptional poet gave each epic its final large-scale unity,
- how composition-in-performance interacted with writing,
- when written versions emerged,
- how fluid oral multiforms became increasingly stabilized texts.

The dossier must preserve this uncertainty rather than solve the Homeric Question for convenience.
