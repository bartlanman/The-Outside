## Evidence cautions

Do not state as settled:
- one universally accepted compositional model for the Pentateuch;
- one early universal Jewish canon;
- one single council that simply "created the Bible";
- one identical Christian Old Testament canon across all traditions;
- that canon formation and text composition are the same process;
- that later canonical status reveals original authorial intention;
- that every textual difference is deliberate redaction.

Always distinguish:
TEXT COMPOSITION
from
TEXTUAL TRANSMISSION
from
CANON FORMATION.
