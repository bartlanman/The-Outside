# Whitman — Overview

## Unit of analysis

Primary work:
Walt Whitman, *Leaves of Grass* across its lifetime of revision.

Key internal test sites:
- 1855 *Leaves of Grass*
- "Song of Myself"
- "I Sing the Body Electric"
- "The Sleepers"
- "Crossing Brooklyn Ferry"
- "Calamus"
- "Children of Adam"
- *Drum-Taps* and its later absorption into *Leaves*
- later reorganizations through the 1881–82 and 1891–92 forms

The unit cannot be one edition only because Whitman treated *Leaves of Grass* as an evolving lifelong work.

## Existing role in The Outside constellation

- Whitman as primary witness for the Human Great Ledger
- authorship as permeability
- self as porous to bodies, occupations, places, history, strangers, sexuality, democracy, and future readers
- known danger: permeability becoming absorption

## Spine question

How can the authorial "I" become large enough to enter relation with many others without turning those others into extensions, possessions, or symbols of the self?

## Why Whitman matters after Shakespeare

Shakespeare distributes local standing across separate dramatic voices.

Whitman takes the opposite risk:

HE EXPANDS THE AUTHORIAL SELF UNTIL IT ATTEMPTS TO INCLUDE THE MANY.

This tests another authorship mode:

not release of other voices,
but permeability of the speaking self.
