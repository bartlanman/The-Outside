## Evidence cautions

Do not equate:
- poetic "I" with historical Walt Whitman,
- symbolic inclusion with political equality,
- identification with independent standing,
- catalogue with genuine plurality,
- Whitman's preferred final edition with the only legitimate form of *Leaves*.

Do not treat Whitman's contradictions as external embarrassment to be removed from the poetics.

They are evidence for the central formation problem:

PERMEABILITY CAN BECOME ABSORPTION.
