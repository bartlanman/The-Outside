## 2. CURRENT MILIEU PRESSURE — THE AVAILABLE CAPACITY AND ITS COST

In the current digital environment, producing, replaying, exporting, binding, displaying and relocating very similar output is cheap. It is common for software interfaces to be the only place the user could have observed a generation event, and those interfaces can disconnect or lose visibility. Here a human deliberately retained a first copy outside the glitched/retried process. That **human act of preservation** provides real historical standing neither a model simulation nor a dramatic character supplies.

Specific present capacity: independently identify preserved **objects** via bytes and metadata; compare their textual surfaces; bring the two originals into one reading encounter; version the reader's later response. Specific danger: falsely equate object identity, textual identity, performance identity, and delivery identity.

Independent cultural pressure already admitted at G1/G3: archival provenance uses Object/Event distinctions; at-least-once/retry systems allow duplicated arrival; contemporary archival mediation can hide events even when artifacts remain visible. These are analogies and reference disciplines, **not forensic claims about the ChatGPT execution system**.

**REAL BALLAST:** user-reported experience, two actually preserved different file containers, different byte hashes, larger book context, no independent event log available in this record.

**UNREAL CAPACITY:** the missed hammer, the blank measure, the paper-on-paper impossible register of a sound that nobody can count. Make the imaginary matter intense without asking it to testify.

## 5. FORMAL PROLIFERATION — EIGHT MUTATIONS, NOT EIGHT GENRES

**M1 — NONMERGING SOURCE POLYPHONY.** Two source-bearing surfaces coexist under separate documentary custody. Reorder reading without amalgamating an “authoritative” corrected copy. *Source strength:* true double witness. *Weakness:* a comparative archive, not yet felt art.

**M2 — THE UNSTRUCK COUNTER-MEASURE.** A present interval has an expected second attack but none is given; the receiver experiences the temptation to count while the work provides no second blow. **Removes prose entirely** and can take the form of breath, silence, spacing, or performed timing. *Strength:* felt non-arrival. *Weakness:* too close to **THE BELL AFTER THE HAND**, Beckett, or a banal blank page if independent witness pressure disappears.

**M3 — REVERSIBLE FIRST ENCOUNTER.** The receiver chooses whether to meet the standalone saved run or the later illustrated book first, with materially different initial context but neither privileged as “the true original” simply by sequence. **Live/variable realization**; immutable source exhibits. *Strength:* perception changes without changed documents. *Weakness:* familiar Proust/Calvino double reading alone.

**M4 — THE CAUSALLY BLIND THIRD REGISTER.** A third register marks only what remains unestablished about production: not “a third voice,” “a third Collision Field,” or another supposedly real event. It can remain empty. *Strength:* object/event discipline changes how earlier two sources are received. *Weakness:* could become a bureaucratic evidence table or fake profundity from missing data.

**M5 — THE NONCOERCIVE OUTSIDE SEAM.** The work contains a boundary it cannot author across: later independently authenticated generation logs or material testimony may change what is warranted; otherwise the seam remains unfilled. **Depends on Outside contact** but accepts non-arrival. *Strength:* cannot certify its own mystery. *Weakness:* work might improperly defer its artistic completion until evidence arrives.

**M6 — PARTICIPANT CUSTODY WITHOUT EVENT SOVEREIGNTY.** A receiver can leave a personal, attributable observation and decide what they will preserve; no act of clicking, listening, marking or voting may manufacture a causal event in the originals. **Changes the receiver's office** from passive interpreter to bounded, answerable co-witness. *Strength:* human act of rescue/return matters. *Weakness:* could reduce to instructional interactivity.

**M7 — THE SELF-RETRACTING PERFORMANCE CLAIM.** A versioned work explicitly permits a genuine external counter-record to withdraw the artistic assumption of “two performances” while still preserving both artworks and the first reading. *Strength:* historical operation is corrigible rather than theatrical proof of an error. *Weakness:* may become a computational ethics tutorial instead of felt experience.

**M8 — FINITE ENDING AGAINST THE INFINITE CORPUS.** The work may make a true stop: it has two witnesses, one completed sounding and a place that never fills. The world need not grow another character, infinite generated continuations, or a forced third arrival. **Disturbs/exceeds the default Living Mythic World-Corpus expectation** of indefinite responsiveness. *Strength:* actual aesthetic restraint. *Weakness:* “minimalism” alone cannot be counted as new form.

## 6. COLLIDE THE MUTATIONS — DO NOT RANK GENRES

**M1 × M3:** Co-reading two real saved artifacts changes a receiver's temporal knowledge, but this alone duplicates the project's Two Memories and Borges basin. **Resist promotion as primary.**

**M2 × M4:** The waiting downbeat is alive; a blank causal register is truthful. But when coupled too neatly, an unplayed beat becomes a visual metaphor for “no data,” making the receiver merely solve an essay in sounds. **Keep the material tension, reject the neat equation.**

**M5 × M7:** The work could let an outside record contradict its performance claim. Strong evidence discipline, but if this is a future app feature it cannot satisfy the immediate CF-05B **make-the-work-now** law. **Treat as architecture/trace condition, not a promissory stand-in for art.**

**M6 × M8:** A genuine reader may preserve both works, or leave without producing any new deposit. The receiver's bounded discretion is stronger than a forced “select one run” decision. **Refusability is essential.**

**M1 × M2 × M5 × M6, under M8:** A distinct possibility emerges: not a two-voice composition, and not a forensic report, but an **ENCOUNTER WITH TWO SURVIVING IMPRESSIONS AND ONE UNAUTHORIZED STRIKE**. The receiver must bring the marks into relation while the supposed second blow remains outside their making. Real documentary witnesses hold down the fiction, and the fiction returns pressure to the witnesses; neither can certify the other.

**INVERSE TEST:** If tomorrow authentic records show one generation and two deliveries, the poem's second beat remains withheld, the two file witnesses still stand, and any false claim “two independent performances” must retract. If logs establish two generations, the work can register that without pretending the evidence was generated by the artwork. If no log appears, the finite encounter may still be complete without epistemic closure. **The work must remain alive in all three cases.**

**CAGE BREAK:** Remove the AI names, filenames and protocol labels: what remains is a familiar mark surviving twice, a human who carried the first away, a second carrier swollen with history, and a blow that cannot be counted from its imprint. If this collapses without technological vocabulary, formal mutation has failed. The field presently *retains* sensible bodily and temporal stakes without that vocabulary.
