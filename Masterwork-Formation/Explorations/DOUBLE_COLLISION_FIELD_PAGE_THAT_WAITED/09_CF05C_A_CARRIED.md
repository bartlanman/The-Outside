The light lost its hold on the room  
while the words were still there.

A screen could ask for another beginning.  
A page had already arrived.

Someone carried it out  
with the small, stubborn care  
we give to things that may disappear  
while we are looking at them.

It did not bring the hour.  
It did not bring the mechanism.  
It did not bring the room's permission.

It brought the mark.

At the last door  
a letter leaned crooked  
in precisely the same place.
