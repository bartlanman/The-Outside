A book had taken the long way  
toward the same open page.

Twenty rooms kept their doors.  
One kept an answer to the wrong question.  
Another kept the hand that corrected it.  
Others held color against the glass  
until the glass seemed to remember.

Nothing in the last room  
could fold the earlier rooms away.

There, among the sewn hours,  
the mark waited without knowing  
it had traveled somewhere else.

At the last door  
a letter leaned crooked  
in precisely the same place.
