But the original external binary files are not stored in this GitHub package, so the source encounter remains conditional on separate access. Its strongest artistic body is the tactile suspended beat against a pair of nearly coincident refrains; its weakest is the expository source instruction at each entrance. Do not mislabel the latter as artistic triumph. The performance can stop without a forensic conclusion, and may be rejected by a receiver who finds the physical instruction insufficient.
