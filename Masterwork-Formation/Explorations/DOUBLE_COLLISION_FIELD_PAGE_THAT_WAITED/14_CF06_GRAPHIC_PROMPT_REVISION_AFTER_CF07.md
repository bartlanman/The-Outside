## EXACT CF-06 REVISED GENERATION PROMPT (CANDIDATE)

Create a monumental nearly square abstract mixed-media artwork conceived as a hand-worked shallow lime-plaster relief with translucent casein washes, finely abraded graphite and sparse burnished wax. The field is continuous and spacious: warm alabaster and milky limestone with restrained cool mineral grey and rare dark oxide-red pigment embedded in the material, not a radiant fantasy light. A grazing natural side light catches uneven surface heights and the evidence of repeated artistic decisions—compression, scraping back, interrupted wash, a delicate remade edge.

A single low, oblique disturbance travels irregularly across the lower middle of the field. Seen from across the room it appears to be one soft, almost bodily sweep of pressure, just beginning to turn upward before its movement is lost. Seen nearer, its shape will not remain one continuous contour. Two similarly curved impressions lie in an unequal, overlapping relationship, not side by side as specimens. At one, the plaster is visibly raised into a fragile compression lip; at the other, a near-matching darkened contour is chiefly a thin deposit of graphite and rubbed pigment, with very little corresponding relief. Their marks seem for a moment to register, then slip out of alignment. They are observable consequences with **no visible maker, tool, impact, or countable originating act**. Do not visually label either mark as the first or the second, as authentic or simulated, or as proof of how it was produced.

At an off-center place where the raised curve briefly thickens, give the mineral surface a restrained clearly adult human bodily contour: a flexed knee passing into a substantial calf. The adult proportions and subtly living skin undertones can be sensed, but there is no free-standing leg, foot, pelvis, identifiable person, or complete figure. The knee and calf do not press a second mark into being. Instead, the living contour receives a gathered tension already present in the plaster and seems to withhold further movement: its volume becomes mineral ridge, its skin grain becomes porous casein and chalk, its edge disperses into rubbed graphite and wax. The visual transition belongs to the same material event rather than depicting a limb placed on or beside an object. The body is non-explicit, unsensational and neither posed nor erotic.

Allow the upper and upper-left reaches of the surface to remain vast, almost unworked and genuinely active. A faint interrupted rise from the compressed relief points toward this vacancy without drawing a line through it; the viewer feels an expected continuation that is not furnished. Do not place a ghost limb, implied hammer, missing-tool shadow, floating emblem, dramatic horizon, or invented second strike in the empty region. Let silence be spatially felt rather than diagrammed.

The long view should hold an apparently continuous movement. The middle distance should open a quiet contradiction between related but materially dissimilar impressions and the almost-recognizable adult bodily fold. The nearest view should reveal how graphite, relief and abrasion resist an unambiguous account of origin. Neither scale solves the others. Let the entire artwork balance tenderness against withholding, physical consequence against uncertainty, and the dignity of two surviving traces against the absence of a certified event count. The surface should display disciplined painterly labor, delicate resistance and a genuine second reading—not photorealistic forensic documentation or a neat symbolic puzzle.

No text whatsoever: no letters, words, type, numbers, signatures, Greek symbols, marks resembling captions, signage or UI. No diptych, before-and-after comparison, diagram, explanatory labels, wood, stacked paper, book, printing press, literal hammer, suspended tool, collage scraps, portals, arches, towers, fantasy landscape, circuitry, threads, luminous filaments, blue-and-gold glow, right-edge hand or left-edge torso, or repeated stock surreal composition.

## Controlled changes from CF-06 draft

1. **Remove false origin inference.** Two different marks can be seen; they cannot be declared two separate pressure histories or proofs of different makers.
2. **Retain one field.** Impressions are subtly overlapped and nonparallel in one continuous mineral plane; the second reading is not a specimen comparison.
3. **Assign an adult body office.** Bent knee/calf is a field-integrated receiving contour of withheld motion, not an artist's hand making or authenticating a second impact. Flesh-to-mineral transition is materially described; no complete body or sexual emphasis.
4. **Make vacancy consequential.** Upper area receives an incomplete tendency to continue; no explanatory shadow, missing object or symbolic instrument.
5. **Separate art process from evidence.** Lime-plaster/casein/graphite/wax are proposed artistic means and depicted substance, not forensic documentation of either original digital source.
6. **Preserve recurring visual defenses.** No lettering, literal hammer/press, torn book pages, wood, default cyan/gold fantasy horizon, hand/webbing or house silhouette.
