## THE COMPLETE WORK — BEGIN

# NO NUMBER FOR THE HAMMER

*A double-witness performance for one reader, two entrances, and an unplayed measure.*

**To begin.** Enter through **A** or **B**; choose before reading the other. Read the chosen leaf all the way to its last line. Then read the other. Continue to **THE INTERVAL** and **THE RETURN**. At the Return, speak the shared three-line refrain from whichever leaf you entered first.

You may read without making a mark. You may finish without finding a cause.

---

## A — CARRIED

*Witness A: the separately preserved first-run DOCX.*

The light lost its hold on the room  
while the words were still there.

A screen could ask for another beginning.  
A page had already arrived.

Someone carried it out  
with the small, stubborn care  
we give to things that may disappear  
while we are looking at them.

It did not bring the hour.  
It did not bring the mechanism.  
It did not bring the room's permission.

It brought the mark.

At the last door  
a letter leaned crooked  
in precisely the same place.

---

## B — GATHERED

*Witness B: the HTML book, twenty earlier chapters before its final run.*

A book had taken the long way  
toward the same open page.

Twenty rooms kept their doors.  
One kept an answer to the wrong question.  
Another kept the hand that corrected it.  
Others held color against the glass  
until the glass seemed to remember.

Nothing in the last room  
could fold the earlier rooms away.

There, among the sewn hours,  
the mark waited without knowing  
it had traveled somewhere else.

At the last door  
a letter leaned crooked  
in precisely the same place.

---

## THE INTERVAL

Place your hands a page-width apart.

Do not make the leaves touch.

Between the left hand and the right  
there is room for a sheet of paper,  
room for the thickness of another,  
room for a sound that might have happened  
before either was saved.

There is no place  
to put the maker's missing hour.

Raise one hand  
as though something in it has weight.

The wrist prepares.  
The skin remembers the possibility of impact.  
The air does not help.

*Here the second measure is not played.*

Do not strike.

Keep the hand raised  
for the length of a breath  
you cannot make belong  
to either page.

---

## THE RETURN

Speak the shared refrain  
from the leaf you read first.

At the last door  
a letter leaned crooked  
in precisely the same place.

The words are where you left them.

One of the pages was carried away.  
One of the pages was gathered in.

Your hand lowers  
onto neither.

The mark leans on.

The room where it happened  
does not open.

**END.**
