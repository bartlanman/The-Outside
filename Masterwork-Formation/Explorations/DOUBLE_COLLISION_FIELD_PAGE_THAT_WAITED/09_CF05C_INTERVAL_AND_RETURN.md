# THE INTERVAL AND RETURN

Place your hands a page-width apart.

Do not make the leaves touch.

Between the left hand and the right  
there is room for a sheet of paper,  
room for the thickness of another,  
room for a sound that might have happened  
before either was saved.

There is no place  
to put the maker's missing hour.

Raise one hand  
as though something in it has weight.

The wrist prepares.  
The skin remembers the possibility of impact.  
The air does not help.

*Here the second measure is not played.*

Do not strike.

Keep the hand raised  
for the length of a breath  
you cannot make belong  
to either page.

---

## THE RETURN

Speak the refrain from the leaf you entered first:

At the last door  
a letter leaned crooked  
in precisely the same place.

The words are where you left them.

One of the pages was carried away.  
One of the pages was gathered in.

Your hand lowers  
onto neither.

The mark leans on.

The room where it happened  
does not open.

**END.**

You may depart without explanation, comment or new trace. The two historical witnesses remain separate, whether or not their original files were available during this performance.
