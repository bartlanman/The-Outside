### Outside mass 1 — Preservation metadata / Objects and Events [MAGNETO]
The Library of Congress PREMIS Data Dictionary v3 distinguishes **Objects, Events, Rights, Agents**; an Event has its own identifying metadata and links to Objects. This separates an artifact's identity from the history of actions in which it participated.
Source: https://www.loc.gov/standards/premis/v3/ and https://loc.gov/standards/premis/v3/premis-hierarchical-3-0.html
**Field effect:** The double cannot be settled by comparing two textual surfaces. A complete event history would be a different evidence type than the saved artifacts.

### Outside mass 2 — At-least-once event delivery / retries [INVERSE]
Microsoft Fabric documents at-least-once delivery; the same event can arrive again after retry with the same source+id, while applications deduplicate it. Twilio describes similar retry and duplicate-event handling.
Sources: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/fabric/real-time-hub/fabric-event-delivery-guarantees and https://www.twilio.com/docs/events/event-delivery-and-duplication
**Field effect:** A second appearance can be a *re-delivery*, not necessarily a newly made event. However this does NOT determine what ChatGPT's specific glitch/retry did. The actual original run log is unknown.

### Outside mass 3 — Paleontological footprints / trace formation [SIDEWAYS SEED / MAGNETO]
Falkingham and Gatesy (Palaeontology, 14 January 2026) explain that similar-looking footprints may arise from different trackmakers, while a single foot may leave very different traces depending on movement and substrate.
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/pala.70040
**Field effect:** Similarity of a mark alone cannot prove common provenance. The causal triple FOOT × MOTION × SUBSTRATE resists our impulse to let the finished text tell us how it came into being. An unexpected material analogy, not an equivalence between fossils and code.

### Outside mass 4 — Score versus performance [FEEDING FLOW WITH RESISTANCE]
The scholarly survey on music performance (Lerch et al., 2021) distinguishes score from acoustic realizations; repeated realizations can differ in timing, dynamics, receiver effect. Bede Williams's practice research conducted several works twice with different interpretive approaches.
Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.09018 and https://research-portal.st-andrews.ac.uk/en/publications/the-multiple-interpretability-of-the-score-from-prefigurement-to-/

### Outside mass 5 — Content-addressed deduplication [STRONG INVERSE / PARTLY UNTRANSLATED]
