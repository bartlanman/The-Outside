## CF-03 — POET SING

The screen paled
before the turning finished.

One leaf was carried out
loose, creased against a sleeve.
One was sewn
after twenty rooms of ink.

At the last door
a letter leaned crooked
in precisely the same place.

The press held no witness
under its black jaw.
A wheel caught
in the teeth of another wheel.

Was that the blow returning?
Was that another blow?
The iron, already cold,
kept its silence.

A thumb opened the loose page.
A thread restrained the bound one.
Neither surrendered
the slight missing stroke.

At the edge of hearing
a hand rose for a second beat
and stayed there.

Two sheets
against the air.
No number
for the hammer.
