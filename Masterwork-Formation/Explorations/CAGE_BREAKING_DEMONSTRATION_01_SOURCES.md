# Cage-Breaking Demonstration 01 — Outside Sources

## On Kawara — Today Series
Guggenheim teaching material:
https://www.guggenheim.org/teaching-materials/on-kawara-silence/paintings-today-series-date-paintings

Relevant source facts:
- Today series begun in 1966.
- Date Paintings produced according to repeated procedure.
- Paintings unfinished by midnight were destroyed.
- Dates use local linguistic conventions.
- Works often paired with local newspaper clippings.

Project use:
inverse to universal responsiveness; actual time as governing condition.

## Ragnar Kjartansson — The Visitors
SFMOMA:
https://www.sfmoma.org/exhibition/ragnar-kjartansson-the-visitors/

Relevant source facts:
- nine-channel video installation;
- 64-minute work;
- performers occupy different rooms and play together.

Project use:
participation by movement / attention without adaptive realization.

## Bubble Talk — Ars Electronica Futurelab, 2026
https://ars.electronica.art/futurelab/en/bubble-talk/

Relevant source facts:
- personalized AI guide;
- adapts exhibition material to visitor interests;
- intentionally introduces connections beyond the visitor's familiar cultural bubble.

Project use:
personalization as bridge toward estrangement rather than confirmation.

## Wicked Mirror — Ars Electronica Festival, 2026
https://ars.electronica.art/negotiatinghumanity/en/view/wicked-mirror-39838ddb450c81b0931ee838bd5d6102/

Relevant source facts:
- visitor states opinion;
- AI-generated double using face / voice argues opposing position;
- work deliberately preserves contradiction rather than resolving it.

Project use:
personalized mirror used to generate opposition.

## Hello Worlds! — Ars Electronica Futurelab, 2026
https://ars.electronica.art/futurelab/en/hello-worlds/

Relevant source facts:
- responsive exhibition platform;
- optional personalization;
- visitors can opt out and retain exhibition access;
- temporary visitor record used by opt-in system.

Project use:
personalization as layer rather than ontological requirement of the world.

## Embodied Animation — Ars Electronica Festival, 2026
https://ars.electronica.art/negotiatinghumanity/en/view/embodied-animation-3b338ddb450c80e4bf9cde89db35db32/

Relevant source facts:
- bodily gestures shape real-time visual / sonic outputs;
- work framed as embodied, playable performance;
- feedback loop between movement, generative system, and perception.

Project use:
multiple interaction channels beyond conversational responsiveness.
