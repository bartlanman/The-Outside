Rox is not simply another decorative emblem contributor or an additional chronological biochemical step. Her **enclosing circles embody the membrane condition** that can change:

2. **HEMIFUSION — local joining:** contacting outer surfaces locally become continuous while interiors remain different.

The Threshold design becomes structurally embedded in each enclosing circle; the two are relational, not interchangeable circles stamped beside another symbol. At the deeper relation:

- **διά / THROUGH:** a crossing takes place while the two sides remain separately enclosed.

- **OPENING / PORE:** the two interiors may communicate directly.
- **σύν / WITH:** a new shared enclosure may exist while distinct histories survive.

**DOS belongs at the passage where the previous separating arrangement may relinquish its claim to rule the encounter.** DOS cannot force union, override meaningful refusal, or turn annihilation into a false story of liberation. Rox measures warrant; she does not manufacture a successful outcome.
