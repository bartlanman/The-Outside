# Source Handling Rule

These sources establish outside practices on their own terms.

They do **not** establish:
- THE BETWEEN CAN HAVE STANDING as a universal law;
- THE WHOLE MAY HAVE NO LOCATION as a metaphysical claim;
- every gap as generative;
- silence as inherently relational.

Those are project-level collisions and poetic inferences.

Preserve the distinction.
