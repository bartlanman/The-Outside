## CF-06 — GRAPHIC PROMPT (EXACT LOCK)
Create a monumental nearly square abstract mixed-media masterwork depicting a single irreversible instant in which an elaborate refractory casting shell fractures outward while the bronze form it once constrained remains intact but materially changed by release. The composition is radically asymmetrical: a broad, diagonally descending accumulation of broken chalk-white casting material, oxidized bronze, ash-black residue, fine mineral dust, and exposed casting seams occupies the lower-left and central portions of the image. An immense, quiet expanse of pale, cool atmospheric space remains above and to the right.

The fracture is not an explosion or a spectacle of destruction. It is the slow consequential separation of a containing form from a released form. Multiple distinct pressure histories converge along the fracture, but they do not merge into one uniform texture. Some shell fragments retain precise impressions of what they once contained. Others reveal rough unworked interiors. The bronze carries an unexpected shallow impression visible only where the shell has lifted away.

Integrate a clearly adult, non-explicit human bodily presence directly into the visual event: a continuous natural contour of upper back, shoulder, side torso, and neck emerges through the patinated bronze surface, partly concealed by remaining shell and partly dissipating into mineral dust. The body is neither a portrait nor an erotic pose. Its anatomy is sensuous, volumetric, and materially continuous with the casting; intimate areas remain covered, turned away, obscured, or outside the composition. The flesh receives the pressure of the shell while the metal retains the memory of its release.

A smaller, independent curved impression remains within one displaced fragment. It does not align perfectly with the exposed bronze, making it impossible to reconstruct a single complete original from the surviving pieces.

Use a deliberate combination of impasto oil painting, mineral pigment, blind embossing, dry charcoal, and fine engraved marks. Visible painterly decisions, worked edges, material resistance, and the distinct provenance of each substance are essential. Allow Dalí-like spaciousness without imitating Dalí's objects; inherit Duchamp's temporal simultaneity, Picasso's field construction, Rauschenberg's heterogeneous material standing, and Chuck Close's change of meaning across viewing distances without stylistic quotation.

From afar, the event appears to be one form releasing another. From nearby, different histories of making, containing, receiving, breaking, and surviving remain legible without becoming a completed illustration.

No lettering, captions, Greek symbols, labels, diagrammatic elements, full posed figures, recognizable character portraits, conventional archways, mystical portals, ornamental filaments, sacred geometry, glowing fantasy architecture, exposed explicit anatomy, or erotic display. The released space must participate in the event rather than function as passive background.
## CF-07 — EXEGESIS

CRAFT appears as the event of shaped, consequential making. SUBLIME is the receiver's encounter with significance unavailable when viewing only the mold. DOS releases an arrangement's authority without destroying its entire history. The adult contour belongs to the material of the event; it does not claim a literal offspring, speaker, or autonomous entity.

**διά:** fracture opens passage. **σύν:** shell, bronze, impression, anatomical volume, residue and atmosphere remain materially distinct in one relation. The strongest second reading comes from the mismatched traces, which prohibit a false complete restoration. Large open space receives rather than contains.
