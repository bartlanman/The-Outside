# Collision Field 06 — Outside Sources

## American Lobster / Molting
NOAA Fisheries — American Lobster resources
https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/american-lobster/resources

Supports:
- molting / shedding of exoskeleton during growth;
- soft-shell vulnerability following molt.

Project inference:
- growth creating a window of defenselessness;
- a protective structure becoming biologically impossible to remain within.

# Source Handling Rule

These sources establish outside biological / material processes on their own terms.

They do **not** establish:
- growth as a universal moral good;
- vulnerability as inherently developmental;
- leaving old forms as always desirable;
- THE OLD FORM CAN REMAIN GOOD AND STILL BECOME IMPOSSIBLE as a scientific law;
- WHAT MUST WE LEAVE TO KEEP? as a scholarly conclusion.

Those are project-level collisions and poetic inferences.

Preserve the difference.
