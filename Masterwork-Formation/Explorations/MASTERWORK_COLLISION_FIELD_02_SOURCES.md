# Collision Field 02 — Outside Sources

## Getty — The Scores Project
https://www.getty.edu/publications/scores/intro/

Supports:
- score as inscription / mark / tally;
- score as notation translating dynamic time into instructions;
- postwar experimental scores as transdisciplinary medium;
- realization as performer translation of open score;
- score practices expanding into conceptual art, performance, poetry, dance and intermedia;
- global and informal notation systems complicating any universal Western score model;
- social / racial history complicating claims of avant-garde invention.
