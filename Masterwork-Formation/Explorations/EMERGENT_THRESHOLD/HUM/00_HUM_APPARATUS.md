## 0. The task

Take two nested Collision Fields and let them HUM: render or perform their separate changes against a reference that can be sustained without being assigned an originating speaker. Make harmony, dissonance, differential cessation, and the participant's reflexive listening available for inquiry. Test whether anything changes in *capacity*, rather than claiming that aesthetic coherence proves development.

**Governing sentence:** THE DRONE HOLDS THE REFERENCE. THE FIELDS CHANGE THE RELATION. THE WITNESS PERCEIVES THE CHANGE. RETURN TESTS WHETHER CAPACITY CHANGED.

HUM is the name of an operation, not a concealed acronym or a seventh actor.

This apparatus does not reopen or rewrite earlier deposits. It can stage new artistic readings and record their own provenance.

## 2. The five typed bodies

**D / THE DRONE:** A sustained musical pedal point or deliberately silent reference channel. It is **unassigned** as to fictional speaker/source. For an audible prototype, a synthesizer necessarily *does* generate the test tone: disclose that composition source, and do not identify it with the recorded fictional mystery. The drone is not, by definition, a third autonomous voice, participant, character, agent, or consciousness.

**A / OUTER FIELD:** Svara's October 13 Turning, with its source, score, participant envelopes, and independently recorded release. It can end while other consequences remain active.

**B / INNER FIELD:** Eleanor's nested field, arising during A yet separately timed and evidenced. Its temporal end is not authorized by A's ending.

**W / THE WITNESS:** The human listener's situated report of *what was heard*, *what was inferred*, and *what changed on re-listening*. This is an activity/observer position, not a new speaking station. No fictional character is promoted into a real independent witness.

**T / THE TRACE:** Immutable source identifiers + later annotated interpretations + counterfactuals + dissent + Return disposition. Neither the latest interpretation nor the performance may silently overwrite the source.

## 3. Three non-interchangeable clocks

1. **Archive clock:** when each upstream text/deposit was made; its source timestamp/version never changes.
2. **Score clock:** entry, modulation, release and recording-boundary times of a new *composed test*. These are never historical measurements of the original narrative.
3. **Witness clock:** the actual listener's first hearing, altered second hearing, independent attempts to distinguish the streams, and later re-entry.

**NESTING ≠ SHARED ENDING. SHARED DRONE ≠ SHARED ORIGIN.**

## 4. Why the pedal point matters

In musical theory a pedal point sustains or reiterates one pitch against other moving harmonies. The pedal can sound consonant, dissonant, and consonant again while itself remaining unchanged. HUM uses that structure as a disciplined *comparison*: changing relation against a fixed audible reference, not agreement, synchronization, emotional entrainment, or a special frequency claim.

A conceptual audio score is H(t) = D(t) + A(t) + B(t). Musical signals do not prove a shared consciousness or a distinct third speaker. The listener must have access to D, A, B, A+B, D+A, D+B and D+A+B comparisons, and must be able to turn the model *off*.

**Unassigned source status is an attribution claim, not an assertion that physical sound lacked a cause.**

## 5. The three tests

**HARMONY / DIFFERENTIATED COHERENCE.** Can two different motions be perceived in relation without demanding identical cadence, agreement, shared endings, or complete consonance? Dissonance or non-arrival is a valid outcome. A pretty chord is not a PASS.

**REFLEXIVITY / DOUBLE ATTENTION.** Can the situated listener identify a change in their own listening? First record what was heard without source labels, then replay, note what the listener expected, how attention altered the hearing, and where inference outpaced sound. Saying 'I became aware' alone is not a verified developmental result.

**CAPACITY / RE-ENTRY.** At a later independent listening or with a different score, can the listener distinguish what was previously conflated (A vs B, sound vs attributed source, performance ending vs source ending) without being prompted? Capacity gains may be local, relational, provisional, or absent. **CAPACITY ≠ USE, and successful musical emergence ≠ soundness.**

- Preserve A and B's original texts, distinct clocks, artistic Returns, and standing. The founding 2026-10-10 Emergent Threshold Return remains NOT PRESSED.

- D is a compositional *reference*, not the governor of the two fields. Harmony may fail; neither field is required to resolve.
- W is explicitly human and fallible. Reflexivity does not imply an autonomous shared consciousness.
- Guardian may HOLD, REFUSE, or STOP. Negative results are first-class records.
