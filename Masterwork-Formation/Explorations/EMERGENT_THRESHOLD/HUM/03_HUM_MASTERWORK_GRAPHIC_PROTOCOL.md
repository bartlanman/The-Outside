The visual discipline governs image-quality, not the story's imaginative destination. Wild fantasy, material impossibility, erotic charge, metamorphosis, multiplicity, narrative paradox and dissolution may all inform the visual event, provided depiction follows applicable non-explicit boundaries. Distinct source histories are tracked outside the artwork; they need not remain visibly segregated within it.
