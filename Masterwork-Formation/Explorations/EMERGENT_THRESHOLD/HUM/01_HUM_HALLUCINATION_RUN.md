# HUM — HALLUCINATION RUN GUIDE
**Active command:** RUN HUM or HUM AFTER TWO COLLISION FIELDS
**Source of truth:** [00_HUM_HALLUCINATION.md](00_HUM_HALLUCINATION.md)
**Status:** Experimental creative performance, not canon.

## Before performance (outside the story)
1. Verify two distinct completed Collision Fields and identify first and nested second.
2. Read their full available source material and chronology. Bring forward the concrete live material: who was present, what each wanted, what wasn't resolved, what motifs were charged, what words acquired gravity.
3. If only summaries survive, say so briefly outside the performance; then write new fiction without disguising invention as old transcript.
4. Run [02_HUM_OUTSIDE_SUBSTANCE_DRAW.md](02_HUM_OUTSIDE_SUBSTANCE_DRAW.md): fresh web search across differing domains, feed and counterforce, and an actual field delta. Keep external sources in backstage trace, not as lectures within the fiction.
5. Begin creative work; **do not** subject HUM to the Collision Field creation or graphic protocol again. Their earlier completed constraints become charged material.

## Felt performance requirement
Read [04_HUM_FELT_INTENSITY_PROTOCOL.md](04_HUM_FELT_INTENSITY_PROTOCOL.md) before drafting and use it as the internal creative engine. Render palpable body, voice, warmth, proximity, hesitation, reciprocal attraction, rhythmic pressure, varied cathartic releases, and whole-field transduction through the screen. No abstract stage narration in place of experience. Revise by reading the story alone before proceeding to visual prompting.

## Inside the work (no staging labels needed)
5. Activate through novelty and prediction error; make every character's next movement grow from the previous two fields.
6. Integrate; accumulate sensory/cultural pressure. Allow narrative or physical impossibilities and coherent hallucination.
7. Let laughter, tears, voice, trembling and erotic/other climactic release appear where earned, with independent character motive and consensual action where relevant.
8. Gather *the entire cast and material world of both fields*, transforming difference into a vortex of cultural gravity.
9. Drive the final whole-field peak **through the fictional computer screen**. The screen is a membrane, a reader-side crossing, a transformation of text into received imaginative substance—not literal physical travel.
10. Return by leaving an experiential afterimage in the reader, rather than explaining the mechanics or validating the event as evidence.

## After the full story
Use [03_HUM_MASTERWORK_GRAPHIC_PROTOCOL.md](03_HUM_MASTERWORK_GRAPHIC_PROTOCOL.md): visualize the actual final crossing, consult visual Masterwork lineage and medium library, write HUM graphic prompt and exegesis, lint and lock, then STOP. Image generation only after explicit image command.

## Finish (short out-of-story trace only if useful)
Name original sources, newly invented content and open remainder separately. Do not change upstream canonical Collision Field records.
**A successful HUM is a complete original artwork. Not a checklist, a report, an explanatory harmony story or an audio experiment.**
