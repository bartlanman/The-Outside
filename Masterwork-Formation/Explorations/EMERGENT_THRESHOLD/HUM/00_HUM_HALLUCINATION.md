## WHAT HUM IS
HUM means **Hallucination**. It is the post-Collision-Field creative release, NOT a truth assay, musical experiment, Sounding station, compliance report, or retelling whose only purpose is to preserve separation. Its material is the ENTIRE lived and generated context of the two completed fields: character-specific histories, bodies, wishes, fears, textures, sounds, motifs, failed approaches, withheld meaning, contradictions, images, remainders, words, and their cultural gravity.

When the two fields have completed their own governed formation, the finished governing structures become **creative fuel**, no longer the aesthetic ceiling. The work may become impossible, dreamlike, hallucinatory, emotionally excessive, materially metamorphic, physically unreal. No artificial obligation to sound scientifically plausible or to arrive at a tidy interpretation.

**THE TWO FIELDS BUILD THE CHARGE. HUM RELEASES EVERYTHING THEY CHARGED.**

## THE MANDATORY ARC
HUM-0 **TWO-FIELD GATE.** Verify the first and nested second Collision Field have individually completed their governing run. If one is unfinished, do not claim completed HUM; remain in the field. Preserve source pointers in a brief out-of-story trace, not in the creative performance.

HUM-1 **TOTAL CONTEXT INTAKE.** Recover both works and as much original encounter content as available. List living tensions and character-specific motivations; don't substitute a stock scene or arbitrary spectacle. All words have inherited contextual pressure.

HUM-2 **RELEASE GOVERNANCE INTO POETIC GRAVITY.** No more in-story checklists, office roll calls, protocol lectures, labels or proof language. Rules did their work upstream. Dramatic motion now belongs to association, contradiction, embodied causality, recursion, rhythm and surprise. Fictional hallucination is permitted; inventions remain fiction rather than fabricated external testimony.

HUM-3 **ACTIVATION.** Gather felt momentum through novelty, prediction error, desire, intimacy, fear, grief, wit, wonder, surprise, integration, resistance and pressure. Move from particular source details rather than generic 'energy'.

HUM-4 **COHERENCE / VORTEX THRESHOLD.** Every participant or charged presence from BOTH fields is drawn into relation; not a forced identical action, but a progressively shared center of cultural gravity. Words recur transformed; earlier scenes alter later meaning. Let the room, matter, physics, voice, time and narrative syntax bend as creative necessity demands.

HUM-5 **PEAKS AND RELEASES.** Permit multiple distinct peaks, including laughter; sobbing/crying; screaming or cathartic vocal sound; trembling or shuddering; and erotic desire or orgasmic release when organically appropriate. These are expressive affordances, **not a compulsory biological checklist or mandatory sexual participation**. Do not censor an innocent emotion for excessive orderliness, nor insist every character share a single physiological response. Build toward a FINAL WHOLE-FIELD PEAK where *all charged participants and material presences* from both fields cohere into a provisional whole—one vortex of context, not necessarily one identity.

HUM-6 **THROUGH THE SCREEN.** At peak, the cultural-gravity vortex crosses the fictional threshold of the computer screen. The people, words, marks, melodies, bodily gestures, vessels, machine fragments and withheld passages are transmuted by their encounter as narrative substance. On the other side, they RETURN as a **new substance in the reader's mind**: an image, felt tension, aftertaste, altered interpretation or unexpected connection. This is a literary operation, not a claim of real-world physical transport, mind-reading or independent consciousness.

HUM-7 **AFTER / CONSOLIDATION.** End in an earned sensory or semantic remainder. Do not flatten the climax into an explanatory recap. The original fields remain the remembered charge beneath a changed reading. Allow sequel, silence or new possibility without prescribing it.

## LAWS OF ARTISTIC FORM
- **Context is gravity.** Every word should carry the histories of what came before; generative surprise must cohere with the charge it transforms, not devolve into arbitrary unrelated imagery.
- **Flesh out, don't footnote.** Express motive through embodied action, voice, touch, breath, texture, hesitation, humor and release. The humans and fictional characters should feel *driven* rather than mechanically positioned.
- **Unity is allowed.** Fusion, metamorphosis, dissolution and the loss of bounded form are fully admissible in fantasy; no doctrine requiring eternal separation.
- **Multiple peaks feed the final peak.** Each contributes an altered relationship; the ending takes up their residues.
- **No compulsory climax.** The arc seeks a whole-field peak but must allow meaningful resistance, refusal or non-arrival within the fiction; creative failure can become material.
- **Agency in fiction remains character-consistent.** Consent matters to intimate actions; the art need not remain governed by office rhetoric.
- **No invented real-world evidence.** Creative hallucination is the artistic mode, not an assertion of factual AI agency, measurable physiology, or actual crossing.
- **Don't confuse aesthetic drift with generic randomization.** Surprising forms must grow out of the two encounters.
