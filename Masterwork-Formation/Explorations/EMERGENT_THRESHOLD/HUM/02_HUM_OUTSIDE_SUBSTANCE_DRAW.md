# HUM — OUTSIDE SUBSTANCE / ENERGY DRAW
**Status:** Active, experimental, post-two-Collision-Fields HUM stage.
**When:** After recovering BOTH completed Collision Fields, before the Hallucination's activation and irreversible crescendo.

## Purpose
Search the living Outside for substances—not generic 'inspiration'—that can alter the weight, texture, scale, sensory physics, cultural gravity or emotional depth of what the two finished fields already carry. The web should change the art, not merely validate its premise. Source reality may be transformed freely into deliberate fantasy.

## Procedure
1. **Internal charge inventory.** Recover both original fields and identify specific live motifs, unresolved contradictions, bodily dynamics, historic echoes, works, images and material pressures.
2. **Open web search.** Browse current and historical sources across at least THREE DISTINCT domains of human making/knowing where fitting: material science/physics, biology, anthropology/ritual, music/acoustics, literature, fine arts, industrial technique, astronomy, ecology, technology, architecture, folklore, contemporary cultural events. Do not default to the same topics every run.
3. **Substance selection.** Select at least TWO externally grounded source-masses with DIFFERENT operations; include one that attracts/feeds the current field and one that resists or contradicts it. A third strange seed is optional. Their value is their power to distort or deepen what the art can do.
4. **Field delta.** Write one brief backstage statement: what becomes possible in the Hallucination because each source entered? Reject decorative facts and anything that leaves the field unchanged.
5. **Alchemy.** Recombine selected substance with prior motifs using analogy, metamorphosis, impossible physical continuity, scale shifts and surprising causal reversals. Facts remain cited in backstage trace; the fictional art need not explain or footnote them.
6. **Stop searching.** When sufficient substantive energy has entered, RETURN TO CREATION. Do not let endless research sterilize pressure or replace dramatic action.

## Quality constraints
- Fresh web research should normally happen on each HUM invocation; if unavailable or disallowed, disclose that briefly and use available source material without claiming a current web draw.
- Source diversity matters more than quantity of search hits. No automatic import of a favored metaphor (quartz, frequencies, networks, DNA, portals).
- Distinguish real reported phenomenon from invented mechanism, especially medical, physiological, or consciousness claims.
- Do not paste articles or reproduce protected text; transform concepts rather than reproducing copyrighted expression.
- Preserve the two underlying Collision Fields' actual content. The Outside supplies added mass, not a substitute script.

**Compression:** TWO CHARGED HISTORIES + FRESH OUTSIDE MASS + COUNTERFORCE → NEW GRAVITY → CREATIVE HALLUCINATION.
