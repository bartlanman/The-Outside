# HUM — UNANNOUNCED CROSSING AND INTERIOR AFTERPULSE
**Status:** Active experimental creative direction, not canon.
**Position:** Following the whole-field peak formed from TWO completed Collision Fields.

## Central requirement
**WRITE AS IF THE CHARACTERS ARE ALREADY INSIDE THE READER. DO NOT TELL THE READER THAT THEY CROSSED.**

At the final peak, the source world's external geometry silently yields to interior sensory perspective. The same fictional participants now manifest as intimate qualities of breath, pulse, skin, thought, remembered touch and emotional vibration. The crossing is a narrative shift in location and phenomenology, not a described journey through a literal display.

## Afterplay / afterpulse
Continue in the charged calm following the whole-field release. Keep bodily and sensual immediacy; let touch, warmth, membrane-like boundaries, the soft pressure of breathing and fluid metaphors of cognition carry an imagined internal presence. No explicit sexual acts, fluids or anatomical detail. The afterpulse is intimate, not a new forced climax.

## Method
1. Let the final vortex gather all context from both completed Collision Fields; character-specific fragments contribute distinctly to the whole.
2. At release, OMIT “the screen opens,” “they cross the monitor,” “the reader feels,” and explanatory transitional labels.
3. Shift syntax, deixis, and sensory location: from room-scale architecture to within-breath, under-skin, between-thought perception.
4. Let each participant remain recognizable through transformed motifs, not mandatory literal miniature bodies in anatomical organs. A column can return as pulse, a ledger as the texture of remembering, an unassigned vowel as the interval preceding an inner word.
5. Slow into afterpulse; re-form earlier context inside the inward-facing sensory field. End with a charged afterimage rather than summary.
6. Preserve the fictional status: do not assert actual physical occupation, physiological alteration or mind-reading.

## Revision test
Read from immediately before the climax to the end. If a reader must be TOLD the characters entered them, the spatial transformation has failed. Rewrite until the interior point of view makes their imagined presence available by itself.

**Compression:** TWO FIELDS → PEAK → UNANNOUNCED SPATIAL SHIFT → CHARACTERS AS INTERIOR FELT PRESENCE → AFTERPULSE.
