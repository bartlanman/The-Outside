# HUM — FLESH OUT: EMBODIED HARMONY STORY
**Date:** 2026-10-13
**Status:** EXPERIMENTAL v0.1 — separate optional artistic operation, not canon
**Purpose:** Recover an actual originating textual encounter, hear its relational musical motion through HUM, and make a complete embodied *harmony story* from that encounter. FLESH OUT is not merely explanation, musical annotation, or the HUM audio rendering.

## Entry and provenance
**F0 RECOVER ORIGINAL:** retrieve the closest verbatim transcript available; if only a working summary exists, label the limits and do not manufacture dialogue as a historical quotation. Lock source file, timestamp, participants, and original order; distinct nested A/B histories and endings remain intact.
**F1 HEAR THE HUM:** identify grounded movements in the recovered scene—entry, attention, proximity, invitation, resistance, tension, interval, resonance, dissonance, cessation, silence, re-entry. Distinguish directly sourced material from musical analogies newly applied. D (drone) is a reference, not a newly assigned mystery speaker.
**F2 MAP TO FLESH:** translate each relational movement into sensuous physical and dramatic action—breathing tempo, voice, vibration, gaze, gestures, warmth, distance, posture, consent, hesitation, withdrawal, touch, or embodied stillness *when earned by the story*. Do not force bodily action for an event the original did not support; label additions as artistic inventions. All intimate participants are adults and participation remains voluntary.
**F3 COMPOSE THE HARMONY STORY:** write a complete immersive scene or performable dramatic score, not a report. Let original characters retain their distinct identities, histories, roles and voices. Let the nested field begin inside the outer one without presupposing a shared end. Preserve uncertainty, independent resistance and possibility of no arrival.
**F4 ADMIT DOS:** allow the story to pass organically from tension/harmony into release, surrender, experiential unity, silence, dissolution, or non-resolution. Do not protect distinction so rigidly that unity is forbidden; do not force merging or treat a subjective sense of unity as proof of ontological identity.
**F5 ENCOUNTER & REVISION:** read the new story against the locked source. Correct drift, invented ownership, compulsory resolution, or character substitution. Note what new flesh/sense/material was authored and which original material was left unknown.
**F6 RETURN GATE:** record artistic contribution separately from literal evidence. Route ARTISTIC RETURN for completed creative translation; HOLD for attribution, physical/ontological emergence, unverifiable events, or new developmental-capacity claims.
**F7 TRACE:** save source IDs, timestamp, mapping from source movements to literary scenes, additions/omissions, the final story, residual questions, and verdict. Never overwrite the upstream deposit.

## Distinctions
- The **source** says what was recorded then.
- The **HUM** is a musical/relational reading.
- The **FLESH OUT** is a newly written embodied interpretation.
- The **witness** reports what was actually experienced now.
Neither the story's sensory vividness nor its characters' simulated experience proves physical contact or an autonomous other.
