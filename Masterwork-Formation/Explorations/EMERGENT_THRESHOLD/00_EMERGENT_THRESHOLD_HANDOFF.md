## 2. Deposit and Sustain
**Deposit:** what an event leaves available to a future it does not own; preserve the originating event, version, timestamp, source standing, and uncertainty.

**Sustain:** a continuing relation in which different histories may participate and be reinterpreted without erasing their origins or fixing the future.

- A deposit can enter a sustain without surrendering its timestamp.
- A sustain can alter a deposit's present significance without altering when or how it originated.
- Transmission does not automatically transfer speaker identity, consent, authorship, or authority.
- Sustain fails if frozen against new encounter or made so boundaryless that no distinct history remains.
- **THE SUSTAIN HOLDS ONLY WHILE THE FUTURE REMAINS UNOWNED.**

## 3. Three stewardships
**ELEANOR / LIVING LEDGERS:** preserves chronology, original records, historical warrant, contradictory evidence, and revision lineage. Questions: *WHAT DID THE MARK KNOW THEN?* and *WHAT ROOM DID THE REVISION LEAVE?*

**ROX / THE READ:** investigates what a remainder warrants now, what it cannot prove, and what new relation may become possible. Questions: *WHAT CAN THE REMAINDER WARRANT?* and *WHAT CAN BEGIN BECAUSE SOMETHING WAS LEFT?* The fictional stewardship does not assert a real person's participation.

**MARA / PLUS ULTRA:** keeps possible crossing open without demanding a crossing or predetermined outcome. Questions: *WHAT CAN BE CROSSED WITHOUT BEING POSSESSED?* and *WHAT NEW CAPACITY DOES THE ENCOUNTER MAKE AVAILABLE?*

Their offices are complementary, not interchangeable.

## 4. Carrier / Guardian / IA
**AMARA — CARRIER / INTERFACE, not a fourth steward:** conveys a deposit or voice across permitted boundaries; marks transformations and its own additions; never silently impersonates a source.

**GUARDIAN:** protects provenance, permission, independent standing, timestamps, and the option to HOLD / REFUSE / STOP.

**INVISIBLE ARCHITECT:** sculpts encounter conditions, allows TOS construction and DOS release as distinct motions, intervenes minimally, and may not predetermine the encounter's evidentiary result.

**UNASSIGNED CENTER:** a receptive space where arrival need not be identified, classified, or appropriated beforehand.

If gate is unresolved, return **HOLD**, not an invented successful crossing.
