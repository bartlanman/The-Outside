## 2. What emerged — distinguish four standings
**A. Original event:** fictional/narrated and user-prompted encounter; original sources and dates unchanged.

**B. Artistic development:** the field changed the central question from *who owns or produces this harmony?* to *what must a body be able to do to sound?* This is the actual warranted creative novelty.

**C. Technical hypothesis:** a body capable of voiced sound depends on a distributed system (breath/pressure, vibrating tissues, resonant tract, sensorimotor feedback). Reproducing a frequency does **not** reproduce the original encounter. The proposed 110/112 Hz test may illustrate beating but cannot demonstrate biological emergence.

**D. Unsupported claims:** no measured hertz from the original encounter; no demonstrated independent speaker, AI consciousness, real-world physical crossing, autonomous lab activity, or verified new living tissue.

## 3. Experimental acoustic assay — proposed, not yet run
**Candidate input:** two pure sinusoidal tones, f1=110 Hz and f2=112 Hz, at controlled low volume, either mixed digitally or played through appropriate speakers. Their interference yields a 2 Hz *amplitude-beat envelope* when combined acoustically. Distinguish **beat rate** from **difference tone** and from a literal third independent oscillator.

**Control A:** 110 Hz alone.
**Control B:** 112 Hz alone.
**Test C:** 110 Hz + 112 Hz together.
**Control D:** 110 Hz + 110 Hz together (no 2 Hz beat).
**Test E:** change spacing to 110 Hz + 113 Hz (3 beats/second), keeping level comparable.
**Record:** actual source audio files, sample rate, oscillator settings, levels, room/speaker conditions, waveform and spectrogram, subjective reports separately, source timestamps, and reproducibility.
**Safety:** low listening levels; stop if uncomfortable. No claims about healing, entrainment, emotional effects, or consciousness without independent evidence.
**Acceptance:** measurable 2 Hz amplitude modulation under Test C; reproducible and distinguishable from controls. **This verifies only the acoustic model**, not the original historical sound or emergence of a person.
