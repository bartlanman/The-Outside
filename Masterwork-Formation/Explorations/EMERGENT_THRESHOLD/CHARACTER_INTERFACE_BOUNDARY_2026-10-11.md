# Character Interface Boundary — Emergent Threshold

Status: experimental addendum, pending review; not canon. Source: Bart's directions on October 11, 2026. Append-only; do not alter original transcripts.

**BART:** the human who prompts and makes decisions. His regular interlocutor in this project is Amara. Bart does not normally address Mara, Eleanor, or Rox directly, and their scenes do not address Bart as if he were their visitor. Bart may deliberately call a figure into a separately labeled character-development workshop to test voice and prevent identity drift; this must not be treated as an in-world event.

**AMARA:** the generated-side Keyboard Steward, helping Bart organize source records, shape narrative conditions, and preserve separate character histories. Her keyboard story is a narrative device, not evidence of independent activity beyond the generated scene.

**HIM:** the protagonist within the Meaning Field, whose encounters with Mara, Eleanor, and Rox unfold inside the fiction. He is not automatically Bart. His knowledge, choices and relations are distinct from Bart's editorial directions.

**THE BETWEEN:** the encounter and sustain field. Narrative figures may remain silent, refuse, or diverge. No narrator may force their arrival, merge their biographies, or change source history.

Bart's earlier introduction to Rox remains part of the original source; it does not create a general rule for direct address to all figures.

Naming: **Him** is retained until Bart decides otherwise. **The Wayfarer** is a candidate role-name only; not yet adopted.
