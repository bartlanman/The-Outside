## CF-03 — AEOLIAN POET SING: THE UNSIGNED VOWEL

Six throats
and a sound
that would not give its name.

Water leaning
into the metal lip.

Root beneath root
beneath the hand
that thought it knew
what the tree had said.

The mouth made room.
The vowel entered.
And all the gathered bodies
shook with the making.

But nowhere
in the shining pressure
was a signature.

One woman kept the hour.
One kept the difference.
One held the crossing.
One withdrew her hands.
The fifth carried what she had heard.
The sixth could not find the floor.

Below them
a second mouth
opened inside the first.

The vessel rang.
The water fell.
No one could say
which had begun the other.

And the unclaimed sound
passed through them
without becoming theirs.
