## II. The physical source and the limit of the analogy

A taut vibrating string supports modes whose frequencies depend on its length, tension, and linear mass density. For an ideal uniform string fixed at both ends,

`f_n = (n / (2L)) × sqrt(T / μ)`, for positive integers `n`.

Here `L` is vibrating length, `T` is tension, and `μ` is mass per unit length. Physical boundary conditions select which standing-wave patterns are allowed. Changing those conditions can change the frequencies and mode shapes available.

A **single** vibrating string can produce a fundamental and overtones. A **musical chord** normally refers to multiple pitches heard together, whether supplied by several strings, voices, instruments, or other means. The cord → chord transformation in this document is **not** a claim that one line automatically produces a musical chord simply by vibrating. It is the fictional expansion from one relationship to **plural distinguishable simultaneous relations**.

Helpful physical concepts and their boundaries:

| Physical concept | Actual meaning | Bounded fictional analogy |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency | Cycles per unit time | Recurrence of a specific relational activity |
| Amplitude | Magnitude of a measurable oscillation | The degree to which a particular response is expressed, **not** a direct measure of emotional truth |
| Phase | Relative position in oscillation cycles | Whether responses lead, follow, coincide, or fall out of step |
| Resonance | Large response under particular driving and damping conditions | A condition that makes some possible responses more available |
| Interference | Superposition of waves, with reinforcement and cancellation | Some expressions become more visible while others are obscured; **not** necessarily interpersonal influence |
| Overtones / harmonic content | Spectral components of periodic signals | A developed activity may support several distinguishable patterns at once |
| Consonance / dissonance | Musical-perceptual relations that depend on acoustics and context | A tension need not be a defect and harmony need not be compulsory agreement |
| Boundary conditions | Constraints affecting permissible modes | Rules of admissibility, permeability and standing that condition an encounter |

**Scientific boundary:** Actual membrane transport, vocal-tract resonances, string vibration, and psychological or narrative interactions have different mechanisms. They cannot be collapsed into a universal vibrating-field law. This project may compare them as *relations* only where it keeps the difference between source physics and invented operation visible.

**Relation to holography:** Celestial holography motivates the question of alternative mathematical descriptions and preserved structure. The harmonic model here is a creative analogy; it is not a derived prediction of celestial holography, proof of a boundary encoding all inner states, or a physical mechanism of cosmic consciousness.

## IV. Scales of the sounding boundary

### A. Cellular membrane — selective response

Membranes are dynamic, selective interfaces. Real molecular and ionic phenomena may oscillate, and cells can exhibit rhythmic processes, but there is no single universal cellular "chord."

In the creative microscopic register:
- **Cord:** the distinguishable interface and its structural constraints;
- **Sounding:** the changing response to a perturbation;
- **Chord:** multiple simultaneously operative processes or responses, held distinct;
- **Measure:** what actually passed, what did not, what condition changed, and what new exchange became possible.

No magical membrane fusion; no assertion that literal acoustic vibration occurs in every process.

### B. Collision Field — relation without forced accord

The Between may hold plural participants, each with its own temporal, cultural, embodied and source standing.

In the working model:
- **Cord:** boundary of admissible encounter;
- **Sounding:** distinct participants respond under actual field conditions;
- **Chord:** the relation among responses becomes perceivable;
- **Measure:** preserved distinctions, timing, transformation, resistance, what cannot be reconstructed;
- **New capacity:** only if a subsequent field can do something consequentially new that its prior conditions did not allow.

**Guardrail:** dissonance, rests, refusal, asymmetry and unresolved difference remain legitimate. This is not a mandate to unify, fuse, synchronize or harmonize everyone.

### C. The Outside boundary — HERE ⇄ THERE

The outer relation includes **Bart (HERE)** as actual human participant and **Him (THERE)** as a distinct fictional pole of Bart's participation. They are coupled through prompt, narrative encounter, carrying, interpretation and return, not literally quantum-entangled.

In the working model:
- **Cord:** the HERE/THERE seam;
- **Sounding:** a prompt alters story conditions, Him encounters them, and a different or unexpected consequence returns;
- **Chord:** multiple perspectives, including Svara's witnessing and Sabrina's description, reveal a relational structure unavailable from either pole alone;
- **Measure:** explicit comparison between intent, encounter, trace, response, remaining difference and next possible action;
- **Founding action:** **Bart actually receives and decides** whether to authorize change. A character or narrator may not manufacture Bart's reception or press a real Return for him.
