CF-05B — MASTERWORK CREATION
The Impression That Leaves

A complete artwork for two surfaces, pressure, movement, and retained
difference

I — RECEIVE

Lay a sheet of soft clay beneath a piece of fine woven cloth.

Press one fingertip gently into the cloth.

Leave the pressure long enough for the clay to register its shape.

II — MOVE

<PARSED TEXT FOR PAGE: 89 / 320>

Without lifting the finger, slide it slowly across the cloth.

Allow the weave to resist.

Do not correct the resulting deformation.

III — RELEASE

Lift the finger.

Remove the cloth.

The original impression is no longer where the final contact occurred.

IV — TWO READINGS

Let another person inspect the clay.

Ask them to identify where the fingertip rested.

Then allow them to inspect the cloth.

Ask where the movement began.

Preserve both readings, even if they disagree.

V — THE RETURN

Place the cloth above the clay again.

Do not press.

The materials now occupy the relation they previously occupied, but no longer
carry the same force.

Let the receiver look once more.

VI — END

Remove the cloth.

Keep the impression.

Do not reproduce the finger.
