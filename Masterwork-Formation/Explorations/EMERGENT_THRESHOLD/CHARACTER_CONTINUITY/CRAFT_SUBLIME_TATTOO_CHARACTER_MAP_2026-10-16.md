## Three-tier inheritance (never flatten the levels)

1. **Ancient symbol**: the original, character-specific symbolic form carried in the tattoo. Preserve its distinctive morphology; historical cultural attribution describes the *symbol lineage*, not a claim of literal unaltered ancient tattoo practice.
2. **Deconstructed Craft Sublime emblem component**: the character's irreducible structural contribution to the complete emblem.
3. **Craft Sublime becoming / unified emblem**: the individual symbol and the emblem component become ONE tattoo composition through material transformation. In the composite emblem all seven functions become relational, while their origins remain inspectable.
