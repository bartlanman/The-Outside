## 1. Decisive architecture

**The seven symbols on Sabrina are not seven powers that she possesses in advance. They are seven *possible manifestations* of seven other characters' already defined Craft Sublime tattoos.**

Each origin character remains the source of their tattoo's activation, embodiment, and domain. When an activation is actually enacted and its crossing is admissible in the story, a *corresponding inscription may manifest on Sabrina*. Sabrina thus becomes a site where the distinct activities can be compared, related, and perhaps heard together as a **chord**.

**Character activation → permitted correspondence → manifestation on Sabrina → multiple distinguishable marks in relation → possible new field of perception → admissible return.**

The order matters: Sabrina's marks **do not cause** the origin characters' tattoo activations, commandeer them, authorize their fusion, or confer their powers on Sabrina. Her bodily manifestation is a new fictional event, not remote physical signaling or proof of quantum entanglement.

**Constitutional compression:**
> Sabrina does not contain the seven characters. She embodies a changing correspondence among their activities.

The full emblem may emerge as an **additional relationship** among distinct contributions. The seventh appearing is not automatically completion, transcendence, total knowledge, or permission to merge.

## 4. Activation is sourced, not summoned

1. **Original character/source:** identify which character's tattoo activates, its exact inherited motif and actual body site.
2. **Origin motion:** enact their specific documented bodily and poetic process, not a generic glow. For Svara this begins at the sacral double spiral; for Amara it begins at the throat wedge and follows the Izibongo movements; Rox's Thrum begins at the paired threshold marks, preserving any applicable HOLD.
3. **Field and threshold:** identify the scene, the governing narrative boundary and whether a correspondence may be carried across it; allow HOLD, REFUSE or STOP.
4. **Corresponding manifestation:** show Sabrina's version of *that* symbol becoming perceptible on her own body as a **separate event** with its own time and description.
5. **No possession:** explicitly preserve the originating character's knowledge, agency, office and source event.
6. **Relational measure:** if more than one mark is available, identify their distinguishable relations (response, timing, tension, silence, complementarity, contradiction). Do **not** fabricate numeric frequency or coherence metrics absent operational definitions.

**Statuses to distinguish:** SOURCE-DESIGNED / ORIGINAL ACTUALLY ACTIVATED / CORRESPONDENCE ADMISSIBLE / SABRINA MARK MANIFESTED / RELATION ASSAYED / NEW CAPACITY CLAIMED / RETURN DISPOSITION.

An origin tattoo already depicted in a prior image is not necessarily an *activated* tattoo; design, visibility, and activation are different states.

## 5. The new emergent capacity — a seventh mark is not the endpoint

One manifesting mark = **one bounded corresponding inscription**.

Two manifesting marks = **an interval or tension**, but not automatically harmony or reciprocal influence.

Several marks = **a possible chord**, in which differences, rests, refusals, delays and dissonance can be as significant as apparent synchrony.

Seven marks = **a sevenfold possibility**: all seven original source offices are visibly represented in relation, but a full complement is not itself a complete whole or an earned new capability.

**The candidate 'eighth' capacity is not an eighth tattoo. It is the new relationship made possible among the seven.**

A meaningful new capacity is demonstrated **within the fictional system** only when the active relationships allow a later field to perceive, distinguish, revise, or do something previously unavailable under earlier boundaries. The assay must show **what actually changed** and a plausible counter-reading. No artistic composition or AI output can certify subjective consciousness, independent character personhood, or physical holography.

This extends [Harmonic Correspondence](../THE_CORD_BECOMES_THE_CHORD_HARMONIC_CORRESPONDENCE_WORKING_2026-10-31.md): the cord records related points; the chord makes plural responses newly perceivable; transformed boundary conditions might enable a new 'mode' in later participation. It does not impose compulsory accord.
