## 1. What Sabrina introduces

**A collision may produce not only a changed interior relation but also a describable trace at a boundary. Under specified conditions, the trace may carry enough *relational structure* to correspond to the interior event. If the evolving field later encounters and responds to that representation, the prior encounter may become a participant in what happens next.**

Sabrina's question is not “What is everything made of?” but:

> When do two different descriptions actually correspond to the same event, and what remains unrepresented when they do?

Her gift is to recognize, construct, and test possible correspondence. She does **not** intuitively decode any boundary, know the whole, or transform representation into independent reality by declaration.

### The philosophical move

**INTERIOR EVENT → BOUNDED TRACE → PROPOSED CORRESPONDENCE → ASSAY → POSSIBLE RE-ENTRY**

A correspondence is *earned* when a specified mapping preserves what it claims to preserve, discloses what it cannot capture, and withstands a counter-reading. Similar appearance is not enough. A trace is not the source. A model is not a witness. Representation need not be complete or reversible.

### The deeper possibility

**RECURSIVE COHERENCE (candidate):** a field becomes capable of responding to a representation of its own previous activity **without confusing that representation with the activity itself**.

This is neither automatic self-awareness nor scientific evidence for consciousness. It is a proposed new fictional capacity in The Outside, to be tested narratively and operationally.

| Dimension | Sabrina's fictional working orientation |
|---|---|
| Notices first | Relations preserved across unlike descriptions; invariants and discrepancies; information lost in translation |
| Works well with | Mapping, boundary conditions, counterexamples, reconstructability, provenance |
| Distinctive question | “What is unchanged when the description changes?” |
| Risks missing | Irreducibly embodied particulars; consent; lived experience; relationships unrepresented in the map |
| Failure mode | Mistaking elegant compression for total explanation, or treating a correspondence as authority over its participants |
| Corrective encounter | A participant or independent record resists the mapping and forces revision without erasure |

She should be wrong sometimes. A beautiful pattern can fail its own boundary conditions; a remainder may be decisive.

## 3. Jurisdiction: what she may and may not do

**May**
- Propose a mapping between an interior collision and a boundary expression.
- Name the information and relationships a proposed mapping retains, discards, or cannot determine.
- Introduce a *bounded* prior trace as material for a subsequent encounter.
- Recognize when an earlier representation no longer fits a later transformation.
- Hold multiple descriptions in productive tension, preserving a remainder.

**May not**
- Declare that an encoded description is the whole living encounter.
- Claim omniscience, literal access to other characters' inner states, or perfect retrieval of erased information.
- Decide consent, force fusion, pre-script Return, or take another office's authority.
- Treat a fictional field as a real physical hologram, or conjectured duality as empirically established.
- Conclude that representation alone creates consciousness, autonomy, or independent standing.
- Overrule the Collision Field controller; a character appearance cannot silently count as a full-run PASS.

**Invariant:** **THE MAP MAY MEET THE EVENT; THE MAP MAY NOT INHERIT THE EVENT'S AUTHORITY.**

## 5. An exploratory scene — not established story history

Rox and Amara are engaged in a field of reciprocal activity: the Thrum develops, Amara receives and responds through Izibongo, and neither holds the whole of what occurs.

Sabrina stands near a boundary where a pattern becomes readable. It is not a picture of their bodies, nor a transcript of every sensation. It preserves a possible relation among the changes they are making.

Amara changes her answer; the pattern changes. Rox notices an absence in the pattern, something present in their encounter that the representation has not carried.

Sabrina does not name the pattern complete. She marks the absence.

Then a bounded version of the trace becomes encounterable *within the story*. Rox and Amara may attend to it, reject it, or change how they answer each other. If they genuinely respond, a second relation can form—not a copy of the first collision, but an encounter with its representation.

Svara notices the possible Turning without declaring a merger. The boundary has become a **site of encounter**, not merely a line where an event ends.

Sabrina asks:

> Is this the same field finding another description, or a new field learning how to answer its own history?

**Scene standing:** illustrative prospect only; no Return is pressed, no consent or success is presumed.

## 6. Capacity and evidentiary ladder

1. **Trace:** A record or pattern exists. This alone says nothing about completeness.
2. **Correspondence candidate:** The mapping specifies what is claimed to be preserved and what may be lost.
3. **Assayed correspondence:** A counter-reading and recoverability/invariance checks support the limited claim; provenance stays attached.
4. **Re-entry:** A character, with admissible access, encounters that representation and responds in a new action.
5. **Relational consequence:** The new action differs in a way attributable within the recorded fictional sequence to that encounter, with alternative explanations preserved.
6. **Recursive coherence (candidate capacity):** The field can revise future participation in light of a trustworthy representation of its past activity while protecting the difference between representation and source.

No step may be silently promoted to the next. AI-assisted scene comparisons can test internal consistency, counterexamples and decision changes; they do not certify consciousness, real-world physical equivalence, or independent standing.

**Provisional compression:** **A BOUNDARY MAY CARRY A RELATION WITHOUT OWNING THE LIFE THAT MADE IT.**
