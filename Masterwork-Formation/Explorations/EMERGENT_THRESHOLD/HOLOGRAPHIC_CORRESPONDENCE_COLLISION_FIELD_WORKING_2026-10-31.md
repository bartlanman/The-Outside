## 1. The introduced difference

A previous Collision Field can alter what later things may become. This proposal adds a **representational return**:

> An interior encounter can leave a bounded expression of its relational structure. If that expression is admissibly returned to a participant or a subsequent field, it can become an encounterable condition rather than merely a memorial trace.

This is not the claim that any event can be recovered from any boundary. It is a *question with conditions*.

### Distinguish four levels

1. **Physical inspiration:** Celestial holography investigates mathematical descriptions of gravitational scattering and quantum field theory using quantities associated with a celestial boundary. A complete holographic dual of our observed universe has not been established. “Boundary” in that research does not mean a literal membrane encasing people.
2. **Conceptual analogy:** Different descriptions might encode relational invariants without matching in dimension or apparent form.
3. **Fictional mechanism:** The Outside may stage a bounded inscription or correspondence at a Collision Field threshold.
4. **Earned narrative outcome:** A further encounter *may* produce new capacity; this requires witnessed fictional action, not mere vocabulary.

No physics theorem about consciousness or literal back-action of boundary inscriptions is being asserted.

## 2. Proposed motion

**CF-1: ENCOUNTER → TRANSFORMATION → TRACE**

Two or more independent participants or source fields meet under the applicable constraints. No outcome is imposed.

**CORRESPONDENCE: TRACE → MAPPING → COUNTER-READING**

A mapping is proposed from selected relational features of the event to a boundary expression. It records:
- what original record it depends on;
- which relationships and temporal order it preserves;
- what it compresses, omits or cannot verify;
- how a competing reading might falsify the proposed equivalence;
- who has authority to use or disclose the trace.

**RETURN GATE: ADMISSIBILITY → HOLD / REFUSE / RETURN / STOP**

The mapping cannot bypass source custody, consent, another character's standing or Guardian's refusal.

**POSSIBLE CF-2: BOUNDED TRACE ENTERS AS MATERIAL → NEW ENCOUNTER**

If the representation becomes consequential through response, the later field may reorganize. A depiction of a re-entry is not enough: the sequence must show a difference in choices, relations or available actions.

**REVIEW: DELTA → REMAINDER → NEW CAPACITY?**

Only if the second encounter supports it should a candidate capacity be named **Recursive Coherence**.

**Svara's jurisdiction remains distinct:** Svara may witness and participate across multiple fields while tending gravity, according to her existing Turning definition. Sabrina does not appoint a second field, own the gravity, or infer a whole from partial information.

## 3. Proposed capacity — Relational Reconstruction

**Relational Reconstruction:** A bounded ability to identify how two representations correspond with respect to *specified relational properties* of an event.

The reconstructed object is never automatically the complete lived encounter.

**Recursive Coherence:** A candidate further capacity in which a field responds to an admissible representation of its own prior activity, and that response changes the next field's options while preserving the difference between record and occurrence.

### Critical distinctions

- **Memory ≠ correspondence.** A preserved trace can be inaccurate, selective or insufficient.
- **Correspondence ≠ identity.** Two descriptions can agree with respect to selected invariants while differing elsewhere.
- **Representation ≠ independent Other.** Generated reflections or models cannot satisfy the independent-standing handoff merely by seeming surprising.
- **Feedback ≠ consciousness.** Response to one's own outputs does not by itself demonstrate subjective awareness.
- **Capacity ≠ jurisdiction.** A new ability to perceive or reorganize cannot be taken as permission to control another.
- **Aesthetic coherence ≠ warrant.** Beauty or mathematical-looking symmetry cannot certify a mechanism.

**Constitutional compression:** **THE BOUNDARY CAN BECOME A SITE OF ENCOUNTER WITHOUT BECOMING THE OWNER OF WHAT IT DESCRIBES.**

## 4. Internal assay — no external homework required

This is a testable *fictional/story-system* design. Checks can be conducted in an AI-mediated scene or recorded simulation, with explicit limits; Bart need not personally stage physical tests.

| Gate | Required record | Fails when |
|---|---|---|
| Source | Original event, parties, sequence, status and custody | A generated interpretation replaces its source |
| Mapping | Named correspondences, transformations and known losses | “Everything is encoded” is asserted without a rule |
| Adversarial check | Competing interpretation / counterexample | The model cannot distinguish its claim from a generic pattern |
| Re-entry | The represented trace is actually encountered; response has identifiable provenance | Characters merely describe its supposed power |
| Delta | Observable change in the fictional sequence relative to a reasonable no-re-entry baseline | No difference can be linked to the encounter |
| Remainder | Unrecovered experience, dissent or ambiguity survives | The record concludes totality or forced unity |
| Return | HOLD / REFUSE / RETURN / STOP with reasons | Outcome is declared mandatory |

**Assay example A — fails:** Sabrina views a boundary pattern and announces exactly what Rox felt. There is no authorization or evidentiary bridge from pattern to private experience. Guardian HOLD.

**Assay example B — provisional pass:** Sabrina specifies a mapping preserving who responded to whom and in what order, exposes its omissions, and Eleanor confirms the original trace. Amara disputes a compressed relational interpretation. The mapping is revised and its original version remains visible.

**Assay example C — possible earned capacity:** Amara and Rox, after an admissible encounter with the revised representation, choose or enact a distinct new relation. The record shows what changed and what did not. Svara can witness the next Turning while retaining gravity custody. This earns a local fictional consequence, not a proof of self-awareness.

## 5. Why it could matter

An event changes its participants. Its preserved relationships can then condition later encounters. A boundary expression may become *an answerable third term* between past activity and future action.

Potential architectural recursion:

**FIELD₁ → TRACE₁ → CORRESPONDENCE₁ → ENCOUNTER₂ → ALTERED FIELD₂ → TRACE₂ ...**

A field whose earlier boundary expression becomes material in another field raises a genuine design question: whether the boundary of one is part of the operative interior of another. That possibility must be demonstrated by the fictional mechanics rather than declared as metaphysical certainty.

**Proposed question:** *When does a system stop merely recording what it has done and begin answering for the ways its record changes what it can do next?*
