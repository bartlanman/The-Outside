# Collision Field 03 — Outside Sources

## Ise Jingu — Shikinen Sengu
Ise Jingu:
https://www.isejingu.or.jp/en/about/index.html

Supports:
- Shikinen Sengu as periodic renewal / transfer;
- new divine palace constructed every twenty years;
- Ujibashi Bridge rebuilt every twenty years as part of the renewal.

Use here to distinguish persistence of material instance from persistence of rite, craft, and capacity.

---

## Gustav Metzger — Auto-Destructive Art
Tate:
https://shop.tate.org.uk/gustav-metzger-liquid-crystal-environment/radlan2206.html

Supports:
- Metzger as originator of the Auto-Destructive Art movement in 1959;
- art designed to change over time;
- destruction / transformation as intentional artistic operation rather than external accident.

This source supports the broad historical standing of Metzger's auto-destructive / auto-creative practice. Avoid extending it beyond what the source establishes.

## Ecological Succession
National Park Service:
https://www.nps.gov/subjects/nnlandmarks/ecological-succession.htm

Use for:
- ecological succession as change in biological community through time;
- pioneer / later communities;
- organisms and disturbances changing conditions under which later communities emerge.

The Masterwork language of "generative obsolescence" is project inference, not ecological terminology.

## Loose-Parts Play
Systematic review, PubMed Central:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5680404/

Supports:
- Simon Nicholson's Theory of Loose Parts;
- moveable materials;
- child-led engagement with little or no adult direction;
- increased variables / affordances as conditions for creativity and discovery.

The phrase **UNASSIGNED CAPACITY** is project language.

## Vedic Chanting
UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage:
https://ich.unesco.org/en/lists?RL=00062

Supports:
- oral transmission of the Vedas;
- complex recitation techniques;
- tonal accent and pronunciation practices;
- explicit effort to preserve transmitted texts and keep the sound of words unaltered.

Used here as a counterexample to the assumption that increased living capacity must produce increased variation.

## Archival Authenticity
National Archives — Information Quality:
https://www.archives.gov/about/info-qual

Supports:
- archival records are preserved for authenticity as created;
- authenticity is not identical to accuracy of informational content.

National Archives — Custodial History Note:
https://www.archives.gov/research/catalog/lcdrg/elements/custodial-history-note

Supports:
- changes of ownership and breaks in chain of custody can affect interpretation;
- custodial history matters for authenticity and integrity.

The broader philosophical use of **CUSTODY** is project inference.

## Cryptographic Hashing
NIST — Cryptographic hash functions in digital forensics:
https://www.nist.gov/system/files/documents/2018/11/14/3_singh.pdf

Supports:
- hash functions identify exact duplicates;
- even a slight input change such as one flipped bit produces a significantly changed hash output;
- exact identity / integrity differs from similarity.

The artistic use of the hash as an invariant deposit is project inference.

## Venice Charter / Conservation
ICOMOS — Venice Charter:
https://www.icomos.org/images/DOCUMENTS/Charters/Venice_Charter_EN_2023.pdf

ICOMOS doctrinal texts:
https://www.icomos.org/charters-and-doctrinal-texts/

Supports:
- conservation and restoration should not falsify artistic or historic evidence;
- replacement / integration should remain recognizable in the relevant conservation context;
- interventions are documented;
- restoration is not permission to counterfeit historical continuity.

The formulation **ORIGINAL + LEGIBLE CHANGE** is project language.

# Source Handling Rule

These sources establish the outside practices on their own terms.

They do **not** establish:
- CAPACITY as a masterwork principle;
- DIFFERENTIAL MUTABILITY;
- CUSTODY as an artistic law;
- GENERATIVE OBSOLESCENCE;
- the Aeolian-poet formulation.

Those are project-level collisions and inferences.

Preserve that distinction.
