# Cage-Breaking SCORE 01 — Source Notes

## Getty Scores Project
https://www.getty.edu/publications/scores/intro/

Relevant:
- experimental scores opened performer interpretation;
- many global / popular practices historically used skeletal notation, oral memory, tacit knowledge and improvisation;
- Getty explicitly complicates white avant-garde claims around indeterminacy by discussing jazz, race and George E. Lewis's critique of Cage's "Eurological" framing.

## NOAA Climate.gov — Predictability
https://www.climate.gov/news-features/blogs/enso/what-predictability

Relevant:
- atmosphere is chaotic;
- small initial-state errors grow with forecast horizon;
- physical law does not yield unlimited detailed predictability.

## ECMWF — Chaos and Ensemble Forecasts
https://www.ecmwf.int/en/learning/training/introduction-chaos-predictability-and-ensemble-forecasts

Relevant:
- small changes in initial conditions can lead to divergent trajectories;
- ensemble forecasts represent multiple possible futures rather than one deterministic trajectory.

## Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Theories of Biological Development
https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/Win2023/entries/theories-biological-development/

Relevant:
- developmental systems approaches reject simple read-out / blueprint models;
- organismal form emerges from interacting developmental resources across multiple scales and contexts.

Project uses:
- weather and biological development serve as cage-breakers for SCORE;
- neither is treated as art theory;
- no claim that culture literally behaves as atmosphere or organism.
