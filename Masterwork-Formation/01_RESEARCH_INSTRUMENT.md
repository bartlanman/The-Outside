# Research Instrument

Use this instrument for every masterwork dossier.

## Identity
- Author / source
- Work
- Date(s) of composition/publication
- Existing role in The Outside constellation

## Formation
- What they had in hand
- Historical pressure
- Personal pressure
- Problem perceived
- Intellectual inheritance
- Literary / formal inheritance
- Material constraints
- Audience assumptions
- Market / patronage / censorship conditions
- Accidents / contingencies

## Decision
- Decisive formal choices
- Rejected alternatives
- Abandoned structures
- Editorial interventions
- What was deliberately omitted
- What remained unresolved

## Voice
- Sentence architecture
- Diction / register
- Rhythm
- Stance toward reader
- Proportion: scene / summary / reflection
- What the author notices
- Epistemic position: what the narrator is permitted to know or claim

## Authorial office
Possible offices include:
- carrier
- custodian
- architect
- curator
- participant
- witness
- steward
- depositor

Do not force a work into these categories if the evidence resists them.

## Work-specific vs author-specific
- What recurs across the author’s oeuvre?
- What appears only in this work?
- Which decisions arise from this particular pressure?

## Landing
- Contemporary audience expectation
- Original novelty
- Contemporary reception
- Misreadings
- Later reception
- What later readers found that the originating moment could not fully use

## Outside / Deposit
- What had independent standing in the making?
- What could refuse the author?
- What actually altered the work?
- What survived authorial explanation?
- What remains generative after the original context weakens?
- What is deposited versus what later readers come upon?

## Translation
- Surface feature that should NOT be copied
- Deep operation
- 2026 equivalent pressure
- Contemporary formal equivalent
- What must change
- What must survive

## Evidence status
For every major section record:
- documented
- inferred
- interpretive
- unresolved

## Plate compression

INHERITANCE
-> PRESSURE
-> PROBLEM
-> DECISION
-> FORM
-> AUTHORIAL OFFICE
-> RISK
-> SURVIVING OPERATION
-> CONTEMPORARY QUESTION
